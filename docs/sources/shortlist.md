A name found only on listicles, affiliate roundups or "best tools" pages is recorded in the screened count and **not** rostered.

## Caveats

- Cluster targets are channels and questions, not answers. No cluster asserts that its target exists in the form named; several will return `unknown — checked`.
- P3 roster positions 1–24 are a shape, not a prediction. If the roster rule yields fewer than 24 names, the unused clusters do not run and the roster's screened-versus-rostered count is the finding.
- The seeds named in P3-c1, P3-c4, P5-c1, P5-c4, P5-c6 and P5-c7 were each returned by a search on 2026-09-22 and are recorded with the URL they were found at. They are entry points, not a roster and not a reading list; each is re-verified against its cluster's rule before it is pulled.
- Pass 4 is the pass most likely to return little. Its done conditions are satisfied by a recorded absence with a screened count.
- Every cluster's pull list is a floor, not a ceiling: a pull that surfaces a better primary supersedes the listed one, and the substitution is noted in the `raw/` file's pull notes.
