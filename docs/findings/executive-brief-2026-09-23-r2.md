## The question
> Which segments show real-world demand for visibility and recommendation inside AI assistants, what evidence shows it works, and what did brands that moved actually change? (`method/plan.md`, opening section)

Demand signals exist in 8 of 27 core cells under both readings, mostly enterprise organic, resting on job postings — the weakest spend class; no buyer discloses a price paid (demand map §R-BLOCKED-2; C2, C3). Paid inventory is live on four engines; OpenAI states a "$1 billion" run rate; no engine publishes a rate card (AI-ads evidence C1, C2, C4). Proof of a sales effect is absent: 0 Gold in ~2,620 items screened; every Silver is vendor- or practitioner-measured; the two highest-tier Silvers and 19 tier-2/3 filings run negative or null (proof scorecard C1–C3; §Negative tail). Brands that moved changed content and tooling (63, 58 of 108 cases) far more than paid media (2); 37 name an action with no measured outcome (transition evidence §Tallies; §three-count). No sub-market has a measured size; forecasts disagree 1.92× to 26.3× (market potential C5). The evidence stops at correlation and self-report.
