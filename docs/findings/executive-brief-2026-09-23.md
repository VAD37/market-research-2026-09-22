No published case shows causal sales lift, and no named brand checked confirms a vendor's case on its own site. Spend signals exist, mostly in enterprise organic cells, and 6 of 7 rest on job postings, the weakest spend signal [`findings/demand-map.md` C2, tier 3]. No sub-market has a measured size and no buyer discloses a price paid. Our own engine sampling stopped at a partial first day, by owner decision on 2026-09-22.

## What the evidence does not show

- Causal sales lift from any intervention: no holdout, geo-split or switchback in any screened case [`findings/proof-scorecard.md` C1].
- Whether AI referral converts above organic search: Adobe compares AI to non-AI visits, not to organic search; H1 unresolved [`findings/unknowns.md` H1].
- The size of any sub-market, or any price a buyer paid [`findings/whitespace.md` E2; `findings/demand-map.md` C3].
- What engines return to buying-shaped prompts under neutral conditions: six panel hypotheses are `not produced` [`findings/unknowns.md` HE2–HP4].
- Anything from blocked channels: sec.gov, Reddit, G2/Capterra, Indeed/Upwork and Google Trends were blocked, not exhausted [`findings/whitespace.md` caveats].
