## 1. The question, and what the evidence supports
> Which segments show real-world demand for visibility and recommendation inside AI assistants, what evidence shows it works, and what did brands that moved actually change? (`method/plan.md`, opening section)

Demand signals exist in 8 of 27 core cells under both readings (strict 8 / 1 / 17 / 1 blank; loose 8 / 1 / 18 / 0), sit mostly in enterprise organic cells, and rest on job postings, the catalogue's weakest spend class (demand map §R-BLOCKED-2 tallies; C2). No buyer anywhere discloses a price paid (27 of 27, plus 9 local cells). Paid inventory is live on four engines; OpenAI states a "$1 billion" run rate; no engine publishes a rate card (AI-ads evidence C1, C2, C4). Proof of a sales effect is absent: 0 Gold in ~980 plus ~1,640 items screened; Silver 7 grade_raw / 1 grade_rule1 (2026-09-22) plus 4 raw / 1 rule1 (2026-09-23), every one vendor- or practitioner-measured; the two highest-tier Silvers run negative or null, and 19 tier-2/3 documents state declines (proof scorecard C1–C3; §Negative tail). Brands that moved name content and tooling changes far more often than paid media: 63 and 58 cases against 2 of 108; 37 of 108 name an action with no measured outcome (transition evidence §Tallies; §three-count). No sub-market has a measured size; every dollar size is a forecast, and forecasts disagree 1.92× to 26.3× (market potential C5). The evidence stops at correlation and self-report; whether any of it pays is not shown by any document in the repo.
