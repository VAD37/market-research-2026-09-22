# Brand visibility inside AI assistants — what the evidence shows

Prepared 2026-09-23 from research pulled 2026-09-22 and 2026-09-23. Tier: provenance score, 1 strongest, 6 marketing. Acronyms in the glossary.

**Read this first.** Demand exists and is thin: 8 of 27 buyer segments show a spend signal, decided almost entirely by job postings; no buyer discloses a price paid. Supply runs ahead of proof: ads are live inside four engine owners' assistants, one at a "$1 billion in annualized revenue run rate"; 43 vendor profiles sell tooling around it; yet 0 of ~980 published cases shows causal sales lift, and the two highest-tier cases run negative or null. No sub-market has a measured size. The evidence stops there; it does not say whether to act.

## 1. What this is

Brands want to be named when a person asks ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot or Claude what to buy. Three separate markets serve that want:

| Sub-market | What is sold | Buyer |
|---|---|---|
| Organic recommendation (GEO, AEO) | unpaid appearance in answers; tooling, services | brand marketing, SEO owner |
| Paid placement | ad inventory inside AI answers | media buyer |
| Agentic commerce | checkout executed by the assistant | e-commerce, payments |

Three metrics stay apart: visibility (named in an answer), traffic (a referred visit), sales. Visibility alone proves no sale.

## 4. Who is already there

**Engines.** Four engine owners sell ads inside AI answers as of 2026-09-23: OpenAI, Google (AI Overviews, AI Mode), Microsoft Copilot, Amazon Rufus/Alexa (company-stated, 3); an earlier count reads 5 of 8 engines, Google's surfaces counted apart. Claude refuses; the Gemini app has none; Perplexity is "winding down" by end-2026 (5). Only OpenAI discloses revenue or an advertiser count — "tens of thousands" (3) against panel counts of 820 to 7,378 (5). How often ChatGPT shows an ad conflicts irreconcilably: 26% of US desktop responses, June 2026; 4.47% of ~850,000 US queries, Mar–May 2026; 0.00% of 169,560 UK scrapes, June 2026 — all tier 5. Four checkout programmes are live; Copilot states 0% commission, ChatGPT "a small fee" with no rate, Google and Perplexity unknown — checked 2026-09-22 (3).

**Vendors.** 43 profiles; 34 rostered on the organic side (13 pure-plays, 16 incumbent bundles, 5 agencies). Disclosure is thin: price 14 of 34, customer count 8 of 34, funding 8 of 34, filed revenue 2 of 34, neither broken out to this product. Semrush filed "AI products surpassed $38 million in ARR" (2025-12, filed 2) before Adobe absorbed it; Scrunch went to Sitecore 2026-06-03, value "not disclosed" (3) beside a "$225 Million" press figure (5). 0 of 34 vendors disclose the prompt set behind their visibility score (3). Of 59 customer brands checked on their own domains, 0 corroborate, 0 contradict, 59 are silent; ~107 unchecked.

**Buyers.** 27 segments (3 sub-markets × 3 verticals × 3 sizes), read 2026-09-23: strict 8 spend / 1 attention / 17 none / 1 blank; loose 8 / 1 / 18 / 0 (strict treats a still-blocked channel as unchecked). Organic carries 6 spend cells, paid 1, agentic 1; one of nine small-business cells reads spend. At 2026-09-22, six of seven spend reads rested on job postings — "headcount budget, not category spend — weakest spend class"; the cell added since also rests on postings. Named movers: e.l.f. Beauty (in-house AEO/GEO team; Google "Sponsored deal" pilot; an agentic role at "$110,000.00/yr - $140,000.00/yr", 116 applicants), Coty (10-K names "generative engine optimization", filed 2), Cigna, Pennylane, Walgreens, and a German statutory insurer's agency framework naming GEO, ceiling 11,000,000.00 EUR (filed 2). A fourth vertical, local and multi-location chains, reads 1 spend / 3 attention / 5 none of 9.
