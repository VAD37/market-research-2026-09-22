1 · What this is

The market splits three ways, and sales are never inferred from visibility

Brands want to be named when someone asks an AI assistant what to buy. Three ways to get there:
Organic recommendation
Unpaid appearance in the answer. Served by software tools and agencies. Also called GEO (generative engine optimisation) or AEO (answer engine optimisation).
Paid placement
Ads shown inside the AI answer, sold by the engine that runs the assistant.
Agentic commerce
The assistant itself runs the checkout; the merchant is paid through it.
Three results are measured apart and never crossed: visibility (named in the answer), traffic (clicks to the brand) and sales. A change in visibility alone is never read as proof of sales.
