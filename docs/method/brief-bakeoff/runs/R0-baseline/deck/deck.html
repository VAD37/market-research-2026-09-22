What this is

A brand can appear inside an AI assistant's answer three ways; each is a separate market

Organic recommendation

Unpaid appearance in an answer. Sold as GEO (generative engine optimisation), a cousin of SEO (search engine optimisation).

Paid placement

Ads shown inside or beside the AI answer, bought from the engine.

Agentic commerce

The assistant's agent picks the product and executes the checkout for the user.

The question. Does real demand exist, per buyer segment, and what evidence shows it works?

Buyer segment. Kind of placement × industry × buyer size gives 27 segments ("cells"). Industries tracked: skincare and beauty; B2B SaaS (business-to-business software as a service); high cost-per-acquisition regulated.

Three metrics, never mixed:

  • Visibility: the brand appears in the answer
  • Traffic: sessions referred to the brand's site
  • Sales: revenue attributed to the answer

A visibility gain is not evidence of a sales gain.

Case grades for "did it work" stories:

  • Silver: before/after with an unaffected control
  • Bronze: visibility change only

What is asked of you

No decision is asked; the evidence stops at existence, and three readings are yours to choose

1. Which market

Organic, paid, agentic, or several. Organic has the most spend segments; paid has the one large disclosed revenue; agentic has forecasts 26.3× apart and no floor.

2. Which proof reading

Silver cases as the source graded them, or under the strict rule that caps incomplete cases at Bronze.

3. Which demand reading

Strict or loose. Both show 8 spend segments; strict leaves 1 segment blank, loose 0.

The research produces evidence, not a verdict. Whether to act on it is the director's call.

Appendix C · Scope notes

What this deck leaves out, and how it was built

  • Omitted for length; held in the source research: manipulation-technique research, publisher licensing, per-country EU figures, funding rounds beyond Profound, the full event timeline and the full risk register.
  • Method: visibility, traffic and sales are never mixed. Each success case carries its grade as its source graded it and its grade under the strict rule.
  • Numbers: every figure is carried as the source states it: no rounding, no averaging, no new arithmetic. Where two figures conflict, both appear.
  • Charts: plot only figures stated in the record. Bar positions on the forecast chart use a logarithmic scale; labels carry the stated figures verbatim.
  • Internet sources only: no interviews or outreach.
