# Brand visibility inside AI assistants — the evidence as of 2026-09-23

Research only: evidence, no verdict. Each figure carries source kind, tier (1 strongest, 7 weakest) and date. † = second reading or date in appendix.

## 1. Buyers pay, mostly at enterprise; one engine sells ads at a $1 billion run rate; no published case proves a sales effect

As of 2026-09-23: 8† of 27 tracked buyer cells (three sub-markets × three verticals × three buyer sizes) show a spend signal, mostly job postings (analyst-derived). ChatGPT ads reached "$1 billion in annualized revenue run rate" (company-stated, 3, 2026-08-31). Of ~980 published cases screened to 2026-09-22 and ~1,640 more on 2026-09-23, none used a holdout, geo-split or switchback (measured-by-us). No sub-market has a measured size.

## 2. Why now: ads, filed referral losses and regulation arrived 2026-02 to 2026-09

- **Engines opened ad inventory.** ChatGPT ads: US 2026-02-09, 31 European markets 2026-08-18, "over 40 countries" by 2026-08-31 (company-stated, 3). "Claude will remain ad-free", 2026-02-04 (company-stated, 3).
- **Publishers filed traffic losses blamed on AI answers.** NerdWallet credit-cards revenue "decreased 24% year-over-year", citing "AI overviews and LLMs" (filed, 2, 2026-02-25); IAC/People Inc "63% decline in Google Search referrals over two years" (filed, 2, 2026-05-04); Microsoft court exhibit: "83-93% drops in click-through rates" (filed, 2, 2026-09-17).
- **Regulators named AI search; capital followed.** EU designated ChatGPT a Very Large Online Search Engine (VLOSE) 2026-08-31, ad-repository duty from January 2027; UK Competition and Markets Authority (CMA) fair-ranking requirement covers "search generative AI features", 2026-06-17 (both filed, 2). Vendor Profound raised "$180M Series D at $1.8B valuation", 2026-09-15 (company-stated†, 3).

## 4. Solution space: 43 vendors profiled, none discloses how its score is measured; engines own the rails

- **Visibility tools.** 43 profiles (measured-by-us, 2026-09-23). Of 34 rostered vendors, 14 of 34 disclose a price, self-serve "$20/mo" to "$999/month"; 2 of 34 file revenue; 0 of 34 publish the prompt set behind their score (vendor-reported, 3, 2026-09-22). Only filed product line: Semrush "AI products surpassed $38 million in ARR" (filed, 2, 2025-12).
- **Engines keep the rails.** OpenAI ships Ads Manager, Pixel, Conversions API, "more than 50" measurement partners (company-stated, 3, 2026-08-31). Four agent-checkout programmes live; Copilot's "does not take a commission"; 0 of 6 payment protocols state a fee (company-stated, 3, 2026-09-22).
- **Gaps, as hypotheses, not plans:** a score with a disclosed prompt set; independent measurement of ads in answers (Adthena lists "$350/mo" and "$399/mo", vendor-reported, 3, 2026-09-23); ad-repository compliance tooling — unknown — checked 2026-09-23; brand-side confirmation of vendor claims (1† of 168 brands).

### Method

Three metrics never crossed: visibility (appearance in an answer), traffic (referred sessions), sales (attributed revenue). A cell reads spend only with a spend-class signal at tier 5 or better for that exact cell; job postings are the weakest spend-class signal.
