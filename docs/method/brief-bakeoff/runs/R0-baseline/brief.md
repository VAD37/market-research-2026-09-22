# Brand visibility inside AI assistants — the evidence as of 2026-09-23

Research only: evidence, no verdict. Figures carry source kind, tier (1 strongest, 7 weakest) and date; † = second reading or date in the appendix.

## 1. Spend shows in 8 of 27 buyer cells and one engine sells ads at a $1 billion run rate; no published case proves a sales effect

Strict reading, 2026-09-23: 8† of 27 buyer cells (sub-market × vertical × buyer size) show a spend signal (analyst-derived). ChatGPT ads reached "$1 billion in annualized revenue run rate" (company-stated, 3, 2026-08-31). Of ~980 cases screened to 2026-09-22 and ~1,640 more on 2026-09-23, none used a holdout, geo-split or switchback (measured-by-us). No sub-market has a measured size.

## 2. Why now: from 2026-02 to 2026-09 engines opened ad inventory, publishers filed traffic losses, regulators named AI search

- **Engines opened ad inventory.** ChatGPT ads: US 2026-02-09; 31 European markets 2026-08-18; "over 40 countries" by 2026-08-31 (company-stated, 3). "Claude will remain ad-free", 2026-02-04 (company-stated, 3).
- **Publishers filed losses blamed on AI answers.** NerdWallet credit-cards revenue "decreased 24% year-over-year", citing "AI overviews and LLMs" (filed, 2, 2026-02-25); IAC/People Inc "63% decline in Google Search referrals over two years" (filed, 2, 2026-05-04); Microsoft court exhibit: "83-93% drops in click-through rates" (filed, 2, 2026-09-17).
- **Regulators named AI search; capital followed.** The EU designated ChatGPT a Very Large Online Search Engine (VLOSE) 2026-08-31, obligations from January 2027; the UK Competition and Markets Authority (CMA) fair-ranking requirement covers "search generative AI features", 2026-06-17 (both filed, 2). Profound, a visibility vendor, raised "$180M Series D at $1.8B valuation", 2026-09-15 (company-stated†, 3).

## 4. Solution space: no visibility vendor discloses its scoring prompt set; engines sell ads, checkout and measurement themselves

- **Visibility tools.** 43 profiles (measured-by-us, 2026-09-23). Of 34 rostered vendors, 14 of 34 disclose a price, self-serve "$20/mo" to "$999/month"; 2 of 34 file revenue; 0 of 34 disclose a fixed published prompt set (vendor-reported, 3, 2026-09-22). Filed AI product line: Semrush "AI products surpassed $38 million in ARR" (filed, 2, 2026-03-02).
- **Engines keep the rails.** OpenAI ships Ads Manager, Pixel, Conversions API and "more than 50 technology and measurement partners" (company-stated, 3, 2026-08-31). Of 4 live agent-checkout programmes, 1 states a fee: Copilot "does not take a commission"; 6 of 6 payment-protocol fee clauses unknown — checked (company-stated, 3, 2026-09-22).
- **Gaps, as hypotheses, not plans:** a visibility score with a disclosed prompt set; independent measurement of ads inside answers (Adthena lists "$350/mo" and "$399/mo", vendor-reported, 3, 2026-09-23); ad-repository compliance tooling — unknown — checked, 2026-09-23; brand-side confirmation of vendor claims (1† of 168 brands).
