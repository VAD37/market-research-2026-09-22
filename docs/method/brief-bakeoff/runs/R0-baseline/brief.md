# Brand visibility inside AI assistants — the evidence as of 2026-09-23

Research only: evidence, no verdict. Each figure carries a source kind and a date; tier 1 is the strongest provenance, 7 the weakest (scale in appendix). Acronyms in the glossary.

## 1. Demand is visible, thin and enterprise-led; ads run on one engine at a $1 billion rate; no published case proves a sales effect

As of 2026-09-23: 8 of 27 tracked buyer cells (three sub-markets × three verticals × three buyer sizes) show a spend signal, nearly all from job postings; one engine reports "$1 billion in annualized revenue run rate" from ads (company-stated, 3, 2026-08-31); of ~980 published cases screened to 2026-09-22 and ~1,640 more to 2026-09-23, zero use a holdout, geo-split or switchback; no sub-market has a measured size.

## 2. Why now: ads, referral loss and regulation all landed between 2026-02 and 2026-09

- **Paid inventory opened.** ChatGPT Ads live in the US 2026-02-09, 31 European markets 2026-08-18, "over 40 countries" 2026-08-31 (company-stated, 3). Claude "will remain ad-free", 2026-02-04 (company-stated, 3). Perplexity "winding down" ads "by the end of 2026" (analyst-derived, 5, 2026-02-18).
- **Referral loss is now filed.** NerdWallet credit-cards revenue "decreased 24% year-over-year", cause "AI overviews and LLMs" (filed, 2, 2026-02-25); IAC/People Inc "63% decline in Google Search referrals over two years" (filed, 2, 2026-05-04); Microsoft "83-93% drops in click-through rates" for publisher domains (court exhibit, 2, 2026-09-17); HubSpot blog "5 million" fewer visits in thirty days (company-stated, 3, 2026-09-22).
- **Regulators and capital moved.** ChatGPT designated a Very Large Online Search Engine (VLOSE) 2026-08-31, "approximately 159.1 million" EU users, ad-repository duty from January 2027; EU AI Act Art. 50 in force 2026-08-02; UK CMA fair-ranking order covering "search generative AI features" 2026-06-17 (all filed, 2). Profound "$180M Series D at $1.8B valuation" 2026-09-15 (company-stated, 3); Semrush into Adobe 2026-04-28, "$12.00 in cash" per share (filed, 2).

## 4. Solution space: 43 vendors profiled, one valued at $1.8B, none disclosing its prompt set, product revenue or a price a buyer paid

43 profiles (2026-09-22 to 2026-09-23): 34 organic-recommendation names — 13 pure-plays, 16 incumbent bundles, 5 agencies — plus 4 paid sell-side, 2 ad-measurement, 3 agentic. Self-serve prices "$20/mo" to "$999/month"; 14 of 34 disclose a price, 8 of 34 a customer count, 2 of 34 file revenue, 0 of 34 the prompt set behind their score (vendor-reported, 3). Only filed product line: Semrush "AI products surpassed $38 million in ARR" (filed, 2, 2025-12). Funding: unknown — checked crunchbase.com (403), pitchbook.com (403) 2026-09-22 for 17 of 46 companies; rounds and exits in the appendix.

Engines keep the rails: each engine with ads owns its auction; OpenAI ships Ads Manager, Pixel, Conversions API and "more than 50" measurement partners; Google Search Console reports AI clicks with no AI segment; Anthropic offers nothing (company-stated, 3, 2026-09). Checkout: 4 live programs; Copilot "does not take a commission"; OpenAI "a small fee", rate undisclosed; 0 of 6 protocols state a fee (company-stated, 3).

Gaps, each a possible position for a hypothetical product (hypotheses, not plans): a visibility measure with a disclosed prompt set; independent measurement of ads inside answers (two vendors sell it, "$350/mo" and "$399/mo", vendor-reported 3); compliance tooling for ad repositories or AI labelling on conversational ad surfaces — unknown — checked TrustArc, OneTrust, Didomi, Usercentrics, IAB Europe, IAB Tech Lab 2026-09-23; brand-side confirmation of vendor claims (1 of 168 brands checked). Whether any gap suits an engineering-led rather than distribution-led team: no evidence addresses it.

### Source tiers (provenance, 1 strongest)

1 measured by us · 2 filed (S-1, 10-K, court exhibit) · 3 platform primary (own docs, pricing page) · 4 panel or clickstream with published method · 5 vendor or agency study with n, dates, method · 6 vendor blog, no n — not a source · 7 listicle — not pulled.

### Method

Three metrics never crossed: visibility (appearance in an answer), traffic (referred sessions), sales (attributed revenue). Demand read per cell: spend, attention, or none; spend needs a spend-class signal at tier 5 or better for that exact cell; job postings are a spend-class signal, the weakest. "Strict" and "loose" cell readings differ on whether a blocked-and-logged channel counts as checked. Case grades are assigned two ways: as the census graded (grade_raw) and under the strict rule (grade_rule1).
