# Brands inside AI assistants: what the evidence shows

Evidence as of 2026-09-23. Evidence only; the decision is yours. Figures held in two readings are paired in the appendix.

## The answer: brands have started paying, but no published evidence shows it pays back

**Situation.** AI assistants reach mass audiences: ChatGPT ">1 billion" weekly active users (company-stated, 2026-08-31); Google AI Mode "surpassed 1 billion monthly active users" (company-stated, 2026-05-19).

**Complication.** The assistants now sell placement. ChatGPT ads went live 2026-02-09 and reached a "$1 billion in annualized revenue run rate" (company-stated, 2026-08-31). Publishers lose clicks: Microsoft data in a court filing shows "83-93% drops in click-through rates" on news domains (filed, 2026-09-17).

**Question.** Which segments pay to be seen and recommended inside AI assistants, and does anything show it works?

**Answer.** Spend is real, thin, recent and concentrated in large firms. The evidence supports three statements and stops at a fourth:

- Paid inventory exists and is scaling; prices and payback are undisclosed.
- Buyer spend is sparse, mostly hiring at large firms; no buyer discloses a price paid.
- Tool vendors are funded and consolidating; disclosed revenue is small.
- It stops at proof: no published case isolates a sales effect.

## What the market is: three sub-markets, never summed

Organic recommendation (a brand named or cited in answers); paid placement (ads in or beside answers); agentic commerce (the assistant completes the purchase). Demand was read in 27 cells: three verticals (skincare and beauty; B2B SaaS; high-CPA regulated) × three buyer sizes × three sub-markets.

## Who is already there: assistants own the inventory; tool vendors are funded but small

- **Engines** (company-stated, 2026-09): 5 of 8 carry a live ad product; 0 of 8 publish a rate card; 1 of 8 discloses revenue. Only price stated: OpenAI bid guidance "$3–$5 USD per click" (read 2026-09-22). Advertisers: "tens of thousands" (2026-08-31).
- **Checkout** (company-stated, 2026-09): 4 programs live; 1 of 4 states a fee.
- **Tool vendors** (analyst-derived roster, 2026-09): 34 rostered; 14 of 34 disclose price; 2 of 34 file revenue, neither broken out to this product. Semrush AI products "surpassed $38 million in ARR" (filed, 2026-03-02). Peec AI ARR $10M (vendor-reported, 2026-05).

## Does it work: nothing isolates cause; negatives sit beside positives

- ~980 candidates screened; zero disclose a holdout, geo-split or switchback (measured-by-us, 2026-09-22).
- Best grade is Silver (before/after with a control): 1 under the literal seven-item evidence bar; 7 as first graded.
- Of 52 classed cases, reading R2 (measured-by-us, 2026-09-23): 5 experimental design, 34 observational, 13 action with outcome unknown. Reading R1 exists.
- Negative or null: NerdWallet credit cards revenue "decreased 24%" (filed, 2026-02-25); a replication found optimisation levers move citation on "none" of ten engine families (preprint, analyst-derived, 2026-09-07).
- Paid results: SE Ranking's own ChatGPT campaigns, CTR 1.30% on 97,000+ impressions (vendor-reported, 2026-08-10); one advertiser spent "just 3%" of $250K (vendor-reported relay, 2026-03).
- Positive, none causal: Adobe "AI Conversion Now 42% Higher" than non-AI visits (vendor-reported, 2026-03); TechTarget "2x to 3x higher membership conversion rate" (filed, 2026-03-11).

## What brands that moved changed: content and tools, rarely paid media

Of 108 cases, 99 name a change: content 63, vendor tool 58, other 19, organisation 12, paid media 2 (measured-by-us, 2026-09-23). Both paid cases are one Google pilot. A count by outcome exists.

## Where the evidence stops

- Our own sampling of assistant answers was held 2026-09-22 22:40 and skipped 2026-09-23; ChatGPT and Perplexity got 0 runs.
- Claims resting on filed or platform sources: 11 of 25 = 44.0% (2026-09-23), against a bar of 80 percent or more; other counts exist.
- Channels still blocked 2026-09-23: 5; 3 more need payment. Absence is only as strong as these channels.
- Whether any posting was filled or change shipped: unknown — not in repo.

## What this asks of you

Nothing is requested. Three reading choices move the figures: strict or loose cell reads; Silver count 7 or 1; evidence class R1 or R2. Any product is a hypothesis; the evidence does not pick its sub-market.

## Glossary

- **AI**: artificial intelligence. **AI Mode, AI Overviews**: Google's AI answer surfaces.
- **ARR**: annual recurring revenue.
- **Art. 50**: EU AI Act article on transparency duties.
- **B2B SaaS**: business-to-business software sold as a subscription.
- **Cell read**: spend (money committed), attention (interest, no spend), none (checked, nothing found), blank (not checked).
- **CTR**: click-through rate.
- **EU, UK, US**: European Union, United Kingdom, United States.
- **Gold / Silver / Bronze**: evidence grades. Gold: holdout, geo-split or switchback. Silver: before/after with a control. Bronze: visibility change only.
- **High-CPA**: high cost per acquisition.
- **Holdout, geo-split, switchback**: experimental designs that isolate cause.
- **R1 / R2**: R1 counts a pre/post with control as experimental; R2 counts it observational.
- **SMB**: small and medium-sized business.
- **Strict / loose**: strict reads a cell `none` only if every signal ran; loose also counts blocked-and-logged channels as checked.
- **USD**: US dollars.
- **VLOSE**: very large online search engine, an EU Digital Services Act designation.
