# Brand visibility inside AI assistants: evidence memo

Evidence as of 2026-09-23. Research only: this memo reports evidence; the decision is yours.

## Answer

Paid placement inside AI assistants exists and sells. Brand demand is real but thin, enterprise-led and unpriced. No published case shows assistant visibility raising sales.

- **Supply.** ChatGPT ads reached "$1 billion in annualized revenue run rate" (company-stated, 2026-08-31).
- **Demand.** 8 of 27 buyer segments show spend (measured-by-us, strict reading, 2026-09-23; loose reading agrees; earlier count in appendix). Willingness to pay: unknown in 27 of 27.
- **Proof.** 0 causal (Gold) cases in ~980 screened (2026-09-22) and ~1,640 more (2026-09-23), measured-by-us.
- **Size.** No sub-market has a measured size. Of 18 published size figures, 15 are forecasts (analyst-derived, pulled 2026-09-22).

Evidence stops at sales effect, prices paid, and rate cards.

## What this is

Brands want to be named when a person asks an AI assistant (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, Claude) what to buy. The market splits three ways: **organic recommendation** (mentioned without paying the engine), **paid placement** (ads inside answers) and **agentic commerce** (the assistant completes the purchase). Three outcomes are measured separately, never merged: visibility, traffic, sales.

**Glossary**

- **AI**: artificial intelligence. **EU / UK / US**: European Union, United Kingdom, United States.
- **AI Act Article 50**: EU transparency duties for AI systems.
- **VLOSE**: very large online search engine, a designation under the EU Digital Services Act.
- **MAU / WAU**: monthly / weekly active users. **ARR**: annual recurring revenue. **SMB**: small and medium business.
- **IAB**: Interactive Advertising Bureau. **EMARKETER, WPP Media, Datos, Similarweb, Adthena**: research, media and panel firms.
- **Tier**: source strength, 1 strongest, 7 weakest; 2 filed, 3 platform primary, 4 panel, 5 vendor study, 6 marketing, not a source.
- **Gold**: holdout, geo-split or switchback; causal. **Silver**: pre/post with baseline and unaffected control; correlational. **Bronze**: visibility change only.
- **Grading rule 1**: a case missing any of items 1–7 of the evidence bar is Bronze at best. "As graded in raw" is the census grade.
- **Spend / attention / none / blank**: cell reads; attention never stands in for spend; blank means not checked.
- **Strict / loose**: strict counts a cell `none` only when every signal ran; loose counts a blocked, logged channel as checked.
- **R1 / R2**: R1 counts a pre/post with control as experimental; R2 as observational.
- **Q1**: first calendar quarter.
- **USD**: US dollars. **M / B / T**: million / billion / trillion.
