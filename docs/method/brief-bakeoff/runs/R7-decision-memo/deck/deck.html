What this is

Brands want to be named when a person asks an AI assistant what to buy; the market splits three ways

Organic recommendation

The brand is mentioned in the answer without paying the engine. Brands buy tools and content work to influence it.

Paid placement

Ads shown inside or beside the assistant's answer, sold by the engine.

Agentic commerce

The assistant completes the purchase itself, through a checkout the engine runs.

Three outcomes, always measured separately, never merged: visibility (is the brand named), traffic (does anyone click through), sales (does anyone buy). Evidence for one is not evidence for another.

QuestionWhat the evidence says

Appendix B: glossary

Terms and abbreviations

  • AI: artificial intelligence. EU / UK / US: European Union, United Kingdom, United States.
  • AI Act Article 50: EU transparency duties for AI systems.
  • Very large online search engine: a designation under the EU Digital Services Act.
  • ARR: annual recurring revenue. Run rate: current revenue annualised.
  • SMB: small and medium business.
  • USD: US dollars. M / B / T: million / billion / trillion.
  • Rate card: a published price list for ad placements.
  • IAB: Interactive Advertising Bureau. EMARKETER, WPP Media, Similarweb, Adthena: research, media and panel firms.
  • Series D: a late-stage venture funding round.
  • Rostered vendor: a tool vendor on the programme's tracked list.
  • Load-bearing claim: a claim the conclusions depend on.
  • Evidence bar: the checklist a case must meet to be graded Silver or Gold.
  • Relayed: a figure reported by press, not confirmed by the parties.
