# Brand visibility inside AI assistants: what the evidence supports

As of 2026-09-23. Evidence only; the reader decides. Each figure names its source kind and date. Our own measurements and filings are strongest; vendor case studies weakest. Acronyms: Glossary.

## The answer

Brands have started paying to be seen inside AI assistants; the evidence stops before sales. Demand is real, thin and mostly large-company; one engine sells ads at scale; no published case shows sales caused.

- **Demand.** 8 of 27 segment cells show money spent, strict and loose readings alike (analyst-derived, 2026-09-23). On the 2026-09-22 read, six of seven spend cells rested on job postings; 5 of the 7 were enterprise. The price any buyer paid is unknown in all 27.
- **Supply.** ChatGPT Ads: "$1 billion in annualized revenue run rate" (company-stated, 2026-08-31). Engines: "0 of 8 publish a rate card" (company-stated, pulled 2026-09-22).
- **Size.** No sub-market has a measured size; dollar sizes are forecasts.
- **Proof.** 0 causal-design cases in ~980 candidates (2026-09-22) and ~1,640 more (2026-09-23), measured-by-us over vendor-reported cases.
- **Movers.** Of 108 cases: content changes 63, new tools 58, paid media 2 (measured-by-us, 2026-09-23).

## What this is

Brands want to be named when someone asks an assistant (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, Claude) what to buy. Three sub-markets: **organic recommendation** (also GEO, AEO), cited unpaid, bought by SEO and brand teams; **paid placement**, ads inside answers, bought by media buyers; **agentic commerce**, assistant-completed checkout, bought by e-commerce teams. Visibility, traffic and sales are separate metrics; visibility never proves sales.

## What the proof shows

- Controlled, non-randomised (Silver) cases, rule-1 reading: 1 (2026-09-22) plus "one" (2026-09-23); a raw reading exists. Of 7 raw Silvers, 2 run negative or null: NerdWallet's filed revenue line and a published replication (2026-09-22).
- 108 cases by evidence class, R1 reading: experimental 6, observational 61, outcome unknown 37 (measured-by-us, 2026-09-23); an R2 reading exists.
- Brands confirming a vendor's case themselves: 1 of 168 (measured-by-us, 2026-09-23); an earlier count exists.

Negative: People Inc "22% decline in Core Sessions" (filed, 2026-08-03); HubSpot blog "5 million" fewer visits in thirty days (company-stated, 2026-09-22). Null: optimisation levers move citation on "none" of ten engine families (analyst-derived, 2026-09-07). Positive, vendor-measured: "+300% more site visits from AI Search" ("February 23 to June 29, 2026"). Conversion: Adobe "AI Conversion Now 42% Higher" than non-AI visits (2026-03); Shopify "nearly 50% higher rates than organic search" (Q1 2026); both vendor-reported, neither per engine.

## What could kill it

Threats to the hypothesis that marketing inside assistants pays, in risk-register rank order (2026-09-23). Likelihood, impact, mitigation, owner: no source states one.

- Referral collapse: the court exhibit above.
- Regulation: disclosure duties; AI Overviews ads exclude finance, healthcare.
- Vendor consolidation: the acquisitions above.
- Platform capture: OpenAI names "more than 50 technology and measurement partners"; Google campaigns auto-eligible, "you can't opt out".
- No payback evidence: "0 Gold; 0 of 15 ad results controlled".
- Measurement opacity: ChatGPT ad presence 0.8%–26% across panels.
- Substitutes: Google says no "special optimizations" are "necessary".
- Engine policy reversal: Claude ad-free; Perplexity winding down ads.
- Agentic rails: engine-owned checkout, fees undisclosed.
- Manipulation: Google spam policy names "manipulate generative AI responses" (2026-08-28).
- Unsizeable market: "18 figures, 15 forecasts, 0 measured sizes".
