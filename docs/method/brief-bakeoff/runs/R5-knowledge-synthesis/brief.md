# Brand visibility inside AI assistants — what the evidence supports

As of 2026-09-23. Research only: evidence, no verdict; the reader decides. Every figure names its source kind, evidence tier (1 strongest, 7 weakest) and date. Terms: Glossary.

## The answer

Demand is real but thin and enterprise-heavy; paid placement inside assistants is selling on one disclosed engine; no published case shows it causes sales.

1. **Demand.** 8 of 27 segment cells show a spend signal, strict and loose readings alike (analyst-derived, 2026-09-23). On the 2026-09-22 read, 6 of 7 spend cells rested on job postings, the weakest spend class. Willingness to pay: unknown in 27 of 27 cells; no buyer discloses a price paid.
2. **Paid inventory.** Four engines sell ads inside AI answers (company-stated, 3, 2026-09-23). One discloses revenue: ChatGPT Ads "$1 billion in annualized revenue run rate" (company-stated, 3, 2026-08-31). Rate cards published: 0 of 8 engines (company-stated, 3, 2026-09-22).
3. **Size.** No sub-market has a measured size; every dollar size is a forecast (analyst-derived, 6, 2026-09-22).
4. **What movers changed.** Of 108 cases: content 63, tooling 58, paid media 2 (measured-by-us, 2026-09-23).
5. **Where it stops.** 0 causal-design (Gold) cases in ~980 candidates (2026-09-22) and ~1,640 more (2026-09-23), measured-by-us over vendor cases. The two highest-tier controlled cases run negative or null.

| Term | Meaning here |
|---|---|

| Copilot; Rufus | Microsoft's assistant; Amazon's shopping assistant |
| GEO; AEO; AI visibility | Generative engine optimisation; answer engine optimisation — names for the organic sub-market. GEO is never geographic here |
| SEO | Search engine optimisation, classical |
| Visibility; traffic; sales | Brand appears in an AI answer; a session referred from an AI surface; revenue attributed to an AI surface |
| Cell | One sub-market × vertical × buyer size. Verticals: skincare and beauty; B2B SaaS (business-to-business software as a service); high-CPA regulated (cost per acquisition: credit cards, insurance, supplements); local and multi-location added separately. Sizes: SMB (small and medium business), mid-market, enterprise |
| spend / attention / none — checked / blank | A cell read: money moved; interest only; every signal run and nothing found; not yet checked |
| Strict / loose | Whether a channel blocked-and-logged counts as checked for a `none` read: no (strict) or yes (loose) |
| Tier | Source provenance 1–7: 1 measured by us; 2 filed (filing, court exhibit); 3 platform's own pages; 4 panel or clickstream; 5 vendor study with n; 6 vendor blog, no n; 7 listicle |
| Source kind | vendor-reported; analyst-derived; filed; company-stated; measured-by-us |
| Gold / Silver / Bronze | Case grades: causal design (holdout, geo-split, switchback); pre/post with an unaffected control; visibility change only |
| raw / rule-1 | Two grade readings: as the screener assigned it; the literal rule that any missing item of the seven-item bar caps a case at Bronze |

| Load-bearing claim | A compiled claim a finding rests on; the bar is 80% at tier 3 or better |
| Hypothesis marks | confirmed; killed; unresolved — checked; not produced (producing work did not run); dual = two marks under two readings |
