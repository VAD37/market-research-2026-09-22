# Brand visibility inside AI assistants — what the evidence supports

As of 2026-09-23. Research only: evidence, no verdict; the reader decides. Every figure names its source kind and date; tier 1 is strongest, 7 weakest (glossary).

## The answer

1. Demand exists, thinly, mostly at enterprises. On the 2026-09-23 read, 8 of 27 segment cells show a spend signal under both the strict and loose readings; nearly all rest on job postings, the weakest spend class. Willingness to pay is unknown in 27 of 27 cells (analyst-derived, 2026-09-23).
2. Paid inventory is live inside four assistants (company-stated, 3, 2026-09-23). One engine discloses revenue: "$1 billion in annualized revenue run rate" (OpenAI, company-stated, 3, 2026-08-31). Rate cards published: 0 of 8 engines.
3. No sub-market has a measured size (analyst-derived, 6, 2026-09-22); every dollar size is a forecast.
4. Brands that moved changed content and tooling, not paid media: of 108 cases, content 63, tooling 58, paid media 2 (measured-by-us, 2026-09-23).
5. Where it stops: no published case shows causal sales lift — 0 Gold in ~980 candidates screened (2026-09-22) and ~1,640 more (2026-09-23). The two highest-tier controlled cases run negative or null.

| Copilot; Rufus | Microsoft's assistant; Amazon's shopping assistant |
