The answer

Brands have started paying to be seen inside AI assistants; the evidence stops before sales

Demand is real, thin and mostly large-company. One engine sells ads at scale. No published case shows sales caused.
Demand
Money spent in 8 of 27 buyer segments (sub-market × industry × company size), on both readings (analyst-derived, 2026-09-23). Price any buyer paid: unknown in all 27.
Supply
ChatGPT Ads: "$1 billion in annualized revenue run rate" (company-stated, 2026-08-31). Engines: "0 of 8 publish a rate card" (pulled 2026-09-22).
Size
No sub-market has a measured size; every dollar size is a forecast.
Proof
0 causal-design cases in ~980 candidates (2026-09-22) and ~1,640 more (2026-09-23).
Movers
Of 108 cases: content changes 63, new tools 58, paid media 2 (measured-by-us, 2026-09-23).

What this is

Three sub-markets, three different buyers, three metrics that are never crossed

Brands want to be named when someone asks an assistant what to buy. They can try three ways.

Organic recommendation

Being cited unpaid in the answer. Also called GEO or AEO (generative / answer engine optimisation).
Buyer: SEO (search engine optimisation) and brand teams.

Paid placement

Ads shown inside or beside the assistant's answer.
Buyer: media buyers.

Agentic commerce

The assistant completes the checkout for the shopper.
Buyer: e-commerce teams.

Appendix · Anticipated questions

Questions a director is likely to ask, answered from the record

Is there a market today? Money moves in 8 of 27 segments. No sub-market has a measured size.

How large could it get? Only forecasts exist; for agentic, 2030 forecasts run $190B–$5T, a 26.3× spread.

Does it work? 0 causal-design cases; brands confirm a vendor's case in 1 of 168.

Who pays? Mostly enterprise: 5 of the 7 spend segments on the 2026-09-22 read; 1 of 9 small-business segments.

What do buyers pay? Unknown in all 27 segments. Vendor list prices run "$20/mo" to "$999/month".

Why does traffic matter here? A court exhibit shows "83-93% drops in click-through rates"; People Inc filed a "63% decline in Google Search referrals over two years".

Where is an entrant most exposed? Platform capture: engines sell ads, checkout and measurement themselves, with "more than 50 technology and measurement partners" at OpenAI.

What would change the reading? One controlled result on sales; the six unscored hypotheses; the paid-credential sources.

Sources: as on the main slides; analyst-derived, measured-by-us, filed and company-stated, 2026-02 to 2026-09.
Appendix · About the evidence

How the evidence behind this deck was built

  • Internet sources only: company filings, court exhibits, regulator designations, platform help and pricing pages, panel vendors, vendor case studies, analyst forecasts, job postings.
  • No interviews, no outreach. Our own prompt runs against the engines were skipped (2026-09-23).
  • Evidence frozen at one snapshot; nothing later is cited. Every figure traces to a verbatim source pull held in the research record, with line references kept outside this deck.
  • Conflicting figures sit side by side; none is averaged. Where the record has no figure, the deck says "unknown" and names the channels checked.
  • The research produces evidence. The verdict belongs to the reader.
