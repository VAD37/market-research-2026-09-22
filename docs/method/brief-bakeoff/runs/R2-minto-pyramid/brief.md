# Brand visibility inside AI assistants: what the evidence shows

Evidence as of 2026-09-23. Each figure carries source kind and date; undated census counts are 2026-09. Tier ranks provenance, 1 strongest, 7 weakest. Acronyms: glossary.

## The question, and what the evidence supports

AI assistants (ChatGPT, Google, Microsoft Copilot, Perplexity, Claude) answer buying questions in prose. A brand appears in answers through three markets: **organic recommendation** (unpaid mention, bought as software and agency work), **paid placement** (ads inside the answer), **agentic commerce** (the assistant completes checkout). Does real demand exist, per buyer segment, and does anything show it pays?

**Answer: sellers are live and funded, and large companies are hiring; no published evidence shows it pays, and no market has a measured size.** One engine states a "$1 billion" annualized ad run rate (company-stated, 2026-08-31). One vendor raised "$180M Series D at $1.8B valuation" (vendor-reported, 2026-09-15). 8 of 27 buyer segments show spend, strict reading (analyst-derived, 2026-09-23). No published case of ~980 screened uses a holdout, geo-split or switchback (vendor-reported, 2026-09-22), nor of ~1,640 more (2026-09-23). Willingness to pay is unknown in all 27 segments.

## Who is there: engines own the rails; vendors funded, disclosure thin

**Engines.** Four sell ads inside answers (company-stated, 2026-09-23): ChatGPT, Google, Copilot, Amazon Rufus; another count exists. Claude refuses; Perplexity is winding ads down "by the end of 2026" (analyst-derived relay). 0 of 8 publish a rate card; the only stated price is OpenAI's "$3–$5 USD per click" bid guidance (company-stated). Checkout: 4 live programs; 1 of 4 discloses a fee (Copilot, 0%; company-stated, 2026-09); 6 of 6 protocol fee clauses `unknown — checked` 2026-09-22.

**Vendors (organic).** 34 rostered: 13 pure-play, 16 incumbent bundles, 5 agencies (measured-by-us, 2026-09). Disclosed (vendor-reported): price 14 of 34, customer count 8 of 34, funding 8 of 34, filed revenue 2 of 34 (neither broken out), prompt set 0 of 34. Semrush "AI products surpassed $38 million in ARR" (filed, 2026-03-02). Consolidation: Adobe–Semrush 2026-04-28 (filed); Sitecore–Scrunch 2026-06-03, value undisclosed by Sitecore, $225M per Bloomberg (press), unconfirmed; Yext–GoShine 2026-09-01 (filed).

## Does it work: no controlled result; best-sourced results negative

- Gold (causal design): 0 (vendor-reported cases, 2026-09-23).
- Silver (before/after with control): 7 as graded, 1 under grading rule 1 (vendor-reported cases, 2026-09-22); a re-pass adds +4 and +1 (2026-09-23).
- 2 of 7 Silvers run negative or null: NerdWallet cards revenue "decreased 24%" (filed, 2026-02-25); GEO levers move citation on "none" of ten engine families (preprint, 2026-09-07).
- Brands confirming a vendor case on their own domain: cumulative 1 of 168, Chime (measured-by-us, 2026-09-23); another reading exists.
- Paid ads: 15 screened, 0 cleared; 10 observational advertiser reports, 0 controlled (vendor-reported, 2026-09). One campaign: CTR 1.30% on 97,000+ impressions, "very few sign-ups" (vendor-reported, 2026-08-10).

What 108 movers changed: content 63, tooling 58, org 12, paid media 2, both Google's Direct Offers pilot (measured-by-us over self-reports, 2026-09). Evidence class, R2 reading: 1 experimental, 66 observational, 37 action only, 4 outside (measured-by-us, 2026-09-23); an R1 reading exists.

Blocked 2026-09-23: reddit.com, indeed.com, emarketer.com; paywalled: Crunchbase, Gartner, Forrester ("$1495" per report, company-stated).

## Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| AEO / GEO / SEO | Answer engine optimisation / generative engine optimisation / search engine optimisation. GEO never means geographic here |
| AI; API | Artificial intelligence; application programming interface, a machine-to-machine data feed |
| AI Overviews; AI Mode | Google's AI answer block on a results page; Google's conversational search tab |
| ARR | Annual recurring revenue |
| B2B SaaS | Business-to-business software sold as a subscription |
| CPA; high-CPA regulated | Cost per acquisition; the vertical where acquiring a customer is costly and regulated |
| CTR | Click-through rate: clicks divided by impressions |
| Direct Offers | Google's AI Mode ad pilot, unit labelled "Sponsored deal" |
| EU AI Act Art. 50 | EU transparency duties for AI systems; names no assistant |
| Gold / Silver / Bronze | Evidence grades: holdout, geo-split or switchback, causal / before-after with an unaffected control, correlational / visibility change only |
| Grading rule 1 | A case missing any of brand, engine, absolute date window, baseline, intervention, sample size, who measured is Bronze at best |
| Holdout; geo-split; switchback | Designs with a withheld control group; treated versus untreated regions; treatment alternated over time |
| Pixel; Conversions API | OpenAI's advertiser tracking tag and server-side conversion feed |
| Q1 | First calendar quarter |
| R1 / R2 | Two readings of before-after-with-control cases: R1 counts them experimental, R2 counts vendor-run ones observational |
| Rate card | A published unit price list |
| Rufus | Amazon's shopping assistant |
| SMB | Small and medium business |
| Spend / attention / none — checked / blank | Segment reads: money moved / interest without spend / every signal checked, none found / not yet checked |
| Strict / loose | Whether a blocked, logged channel counts as checked when a segment reads "none" |
| Tier | Source provenance: 1 measured by us, 2 filed, 3 platform's own page, 4 panel data, 5 vendor study, 6 marketing, 7 do not use |
| US; UK; EU; USD | United States; United Kingdom; European Union; US dollars |
| VLOSE | Very Large Online Search Engine, a designation under the EU Digital Services Act that brings ad-repository duties |
