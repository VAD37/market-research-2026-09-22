# Brand visibility inside AI assistants — what the evidence shows

As of 2026-09-23. Each figure carries source kind (vendor-reported, analyst-derived, filed, company-stated, measured-by-us) and date. Tier = provenance: 1 measured by us, 2 filed, 3 platform's own page, 4 panel, 5 vendor study, 6 vendor blog. Acronyms: glossary. Evidence only; the reading is yours.

## The question, and what the evidence supports

AI assistants — ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Perplexity, Claude — answer buying questions in prose. A brand can appear in those answers three ways, sold as three markets: **organic recommendation** (unpaid appearance; tools and agencies), **paid placement** (ads inside the answer), **agentic commerce** (the assistant executes checkout). In 2026 the engines switched the supply on. Is anyone buying, and does it pay?

**What the evidence supports:** money is moving on the supply side and in enterprise hiring; nothing published shows it pays. One engine states a "$1 billion" annualized ad run rate (company-stated, 2026-08-31); one vendor raised "$180M Series D at $1.8B valuation" (vendor-reported, 2026-09-15); 8 of 27 buyer segments show a spend signal, nearly all job postings (2026-09-23). Against that: 0 of ~980 published cases use a holdout, geo-split or switchback (2026-09-22); no sub-market has a measured size; no buyer discloses a price paid.

## 3. Who is there: engines own the rails; vendors funded, disclosure thin

**Engines.** Four sell ads inside answers on their own pages, 2026-09-23 — ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, Copilot, Amazon Rufus (a 2026-09-22 count: 5 of 8 live); Claude refuses; Perplexity winding down. 0 of 8 publish a rate card; the only price is OpenAI's "$3–$5 USD per click" bid guidance (company-stated 3). Google campaigns are auto-eligible: "you can't opt out". "Over 80%" of AI ad spend flows next to AI content, not inside chatbots (analyst-derived, 2026-06). Checkout: 4 live programs; fee disclosed 1 of 4 (Copilot, 0%); 6 of 6 protocol fee clauses unknown — checked 2026-09-22.

**Vendors.** 34 rostered, organic side; price disclosed 14 of 34, "$20–$999/month self-serve"; customer count 8 of 34; funding 8 of 34; filed revenue 2 of 34, neither broken out (vendor-reported; filed, 2026-09). Semrush "AI products surpassed $38 million in ARR", 2025-12 (filed 2). Consolidation: Semrush–Adobe 2026-04-28 (filed); Scrunch–Sitecore 2026-06-03, "not disclosed" (company-stated 3) against "$225 Million" (press, 5); Yext–GoShine 2026-09-01 (filed 2). 0 of 34 disclose the prompt set behind their visibility score.
