# Brand visibility inside AI assistants: what the evidence shows, as of 2026-09-23

**Brands have started paying to be found inside AI assistants, and the record stops well short of showing it pays.** 8 of 27 buyer cells show a spend signal (measured-by-us, 2026-09-23), most resting on job postings, not category budgets. Ads run inside answers on four engines; one discloses revenue. No published case shows a causal sales effect. No sub-market has a measured size. The highest-tier evidence runs negative: AI answers cut clicks to publishers. Research only; the verdict is yours.

## Three separate markets, and three metrics that are never mixed

| Sub-market | What is sold | Buyer |
|---|---|---|
| Organic recommendation (GEO, AEO, "AI visibility") | Brand appears unpaid in an AI answer | Brand marketing, SEO owner |
| Paid placement (AI ads) | Ad units inside AI answers | Media buyer |
| Agentic commerce | Checkout run by the assistant | E-commerce, payments |

Visibility (named in an answer), traffic (visits sent) and sales are counted apart; a visibility gain is never sales proof.

## Who is already there: engines own the inventory; vendors disclose little

**Engines.** Four sell ads inside answers (company-stated, 2026-09-23): ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, Microsoft Copilot, Amazon Rufus. A second count, 5 of 8 engines live (2026-09-22), uses another base. Claude will stay "ad-free" (company-stated, 2026-02-04). Of four live checkout programmes, one discloses a fee: Copilot "does not take a commission" (company-stated, 2026-09).

**Vendors.** 34 rostered on the organic side: 13 pure-play, 16 incumbent bundles, 5 agencies (analyst-derived, 2026-09). 14 of 34 publish a price, "$20/mo" to "$999/month" (vendor-reported, 2026-09). 2 of 34 file revenue, neither broken out (filed, FY2026). ## Glossary

- **AEO**: answer engine optimisation; alias for the organic sub-market.
- **AI**: artificial intelligence. **AI Overviews / AI Mode**: Google's AI answer block on a results page / Google's conversational search tab.
- **ARR**: annual recurring revenue.
- **Ads Manager, Pixel**: OpenAI's self-serve ad-buying tool and its conversion-tracking tag.
- **CMA**: UK Competition and Markets Authority.
- **DSA**: EU Digital Services Act. **VLOSE**: Very Large Online Search Engine, a DSA designation that carries an ad-repository duty.
- **EDGAR**: the US Securities and Exchange Commission's filings database. **OMR**: a German marketing-software review site.
- **EU / UK / US**: European Union / United Kingdom / United States.
- **EU AI Act Art. 50**: the Act's transparency article: notice of AI interaction and marking of synthetic content.
- **FY**: fiscal year.
- **GEO**: generative engine optimisation; alias for the organic sub-market, never geographic here. **GEO levers**: page edits a 2023 paper claimed raise citation.
- **Gold / Silver**: evidence grades. Gold, causal design (holdout, geo-split, switchback). Silver, before-and-after with an unaffected control; correlational.
- **Census grade / literal seven-item rule**: a case's grade as the screening pass assigned it / capped below Silver when any of seven items (brand, engine, date window, baseline, intervention, sample size, who measured) is missing.
- **IAC / People Inc; TW3 / Citead**: a US publisher group; the authors of a published replication study.
- **Industries tracked**: skincare and beauty; business software sold by subscription (B2B SaaS, business-to-business software as a service); high-cost regulated, meaning costly customer acquisition in credit cards, insurance, supplements.
- **R1 / R2**: two readings of "experimental": before-and-after with control counted as experimental (R1) or as observational (R2).
- **Rufus**: Amazon's shopping assistant.
- **SEO**: search engine optimisation, classical.
- **Series D**: a late-stage venture funding round.
- **Source kinds**: vendor-reported (seller about its own product), analyst-derived (third party computed it), filed (regulatory or court filing), company-stated (company about itself outside a filing), measured-by-us (this programme's own pull or count).
- **Spend / attention / none / blank**: cell reads: money moved / interest only / every signal run, nothing found / not yet checked. **Strict / loose**: whether a still-blocked channel counts as checked.
- **Tier**: provenance score, 1 strongest (measured by us) to 7 weakest; 2 filed; 3 a platform's own page; 4 panel; 5 vendor study with sample size; 6 vendor blog.
- **USD**: US dollars.
