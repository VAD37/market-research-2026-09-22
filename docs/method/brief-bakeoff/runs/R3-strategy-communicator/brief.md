# Brand visibility inside AI assistants — what the evidence shows, as of 2026-09-23

**Brands have begun paying to be found inside AI assistants; the record stops well short of proof that it pays.** Eight of 27 buyer segments show a spend signal (repo count, 2026-09-23), nearly all enterprise hiring rather than category budget. Paid ad inventory is live on four engines; one discloses revenue. No published case shows a causal sales effect. No sub-market has a measured size. The highest-tier evidence is negative: AI answers are cutting clicks to publishers. Research only; the verdict is yours.

## What this is — three sub-markets, three metrics kept apart

| Sub-market | What is sold | Buyer |
|---|---|---|
| Organic recommendation (GEO, AEO, "AI visibility") | Brand appears unpaid inside an AI answer | Brand marketing, SEO owner |
| Paid placement (AI ads) | Ad units inside AI answers | Media buyer |
| Agentic commerce | Checkout executed by the agent | E-commerce, payments |

Visibility, traffic and sales are counted apart and never crossed; a visibility change is never sales proof.

## Who is already there — engines own the inventory; vendors disclose little

Engines. Four sell ads inside answers, per their own pages, 2026-09-23 (company-stated): ChatGPT ("over 40 countries"), Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, Microsoft Copilot, Amazon Rufus. Four checkout programmes are live; one fee disclosed — Copilot, "does not take a commission" (company-stated, 2026-09).

## Does it work — no causal proof; the highest-tier evidence runs negative

~980 published cases screened (repo count, 2026-09-22): Gold — holdout, geo-split or switchback — 0. Silver 7 as graded in raw, 1 under the literal seven-item rule; both stand (appendix); two of the seven run negative or null. Brand-side: 0 of 59 brands checked confirm a vendor case on their own domain (repo count, 2026-09-22). 108 brands that moved (repo count, 2026-09-23), reading R1: 6 experimental, 61 observational, 37 action named with outcome unknown, 4 outside; R2: 1 / 66 / 37 / 4. ## Glossary

- **AEO** — answer engine optimisation; alias for the organic sub-market.
- **AI Overviews / AI Mode** — Google's AI answer block on a results page / Google's conversational search tab.
- **ARR** — annual recurring revenue.
- **B2B SaaS** — business-to-business software as a service; a tracked vertical.
- **CMA** — UK Competition and Markets Authority.
- **CPC / CTR** — cost per click / click-through rate.
- **DSA** — EU Digital Services Act; **VLOSE** — Very Large Online Search Engine, a DSA designation carrying an ad-repository duty.
- **EDGAR** — the US Securities and Exchange Commission's filings database.
- **EU AI Act Art. 50** — transparency article: AI-interaction notice and synthetic-content marking, in force 2026-08-02.
- **FY** — fiscal year.
- **GEO** — generative engine optimisation; alias for the organic sub-market (never geographic here).
- **Gold / Silver / Bronze / Fools gold** — evidence grades: causal design / pre-post with control / visibility change only / revenue claim without baseline or control.
- **Grade as graded in raw / literal rule** — a case's grade as its census assigned it / capped at Bronze when any of seven bar items (brand, engine, date window, baseline, intervention, sample size, who measured) is absent.
- **High-CPA regulated** — vertical where customer acquisition (CPA, cost per acquisition) is costly and regulated: credit cards, insurance, supplements.
- **IAC / People Inc; TW3 / Citead** — a US publisher group; the authors of a published replication study.
- **R1 / R2** — two readings of "experimental": pre-post with control counted as experimental (R1) or observational (R2).
- **SEO** — search engine optimisation, classical.
- **SMB** — small and medium business, as the source defines it.
- **Source kinds** — vendor-reported (seller about its own product), analyst-derived (third party computed), filed (regulatory or court filing), company-stated (company about itself outside a filing), measured-by-us (produced by this programme's own pull or count; "repo count" above).
- **Spend / attention / none — checked / blank** — cell reads: money moved / interest only / every signal run, nothing found / not yet checked. **Strict / loose** — whether a still-blocked channel counts as checked.
- **Tier** — provenance score 1 (strongest, measured by us) to 7 (weakest); 2 filed, 3 platform's own page, 4 panel, 5 vendor study with n, 6 vendor blog.
- **WAU / MAU** — weekly / monthly active users, as each engine defines them.
