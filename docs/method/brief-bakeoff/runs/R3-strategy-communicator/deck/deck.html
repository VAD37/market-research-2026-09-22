Brand visibility and product recommendation inside AI assistants

Brands have started paying to be found inside AI assistants; the published record stops short of showing it pays

Research only: evidence, not a verdict
Evidence as of 2026-09
Assistants in scope: ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, Claude. AI: artificial intelligence. The programme produces evidence; the verdict belongs to the reader.

Engines now sell space inside answers, few buyers spend, and no published case shows it pays

What the evidence supports: spend has begun. Where it stops: market size and payback. The verdict is yours.

Appendix A1: glossary

AEO / GEO: answer / generative engine optimisation; names for the organic market. GEO never means geographic here.

AI: artificial intelligence. AI Overviews / AI Mode: Google's answer block on a results page / its conversational search tab.

Ads Manager, Pixel: OpenAI's self-serve ad-buying tool and its conversion-tracking tag.

ARR: annual recurring revenue. Run rate: current revenue pace, stated yearly.

CMA: UK Competition and Markets Authority.

DSA: EU Digital Services Act. VLOSE: Very Large Online Search Engine, a DSA designation with an ad-repository duty.

EU / UK / US: European Union / United Kingdom / United States.

EU AI Act Art. 50: transparency article: notice of AI interaction, marking of synthetic content.

FY: fiscal year. M / B / T: million / billion / trillion. USD: US dollars.

Gold / Silver: evidence grades. Gold: causal design (holdout, geo-split, switchback). Silver: before-and-after with an unaffected control.

Census grade / strict item rule: the grade the screen assigned / a cap below Silver when one of seven evidence items is missing.

IAC / People Inc: a US publisher group. TW3 / Citead: authors of a published replication study.

Reading R1 / R2: a before-and-after with control counted as experimental / as observational.

Rufus: Amazon's shopping assistant. SEO: classical search engine optimisation. Series D: a late-stage venture round.

Spend / attention / none / blank; strict / loose: segment reads and reading rules, defined on slide 10.

Tier; source kinds: defined on slide 4.
