The market splits into three sub-markets, each bought by a different team

Organic recommendation

Brand named in AI answers, unpaid. Called GEO (generative engine optimisation) or AEO (answer engine optimisation).
Buyer: marketing, or the SEO (search engine optimisation) owner.

Paid placement

Ads shown inside AI answers.

Buyer: media buyers.

Agentic commerce

The assistant runs the checkout for the user.

Buyer: e-commerce and payments teams.

Three metrics, never mixed: visibility (is the brand named), traffic (do users click through), sales (do they buy).

Verticals studied: skincare and beauty; B2B SaaS (business software sold by subscription); high-CPA regulated (cards, insurance, supplements; CPA: cost per acquisition); local multi-location businesses, added 2026-09-23.

The evidence stops at sales lift, prices paid, and what engines actually answer

Not shown by any source:

  • Causal sales lift from AI visibility.
  • Any sub-market's measured size, price paid, engine rate card, or checkout volume run by assistants.
  • What engines answer to buying questions: our own sampling ran one partial day (2026-09-22), then stopped.
  • Anything behind blocked or paywalled channels: Indeed, live Reddit, EMARKETER, Crunchbase, Gartner, Forrester (2026-09-23).

Caveats:

  • Published cases are winners; full-page re-grading moved 12 cases up, 0 down.
  • Figures are unaudited self-reports.
  • Load-bearing claims at tier 3 or better: 68.0% (17 of 25, 2026-09-22); 44.4% (12 of 27) and 44.0% (11 of 25), 2026-09-23. Different lists; all stand.
  • Absence is only as strong as the channels reached.
