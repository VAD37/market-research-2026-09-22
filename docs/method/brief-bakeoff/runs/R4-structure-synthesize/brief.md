# Brand visibility inside AI assistants — what the evidence shows

Research files as of 2026-09-23. Each figure carries source kind, date and tier: 1 strongest, 7 weakest. Acronyms: glossary. Figures carried two ways: appendix.

## 1. The answer

Brands are hiring for visibility inside AI assistants, and engines now sell ads inside answers. No published document shows it pays.

- **Demand is real but thin.** Spend signals appear in 8 of 27 buyer segments under both readings (2026-09-23). Willingness to pay is unknown in all 27.
- **Proof stops before payback.** Of ~980 published cases screened 2026-09-22 and ~1,640 more 2026-09-23 (counted by us), none uses a holdout, geo-split or switchback.
- **Size is forecast, not measured.** Disclosed revenue sets floors; every dollar market size is a forecast.

## 2. What this is

Brands want to be named when users ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot or Claude what to buy.

- **Organic recommendation** (GEO, AEO): brand named in AI answers, unpaid; bought by marketing or the SEO owner.
- **Paid placement**: ads inside AI answers; bought by media buyers.
- **Agentic commerce**: checkout run by the assistant; bought by e-commerce and payments teams.

Metrics kept separate: visibility, traffic, sales. Verticals: skincare and beauty, B2B SaaS, high-CPA regulated; local multi-location added 2026-09-23.

## 5. Who is already there

**Ads.** Four engines sell ads inside answers, 2026-09-23 (tier 3): ChatGPT, Google, Microsoft Copilot, Amazon Rufus; an earlier count reads 5 of 8. Perplexity is "winding down" ads (analyst-derived, 5, 2026-02-18). OpenAI states "tens of thousands of advertisers" (company-stated, 3, 2026-08-31; an earlier figure exists); panels count 820 to 7,378 (vendor-reported, 5, 2026-07). "0 of 8 publish a rate card" (tier 3, 2026-09-22). Only engine price: OpenAI's bid guidance, "$3–$5 USD per click" (tier 3, 2026-09-22).

**Checkout.** 4 programmes live: ChatGPT, Google, Copilot, Perplexity (company-stated, 3, 2026-09). Copilot "does not take a commission"; OpenAI: "Merchants pay a small fee", rate undisclosed (tier 3, 2025-09-29). Fee clauses: 6 of 6 open checkout protocols unknown (checked 2026-09-22).

**Tool vendors.** 34 rostered (2026-09): 14 disclose a price, 8 funding, 2 filed revenue, 0 the prompt set behind their score. Semrush, "AI products surpassed $38 million in ARR" (filed, 2, 2025-12), merged into Adobe 2026-04-28.

## 6. Who buys

27 segments: sub-market × vertical × buyer size. Read 2026-09-23, as spend/attention/none/blank: strict 8/1/17/1; loose 8/1/18/0. Earlier readings exist.

Organic holds 6 spend segments, paid 1, agentic 1 (2026-09-23). As read 2026-09-22: exactly 1 of 9 small-business segments read spend, and six of the seven spend reads rested on job postings: headcount, not category budget. Examples: e.l.f. Beauty's in-house "AEO … and GEO … team", and an agentic-commerce role at "$110,000.00/yr - $140,000.00/yr" (company-stated, 3, 2026-09-22). Strongest: insurer KKH's agency framework, "11,000,000.00 EUR", GEO among its themes (filed, 2, pulled 2026-09-23): a ceiling, not GEO spend.

Local multi-location, nine further segments: spend 1, attention 3, none 5 (2026-09-23). Willingness to pay: unknown in all.

## 7. What the published proof shows

Gold: 0. Silver: 7 as graded, 1 under the strict rule (2026-09-22); 4 more as graded, 1 strict (2026-09-23). Two of the seven 2026-09-22 Silvers, the highest-tier ones, run against the category: NerdWallet's filing (2) and the null replication (4).

| R2 reading (R1 differs) | Moved, experimental | Moved, observational | Action, outcome unknown | Outside |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 52 graded cases | 5 | 34 | 13 | — |
| 108 cases of brands that moved | 1 | 66 | 37 | 4 |

Of the 108, 99 name a change: content 63, tooling 58, hires 12, paid media 2 (measured-by-us, 2026-09-23). Against: People Inc "22% decline in Core Sessions", citing Google AI Overviews (filed, 2, 2026-08-03); HubSpot's blog "lost 5 million visits in a thirty-day period" (company-stated, 3, 2026-09-22). Brands confirming a vendor's claim on their own site: 1 of 168 (2026-09-23; an earlier count exists). Paid: 0 of 15 ad results controlled (2026-09-23); one reads 1.30% CTR on 97,000+ impressions, "very few sign-ups" (vendor-reported, 5, 2026-08-10).

## 8. What could kill it

Five of eleven risks, ranked by evidence tier, then breadth; rank 5, no payback, is section 7. Likelihood, impact, mitigation, owner: no source states one.

## 9. What the evidence does not show

- Causal sales lift; any sub-market size, price paid, engine rate card, or agent-executed checkout volume.
- What engines answer to buying prompts: our sampling ran one partial day (2026-09-22), then stopped.
- Anything behind blocked or paywalled channels (Indeed, live Reddit, EMARKETER, Crunchbase, Gartner, Forrester; 2026-09-23).

## Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| AI | artificial intelligence; here, assistant products such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, Claude |
| GEO / AEO | generative engine optimisation / answer engine optimisation: getting a brand named in AI answers, unpaid |
| SEO | search engine optimisation, the classical discipline |
| Copilot, Rufus | Microsoft's assistant; Amazon's shopping assistant |
| EU, UK, US | European Union, United Kingdom, United States |
| VLOSE | Very Large Online Search Engine, an EU Digital Services Act designation that brings ad-repository duties |
| EU AI Act Art. 50 | transparency duties for AI interaction and synthetic content |
| UK CMA | UK Competition and Markets Authority; its 2026-06-17 order requires fair ranking in search generative AI features |
| KKH | Kaufmännische Krankenkasse, a German health insurer |
| ARR | annual recurring revenue |
| LLM | large language model, the model behind an AI assistant |
| API | application programming interface |
| USD, EUR | US dollar, euro |
| Run rate | revenue annualised from a recent period, not booked revenue |
| CTR | click-through rate |
| B2B SaaS | business-to-business software sold by subscription |
| High-CPA regulated | cards, insurance, supplements; CPA is cost per acquisition |
| Buyer sizes | small business under 100 staff; mid-market 100–999; enterprise 1000 or more |
| Spend / attention / none / blank | segment reads: money moved; interest only; checked and empty; not yet checked |
| Strict / loose | strict: a blocked channel leaves the segment unchecked; loose: a blocked-and-logged channel counts as checked |
| Gold / Silver / Bronze | case grades: holdout, geo-split or switchback; pre/post with baseline and unaffected control; visibility change only |
| As graded / strict rule | grade as first assigned; grade capped at Bronze when any of seven evidence items is missing |
| R1 / R2 | R1 counts pre/post with a control as experimental; R2 counts vendor pre/post as observational |
| Holdout, geo-split, switchback | designs withholding treatment from a comparable group, region, or period |
| Tier | 1 measured by us; 2 filed; 3 platform's own page; 4 panel or telemetry; 5 vendor study with n; 6 vendor blog, no n; 7 listicle |
| Rate card | a published price list for ad units |
| EMARKETER | a market-research firm; its reports are paywalled |
| Prompt set | the fixed list of questions a vendor runs to score visibility |
| Open checkout protocol | a published specification for assistant-run checkout |
