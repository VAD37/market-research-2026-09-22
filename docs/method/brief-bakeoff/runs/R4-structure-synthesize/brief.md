# Brand visibility inside AI assistants — what the evidence shows

Date 2026-09-23; nothing cited is newer. Every figure is traced to the research files (appendix). No decision is asked of you. Each figure carries a source kind and a tier (1–7, lower is stronger; 2 filed, 3 a company's own page, 5 vendor study); the glossary defines both and every acronym.

## 1. The answer

Brands are hiring for this and vendors are selling it; nobody has published proof that it pays. Spend signals sit in 8 of 27 buyer segments (strict reading, 2026-09-23; the loose reading agrees), one of them small-business; no buyer discloses a price paid (27 of 27 unknown — checked). One engine reports "$1 billion in annualized revenue run rate" (company-stated, 2026-08-31); 0 of 8 engines publish a rate card. Of ~980 published cases screened, then ~1,640 more, zero use a holdout, geo-split, or switchback. Every dollar size is a forecast. The evidence supports "brands are moving" and stops before "it measurably pays."

## 2. What this is

Three metrics, never crossed: visibility, traffic, sales. Verticals: skincare and beauty, B2B SaaS, high-CPA regulated; local multi-location added 2026-09-23.

**Engines.** Four sell ads inside AI answers as of 2026-09-23 (a 2026-09-22 count reads 5 of 8 surfaces): ChatGPT, "tens of thousands of advertisers" (company-stated, 3) against panel counts of 820 to 7,378 (vendor-reported, 5); Google AI Overviews, existing campaigns auto-eligible, "you can't opt out"; Microsoft Copilot; Amazon Rufus, US, 2026-03-25. Checkout: 4 live programmes; fees stated by Copilot (0%) and OpenAI ("a small fee"); 6 of 6 protocol fee clauses unknown — checked 2026-09-22.

**Vendors.** 34 organic-tooling vendors rostered, 43 profiled: price disclosed 14 of 34, funding 8 of 34, filed revenue 2 of 34. Google's guidance: "no additional requirements... nor other special optimizations necessary" (company-stated, 3). 0 of 34 vendors publish the prompt set behind their score.

## 7. What the published proof shows

| Set | Metric moved, experimental | Metric moved, observational | Action named, outcome unknown |
|---|---|---|---|
| Graded cases, 52 — R1 / R2 | 10 / 5 | 29 / 34 | 13 / 13 |
| Brands that moved, 108 — R1 / R2 | 6 / 1 | 61 / 66 | 37 / 37 |

Gold, 0. Silver: 7 as graded, 1 under the strict rule (2026-09-22); 4 more as graded, 1 strict (2026-09-23). Every positive Silver is tier 5, vendor- or practitioner-measured. The two highest-tier Silvers run against the category: NerdWallet's filing (tier 2) and the ten-family null (tier 4). Beside them: People Inc "22% decline in Core Sessions" (filed, 2026-08-03); HubSpot blog "5 million" fewer visits in thirty days (company-stated, 2026-09-22). Brand-side corroboration: 0 of 59 (2026-09-22); 1 of 168 (2026-09-23). Paid: 15 advertiser results screened, 0 controlled; the best is 1.30% CTR on 97,000+ impressions, "very few sign-ups" (vendor-reported, 5). Of 108 brands that moved, 99 name a change: content 63, tooling 58, hires 12, paid media 2.

## 9. What the evidence does not show

- Causal sales lift from any intervention, organic or paid.
- Whether AI referral converts above organic search: Adobe "AI Conversion Now 42% Higher" compares AI to non-AI visits (vendor-reported, 4); Shopify "nearly 50% higher rates than organic search" is tier 5.
- Any sub-market size, price paid, engine rate card, or per-engine retail breakout.
- What engines return to buying prompts: our sampling ran one partial day, 2026-09-22, Vietnam-localised (0 ad units in 90 Google runs), then was skipped 2026-09-23.
- Anything behind blocked channels (Indeed, EMARKETER, live Reddit) or paywalls (Crunchbase, Gartner, Forrester).

## Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| AI | artificial intelligence; here, assistant products such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, Claude |
| GEO / AEO | generative engine optimisation / answer engine optimisation — getting a brand named in AI answers, unpaid; "AI visibility" is a synonym |
| AI Overviews, AI Mode | Google's AI answer block on a results page; Google's conversational search tab |
| Copilot, Rufus | Microsoft's assistant; Amazon's shopping assistant |
| VLOSE, DSA | Very Large Online Search Engine, a designation under the EU Digital Services Act that brings ad-repository duties |
| EU AI Act Art. 50 | transparency duties for AI interaction and synthetic content, in force 2026-08-02 |
| CMA | UK Competition and Markets Authority |
| ARR | annual recurring revenue |
| CPC, CTR | cost per click; click-through rate |
| SEO | search engine optimisation, the classical discipline |
| B2B SaaS | business-to-business software sold by subscription — a tracked vertical |
| High-CPA regulated | cards, insurance, supplements — costly, regulated customer acquisition; CPA is cost per acquisition |
| SMB | small and medium business, under 100 headcount as banded here; mid-market 100–999; enterprise 1,000 or more |
| Visibility / traffic / sales | brand named in an AI answer; a session referred from one; revenue attributed to one — never crossed without a stated link |
| Spend / attention / none / blank | a segment's demand read: money moved; interest only; checked and empty; not yet checked |
| Strict / loose | strict: every signal checked, a blocked channel counts as unchecked; loose: a blocked-and-logged channel counts as checked |
| Gold / Silver / Bronze | case grades: causal design (holdout, geo-split, switchback); pre/post with a control; visibility change only |
| As graded / strict rule | grade as the census assigned it; grade capped at Bronze if any of seven bar items is missing on the case's own page |
| R1 / R2 | R1 counts pre/post with control as experimental; R2 counts vendor pre/post as observational |
| Holdout, geo-split, switchback | experimental designs that withhold treatment from a comparable group, region, or period |
| Tier | 1–7 provenance score; 1 our measurement, 2 filed, 3 company page, 4 panel data, 5 vendor study, 6 vendor blog, 7 listicle |
| Protocol | open checkout specification (OpenAI/Stripe's ACP, Google's UCP, and four others) |
| Prompt set | the fixed list of questions a vendor runs to score visibility |
| Rate card | a published price list; none exists for any AI ad unit |
| Run rate | one month's revenue times twelve, not booked revenue |
