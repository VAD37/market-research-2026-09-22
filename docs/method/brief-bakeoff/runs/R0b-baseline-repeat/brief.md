# Brand visibility inside AI assistants: what the evidence supports, as of 2026-09-23

Question: is there real demand, by segment, for brands to be recommended inside AI assistants (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, Claude), and does it work? Three sub-markets, never merged: organic (brand named unpaid), paid (ads inside answers), agentic (assistant completes the purchase). Figures carry source kind, evidence tier (lower is stronger), date. Terms: appendix D; prices: appendix E.

## 1. Demand is thin and enterprise-heavy; no published case shows a sales effect

- 8 of 27 segment cells (sub-market × vertical × buyer size) read spend, 1 attention, under both strict and loose readings (measured-by-us, 2026-09-23); an earlier tally exists. Willingness to pay: unknown in all 27.
- No holdout, geo-split or switchback in ~980 candidates screened to 2026-09-22 or ~1,640 more on 2026-09-23 (measured-by-us).

## 2. Why now: ad inventory, lost clicks and new rules, all dated 2026

- Inventory: ChatGPT Ads live 2026-02-09, US pilot; "$1 billion in annualized revenue run rate", "over 40 countries" by 2026-08-31 (company-stated, tier 3).
- Lost clicks, filed, tier 2: NerdWallet credit-cards revenue "decreased 24%" (8-K, 2026-02-25); People Inc "63% decline in Google Search referrals over two years" (2026-05-04); Microsoft "83-93% drops in click-through rates" (court exhibit, 2026-09-17).
- Rules, filed, tier 2: EU AI Act Art. 50 in force 2026-08-02; ChatGPT designated a VLOSE 2026-08-31, 159.1M EU recipients, obligations from January 2027; UK CMA fair-ranking requirement on "search generative AI features", 2026-06-17.

## 5. Evidence ladder: existence is proven, demand is inferred, payback is assumed

- Proven, paid inventory at scale: four engines sell ads, 2026-09-23; $1B run rate (company-stated 3).
- Proven, AI answers cut publisher referrals: 8-K, investor deck, court exhibit (filed 2).
- Inferred, enterprises hire for it: e.l.f., Pennylane, Cigna postings (company-stated 3); intent, not spend.
- Inferred, AI referrals convert better: Adobe "Is Now 54% Higher", May 2026 (vendor-reported 4); earlier reading exists.
- Inferred, content changes move visibility: 7 Silver as graded, 1 under literal rule (2026-09-22); 4 and 1 more (2026-09-23); vendor-reported 5.
- Assumed, any intervention lifts sales: 0 Gold; 0 of 15 ad results controlled (measured-by-us, 2026-09-23).

Evidence quality, measured-by-us, 2026-09-23. 52 proof cases: experimental 10 (reading R1) / 5 (R2); observational 29 / 34; action only 13. 108 brand moves: experimental 6 / 1; observational 61 / 66; action only 37; unclassed 4. Brands confirming a vendor case: cumulative 1 of 168 (Chime). Negative: 2 of 7 first-round Silvers — NerdWallet (filed 2); a replication where 2023 levers move citation on "none" of ten engine families (analyst-derived 4, 2026-09-07).

## 6. Risks: referral loss and regulation carry the strongest evidence

Ranked by evidence tier, then breadth, not severity (2026-09-23); unlabelled tiers: 2 filed, 3 company-stated. Signal = early signal.

1. Referral collapse: "83-93% drops" (2); "decreased 24%" (2). Signal: EDGAR full-text "AI Overviews".
2. Regulation: VLOSE duty January 2027 (2); CMA (2); AI Overviews ads exclude finance, healthcare (3). Signal: EU designation list.
3. Vendor consolidation: Semrush–Adobe (2); Yext–GoShine (2). Signal: EDGAR 8-K on rostered vendors.
4. Platform capture: OpenAI Ads Manager, Pixel, "more than 50 technology and measurement partners" (3). Signal: engine help centres.
5. No payback evidence: 1.30% CTR, "very few sign-ups"; "just 3%" of $250K spent (vendor-reported 5). Signal: EDGAR "ChatGPT ads".
6. Measurement opacity: ad presence 0.8%–26%, unreconciled (vendor-reported 5). Signal: vendor method pages.
7–8. "Do nothing" suffices; engines reverse policy: Google guidance (3); Claude ad-free (3).
9–11. Agentic fees; manipulation, spam policy naming "manipulate generative AI responses", 2026-08-28 (3); unsizeable market, 18 figures, 15 forecasts, 0 measured (analyst-derived 6).

Likelihood, impact, mitigation and owner: no source states one.
