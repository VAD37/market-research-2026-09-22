# Brand visibility inside AI assistants — what the evidence supports, as of 2026-09-23

Question: does real demand exist, per segment, for brand visibility and product recommendation inside AI assistants (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, Claude), and what evidence shows it works. Three sub-markets, never merged: organic recommendation (brand appears unpaid in an answer), paid placement (ads inside answers), agentic commerce (checkout executed by the agent). Every figure carries source kind, tier (1 strongest, 7 weakest) and date; acronyms in appendix D.

## 1. Demand is visible, thin and enterprise-heavy; no published case proves a sales effect

Eight of 27 segment cells read spend, one attention (both readings, 2026-09-23); the only small-business spend cell rests on one job posting; willingness to pay is unknown in 27 of 27. Paid inventory is live on ChatGPT, Google, Copilot and Amazon; Claude refuses it. Zero of ~980 published cases screened uses a holdout, geo-split or switchback (2026-09-22). No sub-market has a measured size.

## 2. Why now: inventory, referral loss and regulation all carry 2026 dates

- ChatGPT Ads live 2026-02-09, US pilot; "$1 billion in annualized revenue run rate", "over 40 countries", 2026-08-31 — company-stated, tier 3.
- Referral loss filed: NerdWallet credit-cards revenue "decreased 24%", 8-K 2026-02-25; People Inc "63% decline in Google Search referrals over two years", 2026-05-04; Microsoft "83-93% drops in click-through rates" for news domains, court exhibit 2026-09-17 — filed, tier 2.
- Rules: EU AI Act Art. 50 in force 2026-08-02; ChatGPT designated VLOSE 2026-08-31, 159.1M EU recipients, ad-repository duty from January 2027; UK CMA fair-ranking requirement covering "search generative AI features", 2026-06-17 — filed, tier 2.
