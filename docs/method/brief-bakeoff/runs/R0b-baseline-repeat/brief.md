# Brand visibility inside AI assistants: what the evidence supports, as of 2026-09-23

The question: does real demand exist, by segment, for brands to be visible and recommended inside AI assistants (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, Claude), and does anything show it works? Three sub-markets, never merged: organic (brand named unpaid in an answer), paid (ads inside answers), agentic (the assistant completes the purchase). Each figure carries source kind, tier (1 strongest, 7 weakest) and date. Terms: appendix D.

## 1. Demand is visible but thin and enterprise-heavy; no published case shows a sales effect

- 8 of 27 segment cells read spend, 1 attention, under both readings (analyst-derived, 2026-09-23). Willingness to pay: unknown in 27 of 27.
- Ads run inside ChatGPT, Google, Copilot and Amazon answers; Claude refuses them (company-stated, tier 3, 2026-09-23).
- No holdout, geo-split or switchback in ~980 cases screened 2026-09-22, nor in ~1,640 more screened 2026-09-23 (measured-by-us).
- No sub-market has a measured size (2026-09-23).

## 2. Why now: ad inventory, lost clicks and new rules all carry 2026 dates

- Inventory: ChatGPT Ads live 2026-02-09 as a US pilot; "$1 billion in annualized revenue run rate", "over 40 countries" by 2026-08-31 (company-stated, tier 3).
- Lost clicks: NerdWallet credit-cards revenue "decreased 24%" (8-K, 2026-02-25); People Inc "63% decline in Google Search referrals over two years" (2026-05-04); Microsoft "83-93% drops in click-through rates" for news domains (court exhibit, 2026-09-17). Filed, tier 2.
- Rules: EU AI Act Art. 50 in force 2026-08-02; ChatGPT designated a VLOSE 2026-08-31, 159.1M EU recipients, ad-repository duty from January 2027; UK CMA fair-ranking requirement covering "search generative AI features", 2026-06-17. Filed, tier 2.

## 5. Evidence ladder: existence is proven, demand is inferred, payback is assumed

| Rung | Claim | Evidence (kind, tier) |
|---|---|---|
| Proven | Paid ad inventory exists at scale | engine pages; $1B run rate (company-stated 3) |
| Proven | AI answers cut referral traffic for named publishers | 8-K, 10-Q, court exhibit (filed 2) |
| Proven | Checkout protocols readable without a contract | Apache-2.0 repositories (company-stated 3) |
| Inferred | Enterprises in each tracked vertical hire for it: e.l.f., Pennylane, Cigna | job postings (company-stated 3); intent, not spend |
| Inferred | AI referrals convert better | Adobe "42% Higher" than non-AI visits, Mar 2026 (vendor-reported 4); Shopify "nearly 50% higher" than organic search, Q1 2026 (vendor-reported 5) |
| Inferred | Content changes move visibility | 7 Silver as graded, 1 under the literal rule (2026-09-22); 4 and 1 more (2026-09-23); vendor-reported 5 |
| Assumed | Any intervention lifts sales | 0 Gold; 0 of 15 ad results controlled (2026-09-23) |
| Assumed | Market size | 0 measured; forecasts tier 5–6 |

Evidence quality, 2026-09-23, measured-by-us. Of 52 proof cases: experimental 10 (reading R1) / 5 (R2); observational 29 / 34; action named, outcome unknown 13. Of 108 cases of brands that moved: experimental 6 / 1; observational 61 / 66; action only 37; unclassed 4. Brands confirming a vendor's case on their own site: 1 of 168 checked (Chime), 2026-09-23; an earlier reading exists. The two highest-tier Silvers run against the category: NerdWallet (filed 2), and a replication finding 2023 optimisation levers move citation on "none" of ten engine families (analyst-derived 4, 2026-09-07).
