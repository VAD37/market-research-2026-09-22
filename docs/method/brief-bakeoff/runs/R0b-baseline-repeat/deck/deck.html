Case grades for "does it work"

  • Gold: holdout, geographic split or switchback test
  • Silver: before and after, with an unaffected control
  • Bronze: visibility changed; sales not shown
  • Fools gold: revenue claim with no baseline or control

Under the strict reading of the grading bar, a case missing any one of seven required items is Bronze at best.
