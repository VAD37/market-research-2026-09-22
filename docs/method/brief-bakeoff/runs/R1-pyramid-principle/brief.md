# Brand visibility inside AI assistants — what the evidence shows as of 2026-09-23

Research only: evidence and where it stops, no recommendation. Every figure carries a source kind and date; tier is provenance, 1 strongest, 7 weakest. Acronyms in the glossary; contested figures, both readings, in Appendix A.

## The question, and what the evidence supports

**Situation.** AI assistants (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, Claude) answer buying questions directly. Three things are sold around that answer, kept separate: **organic recommendation** (brand named unpaid), **paid placement** (ads inside the answer), **agentic commerce** (checkout by the agent). Three metrics — visibility, traffic, sales — are never crossed.

**Complication.** ChatGPT began selling ads 2026-02-09 and stated a "$1 billion in annualized revenue run rate" on 2026-08-31 (company-stated); the largest visibility vendor raised at a "$1.8B valuation" 2026-09-15 (vendor-reported); publishers filed referral declines. Whether any of this pays for a brand is open.

**Answer.** As of 2026-09-23 the evidence shows demand at the level of hiring and inventory, none at the level of payback: 8 of 27 buyer cells show a spend signal, nearly all job postings, mostly enterprise; four engines sell ads inside answers, one discloses revenue; no published case shows a causal sales effect (0 Gold in ~980 screened); no sub-market has a measured size; no buyer discloses a price paid.

## 5. Does it work: no causal case; the strongest filings run negative

- ~980 cases screened; Gold 0 — no holdout, geo-split or switchback (vendor-reported, 5, 2026-09-22). Silver 7 as graded, 1 under the literal rule.
- 108 transition cases, two readings: R1 6 experimental / 61 observational / 37 action named, outcome unknown; R2 1 / 66 / 37 (measured-by-us, 2026-09-23). 99 name a change: content ops 63, stack 58, org 12, paid media 2.
- Brand-side corroboration: 0 of 59 brands confirm a vendor case on their own domain (measured-by-us, 3).
- Paid ads: 15 results screened, 0 controlled. One vendor's own campaigns: CTR 1.30% on 97,000+ impressions, "very few sign-ups" (vendor-reported, 5, 2026-08-10).
- Negative tail, filed, 2: Microsoft "83-93% drops in click-through rates" (2026-09-17); NerdWallet cards revenue "decreased 24%" (2026-02-25); People Inc "63% decline in Google Search referrals over two years" (2026-05-04).
- Null: GEO levers move citation on "none" of ten engine families (preprint, 4, 2026-09-07).

## Glossary

- **AEO / GEO** — answer engine optimisation / generative engine optimisation: aliases for the organic sub-market.
- **AI Overviews / AI Mode** — Google's AI answer block on a results page / Google's conversational search tab.
- **ARR** — annual recurring revenue. **Run rate** — one month's revenue × 12, not booked revenue.
- **B2B SaaS** — business-to-business software as a service, one of three tracked verticals. **High-CPA regulated** — credit cards, insurance, supplements (CPA: cost per acquisition). **SMB** — small and medium business.
- **Cell** — one sub-market × vertical × buyer-size combination; read as **spend**, **attention**, **none — checked**, or **blank** (not checked).
- **Strict / loose** — strict: a still-blocked channel counts as unchecked; loose: a blocked-and-logged channel counts as checked.
- **Gold / Silver / Bronze** — case grades: Gold = holdout, geo-split or switchback (causal); Silver = pre/post with a control, correlational; Bronze = visibility change only.
- **As graded / literal rule** — Silver as the census assigned it / capped at Bronze when any of seven bar items is missing on the case's own page.
- **R1 / R2** — two readings of "pre/post with control": R1 counts it experimental, R2 observational.
- **Tier** — provenance score, 1 (measured by us) to 7 (listicle); 2 filed, 3 platform's own page, 4 panel, 5 vendor study, 6 vendor blog.
- **Source kinds** — vendor-reported, analyst-derived, filed, company-stated, measured-by-us.
- **CPC / CTR** — cost per click / click-through rate.
- **CMA** — UK Competition and Markets Authority. **DSA** — EU Digital Services Act. **VLOSE** — Very Large Online Search Engine, a DSA designation carrying an ad-repository duty. **EU AI Act Art. 50** — transparency obligations in force 2026-08-02.
- **8-K / 10-K** — US SEC current / annual report.
- **Direct Offers** — Google AI Mode ad pilot, unit labelled "Sponsored deal".
