# Brand visibility inside AI assistants — what the evidence shows as of 2026-09-23

Research only: the evidence and where it stops; the decision is yours. Each figure carries source kind, tier (provenance, 1 strongest, 7 weakest) and date. Acronyms: glossary. Figures read two ways: Appendix A.

AI assistants (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, Claude) now answer buying questions. Three things are sold around the answer: **organic recommendation** (brand named unpaid), **paid placement** (ads in the answer), **agentic commerce** (the assistant checks out). Visibility, traffic and sales are measured separately.

Since 2026-02 engines charge for this space, investors fund tooling for it, and publishers file referral declines. Do brands want it, and does it pay?

**As of 2026-09-23 the evidence shows demand at the level of hiring and ad inventory, none at the level of payback.** Spend signals rest mostly on job postings; no published case shows a causal sales effect; no sub-market has a measured size; no buyer discloses a price paid.

## 3. Who is already there: many sellers, little disclosure

**Organic tooling.** 34 vendors rostered 2026-09 (analyst-derived). Price disclosed by 14 of 34, "$20/mo" to "$999/month" self-serve (vendor-reported, 2026-09); filed revenue by 2 of 34, neither broken out for this product (filed, 5, FY2026). Semrush "AI products surpassed $38 million in ARR", 2025-12 (filed, 2). Sitecore bought Scrunch 2026-06-03, value "not disclosed" (company-stated, 3) beside "$225 Million" (analyst-derived, 5). None of the 34 discloses the prompt set behind its visibility score (vendor-reported, 3, 2026-09-22).

**Paid inventory.** Four engines sell ads in answers — ChatGPT, Google, Copilot, Amazon (company-stated, 3, 2026-09-23); an earlier count reads 5 of 8. Only OpenAI discloses ad revenue. No engine publishes a rate card; OpenAI's recommended max bid, "$3–$5 USD per click", is the only engine-stated price (company-stated, 3, 2026-09-22). ChatGPT advertisers: "tens of thousands" (company-stated, 3, 2026-08-31) against panel counts of 820 to 7,378 (vendor-reported, 5, 2026-07). Claude and the Gemini app sell none.

**Agentic checkout.** Of four live engine checkout programmes, only Copilot states a fee: it "does not take a commission" (company-stated, 3, 2026-09). Protocol fee clauses: 6 of 6 unknown — checked 2026-09-22.

## 5. Does it work: no causal case; the strongest filings run negative

- Gold (holdout, geo-split or switchback): 0 in ~980 cases screened 2026-09-22, 0 in ~1,640 more 2026-09-23 (vendor-reported cases, 5). Silver: 7 as graded, 1 literal (2026-09-22); 2026-09-23 adds 4 as graded, 1 literal, all outside the three verticals.
- Quality of evidence, 108 brand transition cases (measured-by-us, 2026-09-23). R1: 6 experimental / 61 observational / 37 action named, outcome unknown; R2: 1 / 66 / 37. 99 name a change: content 63, tooling 58, organisation 12, paid media 2.
- Brand-side confirmation of a vendor's case: 1 of 168 brands checked, cumulative to 2026-09-23 (measured-by-us, 3).
- Paid ads: 0 of 15 results screened have a control (measured-by-us, 2026-09-23). SE Ranking's own campaigns: CTR 1.30% on 97,000+ impressions, "very few sign-ups" (vendor-reported, 5, 2026-08-10).
- Negative filings (filed, 2): NerdWallet credit cards revenue "decreased 24%" (2026-02-25); People Inc "63% decline in Google Search referrals over two years" (2026-05-04); Microsoft, section 1.
- Null: 2023 GEO levers move citation on "none" of ten engine families (analyst-derived preprint, 4, 2026-09-07).

## Glossary

- **AEO / GEO** — answer engine optimisation / generative engine optimisation: names for the organic sub-market. **GEO levers** — content tactics proposed in a 2023 paper to raise citation.
- **ARR** — annual recurring revenue. **Run rate** — current revenue annualised, not booked revenue.
- **B2B SaaS** — business-to-business software sold as a service. **High-CPA regulated** — credit cards, insurance, supplements; CPA is cost per acquisition. **SMB** — small and medium business. **DNP** — a news publisher group, co-plaintiff with The Times.
- **Segment (cell)** — one sub-market × vertical × buyer-size combination, read as **spend**, **attention**, **none — checked** or **blank** (not checked).
- **Strict / loose** — strict: a still-blocked channel leaves the cell unchecked; loose: a blocked-and-logged channel counts as checked.
- **Gold / Silver / Bronze** — case grades. Gold: holdout, geo-split or switchback (causal). Silver: before/after with an unaffected control, correlational. Bronze: visibility change only.
- **As graded / literal rule** — Silver as the case census assigned it / capped at Bronze when any of seven required items is missing on the case's own page.
- **R1 / R2** — two readings of "before/after with a control": R1 counts it experimental, R2 observational.
- **Tier** — provenance: 1 measured by us, 2 filed, 3 platform's own page, 4 panel or clickstream, 5 vendor study, 6 vendor blog, 7 listicle.
- **Source kinds** — vendor-reported, analyst-derived, filed, company-stated, measured-by-us.
- **CTR** — click-through rate. **CPC** — cost per click.
- **CMA** — UK Competition and Markets Authority. **DSA** — EU Digital Services Act. **VLOSE** — Very Large Online Search Engine, a DSA designation carrying an ad-repository duty from January 2027 for ChatGPT. **EU AI Act Art. 50** — AI transparency obligations.
