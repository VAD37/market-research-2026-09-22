# Brand visibility inside AI assistants: what the evidence shows as of 2026-09-23

Research only; the decision is yours. Each figure carries source kind, tier (provenance: 1 strongest, 7 weakest) and date; undated figures come from pulls dated 2026-09-22. Acronyms: glossary. † marks a figure with another reading; both stand in Appendix A.

AI assistants (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, Claude) now answer buying questions. Three things are sold around the answer: **organic recommendation** (the brand named, unpaid), **paid placement** (ads in the answer) and **agentic commerce** (the assistant checks out).

Since 2026-02-09 engines have sold ads in answers, investors have funded visibility tooling, and publishers have filed referral losses. Do brands want this, and does it pay?

**As of 2026-09-23 the evidence shows demand at the level of job postings and ad inventory, and none at the level of payback.** Spend rests mostly on postings; no published case shows a causal sales effect, no sub-market has a measured size, and no buyer discloses a price paid.

## Who is already there: many sellers, little disclosure

**Organic tooling.** 34 vendors rostered 2026-09 (analyst-derived). 14 of 34 disclose a price, "$20/mo" to "$999/month" self-serve (vendor-reported, 2026-09); 2 of 34 file revenue, neither broken out for this product (filed, 5, FY2026). Semrush: "AI products surpassed $38 million in ARR", 2025-12 (filed, 2). "0 of 34 rostered vendors disclose a fixed published prompt set with n" (vendor-reported, 3).

**Paid inventory.** Four engines sell ads inside answers: ChatGPT, Google, Copilot, Amazon Rufus (company-stated, 3, 2026-09-23)†. 0 of 8 engines publish a rate card; 1 of 8 discloses ad revenue (company-stated, 3). Only engine-stated price: OpenAI's max-bid guidance, "$3–$5 USD per click" (company-stated, 3). ChatGPT advertisers: "tens of thousands" (company-stated, 3, 2026-08-31)† against panel counts of 820 to 7,378 (vendor-reported, 5, 2026-07).

**Agentic checkout.** 1 of 4 live engine checkout programmes states a fee: Copilot Checkout "does not take a commission or affiliate fee" (company-stated, 3, 2026-09). Protocol fee clauses: 6 of 6 `unknown — checked` 2026-09-22.

## Glossary

- **AI** — artificial intelligence. **AI Act Art. 50** — EU transparency obligations for AI systems.
- **EU / UK / US** — European Union / United Kingdom / United States. **USD** — US dollars. **FY** — fiscal year.
- **ARR** — annual recurring revenue. **Run rate** — current revenue annualised, not booked revenue.
- **B2B SaaS** — business-to-business software sold as a service. **High-CPA regulated** — credit cards, insurance, supplements; CPA is cost per acquisition. **SMB** — small and medium business.
- **CMA** — UK Competition and Markets Authority. **DSA** — EU Digital Services Act. **VLOSE** — Very Large Online Search Engine, a DSA designation carrying an ad-repository duty from 2027-01 for ChatGPT.
- **CTR** — click-through rate. **DNP** — a news-publisher plaintiff group, co-plaintiff with The Times.
- **GEO** — generative engine optimisation: content tactics aimed at being cited by AI answers.
- **Segment (cell)** — one sub-market × vertical × buyer size, read **spend**, **attention**, **none — checked** or **blank** (not checked).
- **Strict / loose** — strict: a still-blocked channel leaves the cell unchecked; loose: a blocked-and-logged channel counts as checked.
- **Gold / Silver / Bronze** — case grades. Gold: holdout, geo-split or switchback (causal). Silver: before/after with an unaffected control (correlational). Bronze: visibility change only.
- **As graded / literal rule** — Silver as the case census assigned it / capped at Bronze when any of seven required items is missing.
- **R1 / R2** — two readings of a before/after with a control: R1 counts it experimental, R2 observational.
- **Tier** — provenance score on every source, 1 strongest to 7 weakest: 2 filed; 3 platform primary; 4 panel, clickstream, telemetry; 5 vendor or agency study with n, dates, method; 6 marketing, "Not a source".
- **Source kinds** — vendor-reported, analyst-derived, filed, company-stated, measured-by-us.
