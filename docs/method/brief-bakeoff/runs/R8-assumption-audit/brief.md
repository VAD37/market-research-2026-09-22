# Brand visibility inside AI assistants: what the evidence supports

Evidence as of 2026-09-23. Sources were pulled 2026-09-22 or 2026-09-23; an undated figure carries that pull date. Research only: evidence, not a verdict.

## The answer

Paid placement inside AI assistants exists and sells: ChatGPT ads reached "$1 billion in annualized revenue run rate" (company-stated, 2026-08-31). Buyer demand is visible but thin: 8 of 27 buyer segments show spend (strict reading, 2026-09-23; a loose reading and an earlier count also exist). No published case proves a sales effect: zero of ~980 screened cases used a controlled design (measured-by-us, 2026-09-22). No sub-market has a measured size, and no buyer discloses a price paid. The evidence supports "some large firms are spending"; it stops short of "it pays back".

## What this is

Brands want to be named when someone asks an AI assistant (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, Claude) what to buy. The market splits three ways:

- **Organic recommendation**: being mentioned or cited without paying the engine; vendors sell tools for it.
- **Paid placement**: ads in or beside AI answers, sold by engines.
- **Agentic commerce**: the assistant completes the purchase.

Evidence strength uses a tier scale from 1 (strongest) to 7 (weakest); tier 6 is marketing.

## Load-bearing assumptions

- **Buyers pay.** Of the 7 spend segments in the 2026-09-22 count, 5 are enterprise and six rest on job postings, not purchases. The same split for the later count of 8: unknown — not in repo.
- **It works.** Silver cases: 7 as originally graded, 1 under the literal grading rule; both stand. Two of the seven run negative or null. Every positive Silver is tier 5.
- **Platforms leave room.** See risk 4 below.

## Who is already there

- **Vendors**: 34 rostered (13 pure-play, 16 incumbent bundles, 5 agencies; analyst-derived). Price disclosed by 14 of 34, "$20/mo" to "$999/month" (vendor-reported). Filed revenue: 2 of 34, neither broken out to this product.
- **Incumbent revenue**: Semrush "AI products surpassed $38 million in ARR" (filed, 2026-03-02). Peec AI $10M ARR (vendor-reported, 2026-05; an earlier figure exists).
- **Deals**: Scrunch acquired by Sitecore, announced 2026-06-03; value undisclosed by Sitecore, $225M per Bloomberg, unconfirmed.
- **Engines**: 0 of 8 publish a rate card. Only stated price: OpenAI max-bid guidance "$3–$5 USD per click" (company-stated, tier 3).
- **Checkout** (company-stated): 4 live programs (ChatGPT, Google, Copilot, Perplexity); fee disclosed by 1 of 4. Protocol fee clauses: 6 of 6 unknown — checked 2026-09-22.

## What brands that moved changed

Of 108 cases (measured-by-us over self-reports): content operations 63, vendor tools 58, hires or teams 12, paid media 2. Both paid cases are Google's Direct Offers pilot (e.l.f., L'Oréal). 57 of 58 tool cases rest on vendor pages. A 2026-09-23 reading finds 67 of 108 with a moved metric; an earlier reading counts 99 naming a change.

## Where the evidence stops

- Price paid by any buyer: unknown in 27 of 27 segments.
- Measured-by-us sampling of assistant answers stopped 2026-09-22 22:40 at a partial first day: Claude 83 of 160 runs, Gemini 24 of 160, ChatGPT and Perplexity 0.
- Brand confirmation of vendor cases (measured-by-us): 0 of 59 brands checked corroborate; ~107 unchecked.
- Job-board and analyst-size channels: indeed.com and emarketer.com still blocked 2026-09-23.

## Readings you would have to accept

- Demand: strict and loose readings both give 8 spend segments; they differ on 17 vs 18 "none" and 1 vs 0 unchecked.
- Proof: 7 Silver or 1 Silver, depending on grading rule.

## Appendix

### Glossary

- **AI**: artificial intelligence. **AI Mode**, **AI Overviews**: Google's AI answer surfaces in Search.
- **ARR**: annual recurring revenue.
- **CTR**: click-through rate.
- **EU**, **UK**, **US**: European Union, United Kingdom, United States. **USD**: US dollars.
- **MAU**: monthly active users.
- **SMB**: small and medium-sized business.
- **Gold**: holdout, geo-split or switchback design; causal. **Silver**: before/after with baseline and unaffected control; correlational. **Bronze**: visibility or mention change only.
- **Strict / loose reading**: strict counts a segment "none" only if every signal ran; loose counts a blocked-and-logged channel as checked.
- **Tier**: provenance score 1–7 written on every source; lower is stronger.
