## Caveats
- ARR figures are filed (tier 2) and group- or product-line-level; nothing in `raw/` breaks out AI Visibility Toolkit revenue or its customer count. The vendor-reported usage claims "28M — Marketing professionals have already used Semrush" and "35% — Fortune 500 companies use Semrush" (`raw/a-semrush-ai-visibility-2026-09-22.md`) are platform-wide usage, not paying-customer counts.
- All three case studies are vendor-reported, undated on the page, and written by Semrush about its own customers; tier 6, no independent replication. Neither Sure Oak nor Activate Digital states a year for the months it names; Sure Oak's "40% of all new leads" is a referral attribution read, not incrementality; Activate Digital could not be checked brand-side ("`activate.digital` returned no DuckDuckGo index, domain unconfirmed", `raw/e-case-census-c7-2026-09-22.md`).
- The Adobe acquisition is confirmed by three independent-in-kind sources — SEC 8-K, SEC 10-K, and the Adobe release republished on Semrush's newsroom — not by the G2 label alone. The 10-K (filed 2026-03-02) describes the merger as pending; the close (2026-04-28) is documented only in the newsroom source.
- Oldest pull depended on: 2026-09-22. Category turnover is fast; this profile is stale one quarter after 2026-09-22.
