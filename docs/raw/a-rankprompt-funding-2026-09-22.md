# Rank Prompt Launches as Pioneering Platform to Optimize Brand Visibility in AI Search Engines

"The Tool is Designed to Track and Improve Metrics in Models Including ChatGPT and Gemini"

MIAMI, June 16, 2025 /PRNewswire/ -- Measuring and optimizing business visibility across AI's most powerful assistants just became possible with Rank Prompt. The revolutionary platform, developed by a team of veteran marketers, works with ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Grok to function as an AI business visibility scanner. For the first time ever, users are easily able to understand whether their business is being cited, summarized or recommended by major language learner models (LLMs).

Rank Prompt is a revolutionary, new platform created to help businesses and entrepreneurs understand if AI Assistants recommend their business.

The idea for Rank Prompt came about when founders and longtime colleagues in the digital marketing world, Nazareno Castro Bay, Ethan Kramer, Cole Feigl, and Trevor Anderson, all noticed something strange: their own agencies were getting inbound traffic from AI tools, but had no idea why. The result was their own platform where users can test prompts, view their competitors' rankings and receive structured, AI-specific optimization recommendations.

"From our own experience, we quickly learned that having visibility into AI-generated answers is rapidly becoming as important as traditional SEO," said Castro Bay. "People are asking AI questions and recommendations like they would of a friend, and Rank Prompt exists to help marketers and business owners dive deeper into that critical insight."

Rank Prompt offers plans that start at $29/month and includes access to real-time benchmarking tools, personalized to-do lists and trend-tracking dashboards. A partner program has also launched, offering a 30-percent lifetime revenue share for agencies that bring Rank Prompt into their service stack. To sign up and find out more, visit www.RankPrompt.com.

ABOUT RANK PROMPT

Rank Prompt is a revolutionary, new platform created to help businesses and entrepreneurs understand if AI Assistants recommend their business. Rank Prompt is compatible with the most popular models on the market today, including ChatGPT and Gemini.
