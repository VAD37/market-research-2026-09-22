Sponsored Agents in ChatGPT Ads

Learn what Sponsored Agents are and how availability and early access work in ChatGPT Ads.

Updated: 5 days ago

What are Sponsored Agents?

Sponsored Agents are an advertising format that lets people chat with an AI representative of a business in ChatGPT. People can open a conversation from an ad to ask questions about the business's products or services.

Are Sponsored Agents available to all advertisers?

No. Sponsored Agents are currently available only to selected advertisers in a limited alpha test.

You may see Sponsored Agents in ChatGPT even if the format isn't available for your campaigns.

How can I express interest in early access?

We're not accepting early access requests at this time. We'll announce more details about availability and how to participate when we're ready to expand access.
