## Verbatim

Headline: "How Top Beauty Brands Grow Visibility in AI"
Byline: Alice Manners. Published: August 2, 2026.

"[...] That evolution was the focus of BeautyMatter's latest webinar, GEO 2026: How Beauty Brands Get Chosen by AI, where BeautyMatter founder and CEO Kelly Kovack spoke with John Morabito, SVP of Search & Innovation at digital media and market agency Stella Rising [...]

According to Morabito, ChatGPT has grown from roughly 400 million weekly active users to more than 900 million in less than a year, while 2 billion people each month now interact with Google AI Overviews. [...]

Citing research from Boston Consulting Group and WWD, Morabita noted that 25% of beauty consumers now consider AI their primary source for product research, while 75% have used AI to research beauty, wellness, or longevity in the past month. Among male beauty shoppers, 40% already use AI to build personalized skincare routines.

The commercial impact is becoming increasingly clear. Adobe Analytics data cited during the webinar found AI referral traffic has increased 393% year over year, with visitors arriving from AI platforms converting at higher rates than many traditional traffic sources.

[...] According to Stella Rising's analysis, roughly 95% of AI citations come from third-party websites rather than brand-owned channels. [...]

According to Stella Rising's analysis, 94% of AI video citations originate from long-form YouTube content [...]

To support that shift, Stella Rising has developed ChatTrack and SearchTrack, tools designed to measure visibility across AI platforms, traditional search, retail sites, and social search."
