## Verbatim — Gemini Apps Help

"Send feedback or report a problem with Gemini Apps" (13275746):
> If you get a response that's inaccurate or that you feel is unsafe, you can give feedback on that response to let us know. … If you have a general issue with Gemini Apps, or want to tell us about your experience, you can report a problem. … Important: When you submit feedback, the associated conversation, like your prompts and the Gemini app's responses, is added as part of your feedback.

"Gemini Apps Privacy Hub" (13594961):
> Requesting content removal and exporting your information — You can request the removal of content under our policies or applicable laws.
> Other things to know — Gemini Apps are continuously evolving and may produce inaccurate or offensive information (including about people) that doesn't represent Google's views.
> How can I object to the processing of my data or ask for inaccurate data in Gemini Apps' responses to be corrected? LLM experiences (Gemini Apps included) can hallucinate and present inaccurate information as factual.

"Learn about responses from Gemini Apps" (16279220):
> Gemini Apps are continuously evolving and may sometimes produce inaccurate, offensive, or inappropriate information that doesn't represent Google's views. … Gemini Apps may provide inaccurate or inappropriate responses about people, so double-check its responses. … Your feedback is one of the ways Gemini Apps will learn, improve, and become more helpful and safe.

"Report Google AI policy violations that involve your likeness" (16719589):
