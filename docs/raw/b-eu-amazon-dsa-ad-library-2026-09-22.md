## Verbatim

"Updated: 31 August 2026 ... The Digital Services Act (DSA) represents an important milestone for creating a safer, more transparent digital environment. Amazon is committed to meeting these obligations, and we have maintained this approach for many years, long before the DSA came into effect."

"Contact points for regulators

Pursuant to Article 11 of the DSA, the Amazon EU Store's single point of contact email alias to enable direct communication with Member States' authorities, the European Commission, and the European Board for Digital Services for the application of the DSA is amazon-dsa-compliance@amazon.com."

"**Ad library**

Pursuant to Article 39 of the DSA, Amazon makes available an Ad Library of all ads and affiliate marketing content shown on the Amazon EU Store within the past year.
To learn more, visit here."
