Customer: Kiteworks · Industry: File Management & Collaboration Software · Region: United States

About Kiteworks — Kiteworks is a leading cybersecurity platform providing secure communication and data privacy solutions for over 100 million users across 3,650 global enterprises and government agencies. The platform ensures secure file transfers, optimized workflows, and actionable insights, empowering enterprises to confidently manage sensitive communications and maintain regulatory compliance.

The Team — The Kiteworks marketing team, led by Patrick Spencer, VP of Corporate Marketing, collaborated closely with Quattr to implement a new and scalable internal linking strategy. Robert Dougherty, Director of SEO Content, and his team used Quattr's Autonomous Internal Linking technology to automate the site's linking structure and improve overall content discoverability.

THE CHALLENGE — The Complexity of Modern Internal Linking at Scale

Internal linking has always been part of the website optimization toolkit, but as Google search evolves with new surfaces and LLM platforms emerge, it becomes increasingly critical for discoverability. For an enterprise leader like Kiteworks, treating internal linking as a simple maintenance task was no longer an option. The team recognized the need to transform it into a strategic growth engine but faced significant hurdles in two key areas: technical barriers and measurement challenges.

Technical Barriers: 1. Generating relevant internal links at scale beyond basic keyword matching · 2. Creating dynamic anchor text using actual prospect search queries · 3. Building a living link graph free from constraints of fixed URL lists and static anchors · 4. Scaling across multiple languages and markets

Measurement Challenges: 1. Isolating internal linking impact in a zero-click ecosystem · 2. Proving value during market-wide traffic declines and algorithm changes · 3. Monitoring visible change with statistical validation beyond correlation

THE SOLUTION — Quattr's Demand-Weighted Living Link Graph

Kiteworks, a long-time Quattr customer with a robust data lake of first-party search data, partnered with Quattr to execute a completely new internal linking strategy. The goal was to move beyond static, logic-based links (like 'Most Recent Posts') and deploy an intelligent, self-optimizing link graph across their entire site. This strategic shift was designed to transform internal linking from a simple maintenance task into a primary engine for content discovery and user journey optimization.

Powering Relevance with AI and Real-time User Data — The core of the solution was Quattr's Autonomous Linking API, which understands content on a deeper, more human level. Instead of relying on basic keyword matching, the platform uses two key data sources. 1. Vector Embeddings: The vector database intelligence analyzes page and query embeddings to understand the true semantic context of the content, going far beyond simple keyword matching. This ensures every internal link we create is perfectly relevant. 2. Real-time Demand Signals From GSC: The system then connects to your live Google Search Console data. It uses the exact phrases and questions your prospects are searching for on SERP to create powerful, high-intent anchor text for your links.

Automated Refreshes for a Future-Ready Strategy — To ensure the link graph was truly 'living' and not just a one-time fix, the system was designed to be future-ready. As new content goes live, Quattr automatically updates Kiteworks' entire content library based on a scheduled refresh (daily, weekly, or monthly). This ensures new pages get relevant links from existing ones, and older content is updated to point to the new pages, keeping the entire content ecosystem perpetually optimized without manual effort.

Where Do These Modules Go? — Quattr provides a direct API and an SDK for WordPress and HubSpot. Kiteworks leveraged the WordPress SDK integration, and a Quattr engineer was forward-deployed to assist in implementation. The result was dozens of modules connecting various parts of the site across funnel stages. Many of these modules replaced the existing logic for showing related links. Instead of a static list of blogs or only the most recent, these modules now show the most relevant piece of content to move the bot or user deeper into the site or funnel.

Right Rail Featured & Popular Posts — One module was designed to serve relevant blog content within the same subcategory that the user or bot is reading. The other module below was a wider mapping of blogs outside the current blog’s category. Quattr has configuration settings to prevent duplicate URLs from being served, detect if links are already present, and omit them from serving within the API.

This type of content was also meant to guide users to solution pages or later-stage resource material to continue the user on their journey.

The impact of Quattr’s Autonomous Linking API was both immediate and substantial. By replacing over 53,000 static or legacy logic links, Kiteworks achieved statistically validated growth across every key SEO metric, proving the strategy's power even during a period of industry-wide volatility.

A. Breakthroughs in Rankings and Indexation — Within just eight weeks of deployment, Kiteworks reversed a decline in site indexation, achieving a 30% increase in indexed pages.
