Title: "How we grew organic leads by 250% for deeploi, an IT management platform for growing companies"

"When Radyant started working with deeploi in November 2025, their organic presence was still in its early stages: solid fundamentals, but AI visibility at zero and organic lead volume still modest. Eight months later, AI search is a measurable lead channel. Organic leads have grown by more than 3x. A content program built around how buyers actually search, combined with a systematic review platform presence. Several levers working together, not one tactic in isolation."

Client quote: "We set clear goals from day one: more leads, better attribution, and a presence in AI search. Nine months in, organic leads have more than tripled and AI search has become a real acquisition channel for us. The team at Radyant knows what they're doing." — Julian Lübke, Co-Founder & CEO, deeploi

Headline stat callouts: "+250% organic web search leads / 0% → ~20% AI visibility / 24% share of voice in AI search / #2 in the AI search competitor set"

### Executive summary

"- Organic web search leads grew 3× in eight months
- Zero AI visibility at kickoff; #2 in the competitor set by March
- 65+ content pieces built for every stage of the buyer journey
- OMR Reviews presence expanded across every relevant category, not just one profile
- AI Search became a new, measurable lead channel"

### deeploi is the IT management platform for growing companies with limited internal IT resources

"deeploi is an IT management platform built for growing companies. It helps HR teams, founders, and IT managers handle onboarding, offboarding, device management, software management, and account management in one place, designed for companies where internal IT resources are limited. More than 200 companies trust deeploi, from fast-growing startups like Instaffo and HOLY Energy to established mid-sized businesses across the DACH region."

"Radyant started working with deeploi in November 2025 to build a presence in the layer where IT buyers now do most of their research: AI Search, alongside Google."

### 65+ content pieces, built for every stage of the buyer journey

"Buyers research IT management software the same way they research anything else: how-to guides while they're still defining the problem, comparisons once they're evaluating options, alternatives articles right before a decision. The content program covered all three stages, built around real buyer intent rather than keyword volume. Examples from the live roadmap: 'IT-Kosten senken: 11 innovative Lösungen', 'Die 5 besten Atera Alternativen 2026 im Vergleich'."

### Programmatic scale from March 2026: more output, same quality bar

"From March 2026, we added a programmatic content cluster: entire pages built in days instead of weeks, seeded from real product knowledge, without dropping depth. More queries covered means more surfaces owned in AI Search. Example from the cluster: an onboarding checklist built at this pace."

### OMR Reviews: listed in every relevant category, not just the profile

"deeploi already had an OMR Reviews profile. Our contribution: identifying which categories it was missing (IT Service Management, IT Asset Management, Offboarding, Service Desk). That put the profile into the category listicles and comparison pages LLMs actually cite, not just the standalone vendor page. deeploi took it from there with active review acquisition. Result by Q3/26: 3× Leader badges, 1× Top Rated."

### In direct comparison prompts: 32 deeploi citations vs. 4 for Atera

"We tracked comparison prompts, ones that name two tools and ask the model to pick, pitting deeploi against Atera, a close competitor far less present on OMR Reviews at the time. deeploi's review and alternatives pages were retrieved 32 times combined, Atera's 4. Review-platform presence and category coverage, not just the standalone profile, are what AI engines reach for in a head-to-head comparison."

"This finding matches our Software Prompt Study: comparison, alternatives, and category pages, on and off review platforms, are what LLMs reach for most when answering commercial software queries."

### From 0% to #2 in the AI Search competitor set

"At kickoff, deeploi had zero AI visibility across all tracked models. By March 2026, four months in, visibility had climbed to 20.3% with real buyer queries. deeploi has held #2 in the competitor set since. Today, visibility sits at ~20%, with a peak of 24.3%."

### The result that matters: 3x organic leads in 8 months

"Organic leads are the metric that matters, not rankings, traffic, or AI visibility scores. From day one, Radyant and deeploi set shared lead targets and calibrated every initiative against them. Since November 2025, organic web search leads have grown by more than 250%, visible from February 2026 and peaking in March. Content volume, conversion optimization, and programmatic scale all contributed. No single factor can be isolated. Monthly counts are small and move around (a single month is noise, not signal); the sustained step up from the Nov-Jan baseline is what held."
