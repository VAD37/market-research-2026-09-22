> A 2-person growth team up against 95% market share

And international players like Brex and Ramp are gearing up to enter Canada. Meanwhile, Venn's growth team is made up of two people.

> Behind that workflow, AirOps acted as Venn's growth partner, bringing the platform, the data, and a hands-on team that stayed involved from strategy through execution.

"LLM-sourced traffic has better time-to-conversions and sessions-to-conversions than organic traffic from Google," Alex notes. Users arriving through AI search tools already knew what they wanted and moved through the funnel faster.
