Title: "How B2B SaaS Heyflow 3×'d MRR pipeline in 6 months"

"Heyflow's MRR pipeline from AI leads grew 3x in 6 months, and AI Search visibility climbed from 7% to 52%. One playbook ran across on-site content and off-site presence, both aimed at pipeline growth."

Referrals, people who actually recommend Heyflow to others. Huge shoutout to Niklas Buschner and the team at Radyant who helped us build the foundation for this." — Amir Bohnenkamp, CEO & Co-Founder, Heyflow

### Executive summary

"- MRR pipeline tripled in 6 months, from AI Search leads alone
- AI Search visibility climbed from 7% to 52%
- AI citation rate more than doubled to 3.8
- Initiatives spanned on-site content and off-site presence
- Editorial content built from real Heyflow product walkthroughs
- Shipped 5 high-intent programmatic clusters in days, at quality
- Optimized Heyflow's YouTube videos against real tracked search prompts"

Chart, "mrr pipeline from ai leads, relative to nov 2025" (source note: "Source: Radyant / Heyflow internal reporting"):
Nov 2025: 1×; Dec 2025: 0.7×; Jan 2026: 1.5×; Feb 2026: 2.4×; Mar 2026: 3.3×; Apr 2026: 3.2×

### Heyflow is the no-code funnel and form builder behind fast-growing demand engines

"Heyflow lets product, marketing, and growth teams build no-code funnels and forms that convert paid traffic into qualified leads. The product is used across B2B SaaS, e-commerce, energy, financial services, and recruiting by teams running serious paid acquisition."

"The buyer journey is long and research-heavy: prospects compare tools across dozens of touchpoints, ask ChatGPT which funnel builder fits their ad platform, and read Help Center docs before they ever start a trial. AI Search is the layer where most of that research now lands, and it was also the layer where Heyflow was showing up far too rarely."

### Several strategic initiatives, working together

"Our partnership started in December 2025. We started with roughly 7% AI Search visibility, and trial volume growing, but not fast enough. Organic simply hadn't been built as a lead-gen channel yet. From day one, we set shared goals with Amir's team: trial volume, ICP quality, and MRR pipeline."

"From there, we built a strategic playbook of initiatives designed to work together. Product-led content built the foundation, programmatic clusters gave it scale, and Help Center optimization did double duty as a citation source in its own right and a knowledge base other content could draw from. Offsite presence extended reach to the sources AI engines trust most."

Initiatives list (as on page): "01 Product-led editorial content / 02 Programmatic clusters / 03 YouTube video optimization / 04 Help Center optimization / 05 Reddit presence / 06 Review platforms & LinkedIn"

### Product-led editorial content that pre-sells the product

"People searching 'quiz funnel examples' or 'how to build a lead funnel for recruiting' are picturing a solution they can use, not just inspiration. Articles that show working examples built in Heyflow arrive pre-qualified."

"We built editorial pieces this way: real examples, real use cases, never a generic listicle. The content piece about quiz funnel examples became one of the top traffic pages on the site, and the distance from reader to trial collapsed."

### Programmatic SEO: 5 clusters across high-intent topics

"We built and published entire clusters in days, at quality. Speed was a strategic choice, grounded in the shared goal of moving pipeline numbers fast."

"By now we've launched 5 programmatic clusters across high-intent topics: tracking and analytics, integrations like connecting a funnel to Monday.com, form types for specific target groups, and competitor alternative pages. Each cluster page draws on Help Center documentation, product specs, and input from the Heyflow team, so it answers a real job to be done instead of padding a template."

### Help Center, rewritten to be a citation engine

"The Help Center had real depth, product accuracy, step-by-step instructions, but it wasn't structured to be found or cited. We ran a systematic pass: title rewrites, heading structure, use-case sections, feature-name precision."

"Help Center articles started showing up as cited sources in AI answers, and began ranking in Google organic results too. Try it: type 'how to connect Heyflow' into Google."

### Offsite initiatives that 2×'d the AI citation rate

"AI citation rate more than doubled, and total citations climbed by 13,555, over six months. Our approach was to identify the off-site sources with the biggest leverage, then find different ways to enter the conversation there: YouTube, Reddit, LinkedIn, and review platforms."

"On Reddit, we found the exact threads where buyers compared funnel builders, then had Amir engage under his own account, a founder's voice reads differently than a brand's. The same logic shaped his LinkedIn Pulse posts and a stronger presence on review platforms."

### 32 Heyflow videos regularly cited, and showing up natively inside Google AI Overviews

"We cross-referenced Heyflow's existing YouTube library (under 1,000 subscribers, zero AI citations) against the tracked prompt set, then rewrote titles and descriptions. From February, when the optimizations went live, through May, 32 videos were regularly cited, the top one retrieved 110 times in a single month, and several started showing up natively embedded inside Google AI Overviews, thumbnail and all."
