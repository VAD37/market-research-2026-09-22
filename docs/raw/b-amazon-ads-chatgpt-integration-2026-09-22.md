## Verbatim

Headline: "Amazon Ads announces new integration to support advertising in ChatGPT" — dated September 10, 2026.

> "Amazon Ads has announced a partnership with OpenAI to support advertising in ChatGPT. The new partnership enables advertisers to expand their Amazon Ads campaigns with a conversational ad experience on ChatGPT. Select advertisers in the U.S. are currently testing ChatGPT Ads as part of a pilot.
>
> 'Conversational ads represent the fastest growing engagement opportunity for brands to reach new and existing audiences,' said Chris Conetta, Director of Omnichannel Supply at Amazon DSP. 'The AI-driven ad experience is not just a vision of what's to come, but an opportunity for advertisers to meet relevant users where they spend time today. With access to ChatGPT Ads, advertisers can now extend their campaigns further into where their customers are actively spending time in conversational chat platforms.'
>
> For marketers already using Amazon Ads to plan, build, and optimize campaigns that span the full funnel, the integration creates an opportunity to reach active ChatGPT users. Through the new integration, Amazon Ads will enable advertisers to engage with customers as they explore options, compare choices, and make decisions on ChatGPT.
>
> 'Travel planning is becoming increasingly personalized, and travelers expect experiences that feel relevant,' says Katrin Koenig, President of Delta Vacations. 'Through our collaboration with Amazon Ads and ChatGPT Ads, we can leverage deep consumer insights to inform how and when Delta Vacations appear within ChatGPT Ads experiences to create new opportunities for travelers to engage and discover vacation possibilities. We're proud to be among the first brands testing this new approach and helping define what the future of AI-powered marketing can look like.'
>
> Amazon Ads offers programmatic technology solutions that enable advertisers through advanced AI capabilities to plan, activate, optimize, and measure multi-channel media investments across Amazon's owned and operated properties and the open internet. Amazon Ads provides brands with trillions of proprietary browsing, shopping, and streaming signals paired with deep supply-side relationships to deliver campaign precision, improved performance, and efficient outcomes at scale. Amazon Ads offers digital marketing solutions that deliver reach, relevancy, and results across all customer touchpoints without relying on ad identifiers."

No pricing, fee, revenue-share, or commission figure stated anywhere on this page. No advertiser count or campaign volume stated. Delta Vacations is named as the sole confirmed advertiser at pilot launch.
