# What's New in Rank Prompt

Release notes in plain text. Newest updates first.

## v8 — September 2026 — A Lead Widget, Google Keywords and Your Own Domain

Agency Plus can now capture leads where they already are: a form on your own site hands a visitor a free AI visibility report signed by you, and hands you the lead with everything the report found. Google keywords sit next to AI visibility, ChatGPT Ads gains custom audiences, and the whole app runs on your agency domain.

Spotlight — A lead widget for your own website

Paste two lines and a visitor can ask for a free AI visibility report. It runs on your credits, arrives from your sending address, opens on your domain, and the visitor can turn it into an account with one click.

* Every lead, with its report (AGENCY PLUS) — A Leads tab inside Prospecting: the visibility score, the ranked prompts, what the lead cost you read from your credit ledger, and whether they opened an account. Filter, sort, export to CSV, and open one to read what the agent collected.
* Lead events to your backend (AGENCY PLUS) — Signed callbacks when a lead arrives, when its report lands and when it opens an account, with retries, replay, and an endpoint that turns itself off after repeated failures.
* The app on your own domain (AGENCY PLUS) — Serve the whole product on your subdomain with your logo, colors and sender email, give clients read-only seats, and open plans, subscription and credit usage there as the account owner.
* Google keywords, next to your AI visibility (ALL PLANS) — Track the searches you want to win with volume, difficulty and intent, check where your site ranks on demand, and see the Google keyword and AI difficulty behind every prompt in a report.
* Competition and keyword gap — A page for the competitors your reports keep naming: organic keywords, top 10 rankings, estimated traffic, and the keywords they rank for that your site does not.
* ChatGPT Ads audiences and conversions — Custom audiences built from your own lists, bid multipliers, conversions attributed by campaign, and pixel advanced matching.
* Citations by domain, and who owns them — Citations group by domain with their reach, owned citations separate from plain mentions, competitor intelligence on the sources that keep coming back, and tasks and schedules projected onto the calendar.

## v7 — September 2026 — Developer Platform, MCP and Pro-Grade Exports

Rank Prompt opens up: a public REST API, an MCP server that plugs your AI visibility data into Claude Code, Cursor and Claude Desktop, and outbound webhooks. Plus PowerPoint exports, smarter report-driven content, and author-level outreach.

Spotlight — MCP server and the public API

Connect Rank Prompt to any MCP client (Claude Code, Cursor, Claude Desktop) over OAuth, or call the /v1 REST API directly. Mint scoped, brand-restricted keys, get idempotent writes, quota headers, and an OpenAPI 3.1 spec.

* Public REST API (/v1) NEW — Programmatically manage brands and run AI visibility reports across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Scoped keys, idempotent writes, structured errors.
* MCP Server NEW — Bring your AI visibility data into the tools you already work in. Connect Claude Code, Cursor, or any MCP client over OAuth and let your assistant query reports, citations, and brands.
* Outbound Webhooks NEW — Subscribe to events like report.completed, report.failed, schedule.run.completed, and schedule.run.failed. Signed payloads, secret rotation, auto-disable on repeated failures, and per-brand endpoints.
* PowerPoint and Smarter Exports (ALL PLANS) — Export branded decks to PowerPoint (.pptx), reorder sections by drag, save reusable templates, password-protect shares, see share view counts on Agency Plus, add a deck table of contents, and auto-refresh on view.
* Report-Driven Content and AEO Score — Content opportunities now come straight from missed prompts and competitor gaps, grounded by a brand knowledge base, with real images from Google Business Profile and search, schedule-to-publish, an AEO score panel, and new Statistics Roundup and Glossary types.
* Author-Level Outreach — Target the authors and journalists who write the pages AI cites, bulk-import citations from your reports, and track engagement per campaign.

## v6 — June 2026 — Agency Suite, Agency Plus & the New Agent

The biggest release yet: a full agency operating layer (dashboard, client reporting, portal, approvals, prospecting) behind the new Agency Plus plan, plus a rebuilt AI agent with 50+ tools, memory, and BYOK.

Spotlight — Agency Dashboard & the Agency Plus plan

Run every client from one place: cross-brand visibility scores, portfolio trend, a brand-by-platform heatmap, top movers, needs-attention alerts, competitor leaderboard, and usage per brand. 12 widgets, drag-and-resize layout, up to 1,000 brands.

* Automated Client Reporting (AGENCY PLUS) — Scheduled reports now deliver straight to client emails under your branding, with sent, opened, and failed tracking rolled into the dashboard.
* White-Label Client Portal (AGENCY PLUS) — A magic-link portal per client brand: shared reports and pending approvals in one branded place, no client accounts.
* Content Approvals (AGENCY PLUS) — Share drafts for client review with comments anchored to exact text, threads, versions on reshare, and a cross-brand reviews inbox.
* Prospecting (AGENCY PLUS) — AI company search by sector and location, prospect lists with cadences, funnel tracking, and branded pitch reports for winning new clients.
* Agent Mode: Dock, Memory & BYOK NEW — The agent moved into a persistent dock with 50+ tools, live article canvas editing, lasting memories, confirmation flows with an audit trail, and bring-your-own-OpenAI-key support.
* Tasks Board & Cross-Brand Views — Kanban board for tasks plus agency-wide content, shares, emails, and calendar views. Brand tags and client fields keep big portfolios organized.
