> WILMINGTON, Del., July 22, 2025 — Onfolio Holdings Inc. (NASDAQ: ONFO, ONFOW) announced that its subsidiary Pace Generative LLC partnered with ThriveCart to deliver a webinar on Generative Engine Optimization (GEO).
