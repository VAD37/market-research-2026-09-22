## Verbatim

### FY25 Q4 — July 30, 2025
- "Our family of Copilot apps has surpassed 100 million monthly active users across commercial and consumer."
- "we have over 800 million monthly active users" [engaging with AI capabilities across products]
- "Our Copilot consumer app also continues to see strong growth in engagement and successful sessions."
- "Search and news advertising revenue ex-TAC increased 21% and 20% in constant currency driven by continued growth in both volume and revenue per search."

### FY26 Q1 — October 29, 2025
- "We now have 900 million monthly active users of our AI features across our products."
- "our first party family of Copilots now has surpassed 150 million monthly active users across information work, coding, security, science, health, and consumer."
- "daily users of our Copilot consumer app increased nearly 50% quarter over quarter."
- "Search and news advertising revenue ex-TAC increased 16% and 15% in constant currency driven by growth in volume as well as a continued benefit from third-party partnerships."

### FY26 Q2 — January 28, 2026
- "Daily users of our Copilot app increased nearly 3X year-over-year."
- "Microsoft 365 Copilot also is becoming a true daily habit, with daily active users increasing 10X year-over-year."
- "15 million paid Microsoft 365 Copilot seats."
- "Search and news advertising revenue ex-TAC increased 10% and 9% in constant currency, slightly below expectations driven by some execution challenges."

### FY26 Q3 — April 29, 2026
- "Bing monthly active users reached 1 billion for the first time"
- "Copilot queries per user were up nearly 20% quarter over quarter"; "monthly active usage up 6X year to date"
- "over 20 million Microsoft 365 Copilot paid seats"
- "Search advertising revenue ex-TAC increased 12% and 9% in constant currency"

### FY26 Q4 — July 29, 2026
- "over 30 million paid Microsoft 365 Copilot seats, with net seat adds more than doubling quarter-over-quarter"
- "GitHub Copilot now has 50 million users"
- "Search advertising revenue ex-TAC increased 10% and 9% in constant currency with growth driven by higher revenue per search across Edge and Bing"
