## (1) Mechanism — how it works, per source

| Source | Raw path | Mechanism as the source describes it |
|---|---|---|
| C-SEO Bench (Puerto et al.) | `d-citationpref-cseo-bench-2026-09-22.md` | Re-tests the foundational GEO paper's own nine content-rewrite methods (Authoritative, Statistics, Citations, Fluency, Unique Words, Keyword Stuffing, etc.) under a harder multi-domain, multi-actor protocol with a statistical-significance test |
| AutoGEO (Wu et al.) | `d-citationpref-autogeo-2026-09-22.md` | Learns "generative engine preferences" automatically by having frontier LLMs explain why one document ranks above another, extracts these into reusable "preference rules," then uses the rules either as prompt context or as RL reward signals to rewrite content |
| FeatGEO (Liu and Xu) | `d-citationpref-featgeo-2026-09-22.md` | Abstracts a webpage into an interpretable feature space (structural, content, linguistic properties — e.g. fluency_level, keyword_focus_level, easy_to_understand_level) and optimizes over that feature space rather than editing text tokens directly |
| AgentGEO (Tian et al.) | `d-citationpref-agentgeo-2026-09-22.md` | Diagnoses WHY a specific document fails to be cited (via a taxonomy of failure modes spanning parsing, fetching, and generation stages) and applies a targeted repair from a tool library, instead of a uniform rewrite recipe |
| SAGEO Arena (Kim et al.) | `d-citationpref-sageo-arena-2026-09-22.md` | Extends optimization across the full pipeline (retrieval, reranking, generation) rather than generation alone, and includes structural signals (schema markup, meta descriptions, headings) alongside body text as inputs a real search system uses |
| CC-GSEO-Bench (Chen et al.) | `d-citationpref-cc-gseo-bench-2026-09-22.md` | Scores rewritten content on five dimensions — Exposure, Faithful Credit, Causal Impact, Readability & Structure, Trustworthiness & Safety — explicitly naming "Authority and Credibility Building," "Textual Fluency and Engagement," and "SEO Techniques" as its baseline rewrite-method categories |
| E-GEO (Bagga et al.) | `d-citationpref-e-geo-2026-09-22.md` | Tests fifteen named hand-crafted rewrite styles (authoritative, FAQ, technical, fluent, format, etc.) against a simple-prompt baseline, then replaces hand-crafted heuristics with a learned, "universally effective" prompt pattern via meta-optimization |
| Rewrite-to-Rank (Ho et al.) | `d-citationpref-rewrite-to-rank-2026-09-22.md` | Trains a rewriter model (SFT then PPO) to phrase an item's own text to be more retrievable and more included in a generated response, without modifying the retriever — mechanism is a learned rewriting policy rather than a fixed heuristic |
| Venkit et al. (Answer Engine audit) | `d-citationpref-venkit-answer-engines-2026-09-22.md` | Not a rewrite mechanism — measures the inverse: whether citation (the outcome citation-preference content aims at) correlates with factual accuracy and verifiability; finds it frequently does not |
| What Gets Cited (Vishwakarma et al., Sprinklr) | `d-seeding-what-gets-cited-2026-09-22.md` (P5-c1, cited not re-pulled) | Isolates which content factors make a source win the first citation in a two-source competition: "topical relevance and list position are the biggest drivers"; price and recent timestamp help; formatting alone does not |
| Foundational GEO paper (Aggarwal et al.) | `d-seeding-geo-foundational-2026-09-22.md` (P5-c1, cited not re-pulled) | Originates the nine-method taxonomy (Cite Sources, Quotation Addition, Statistics Addition, Fluency, Authoritative, Unique Words, Technical Terms, Easy-to-Understand, Keyword Stuffing) that every paper in this cluster re-tests or extends |
| GEO-Bench (Nimase et al.) | `d-seeding-geo-bench-2026-09-22.md` (P5-c1, cited not re-pulled; also this cluster's second shortlist seed) | Benchmarks the foundational nine white-hat methods against gradient- and prompt-based adversarial attacks under one protocol; "Authoritative" rewriting scores competitively with attack methods on some metrics |
| Searchable (GEO content vendor) | `a-searchable-method-2026-09-22.md` (Pass 3, cited not re-pulled) | "Searchable's content engine understands what makes AI engines cite sources—structured data, authoritative tone, comprehensive coverage—and applies these principles to every piece of content it creates" |
| AirOps (AEO content platform vendor) | `a-airops-method-2026-09-22.md` (Pass 3, cited not re-pulled) | Scores pages on five named dimensions — Overall, Authority & Evidence, Freshness, Structure, Snippet Extractability (plus Brand Alignment on a leaderboard view) — with a published rubric text naming "credible citation[s]" as a scoring criterion |
| Beacon (Change Agents Corp) | `a-changeagents-method-2026-09-22.md` (Pass 3, cited not re-pulled) | "Beacon's agents make the changes for you, from website copy to schema to your business profile" — automated implementation of "Schema and FAQ structure — the technical markup and Q&A that tell AI assistants exactly what you do and where" |
| Ahrefs Brand Radar | `a-ahrefs-method-2026-09-22.md` (Pass 3, cited not re-pulled) | Incumbent SEO tool's AI-visibility add-on; FAQ-format product page, mechanism details on content-rewrite features not fully captured in the cited pull (page truncated mid-fetch) |
| Locafy | `a-locafy-method-2026-09-22.md` (Pass 3, cited not re-pulled) | Contrast case — explicitly NOT a content-rewrite mechanism: "Locafy's stated method is entity/listing-signal optimization" (Google Business Profile, citation syndication across 60+ directories, reviews, PR), not prompt-set monitoring or content rewriting |
