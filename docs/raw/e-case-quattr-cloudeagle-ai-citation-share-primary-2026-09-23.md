## Verbatim

CloudEagle Increases AI Citation Share by 3x and Drives 113% Organic Click Growth with Quattr

Highlights:
- CloudEagle scaled organic acquisition without creating execution bottlenecks
- Optimized 33 product & commercial blog pages using Quattr’s AI SEO Agent
- Achieved 113% growth in organic clicks & 3× increase in AI Citation Share in 12 weeks
- 77% of post-intervention traffic came from bottom-funnel, high-intent buyers
- Captured 328 net-new Page 1 queries that previously drove zero clicks (US Non-brand)

[image: docs/raw/img/e-case-quattr-cloudeagle-ai-citation-share-primary-2026-09-23/01-cloudeagle-113pct-clicks-12-weeks.png] — alt "113% Increase in Clicks Within 12 Weeks of Optimization Using Quattr" (hero position; recurs under The Result)

Customer: CloudEagle · Industry: B2B SaaS (Spend Management) · Region: United States

About CloudEagle — CloudEagle is an AI-powered SaaS Security, Management, and Identity Governance platform that gives enterprises a unified command center to discover, secure, govern, and optimize their entire SaaS and AI ecosystem. By correlating data across IT, Security, HR, and Finance, CloudEagle provides complete visibility into application usage, user access, and software spend while helping organizations eliminate shadow SaaS and unauthorized AI usage. Organic acquisition plays a critical role in CloudEagle’s growth, especially for reaching buyers during high-intent evaluation stages such as pricing comparisons, alternatives research, and vendor shortlisting.

The Team — CloudEagle’s SEO lead partnered with Quattr to solve a problem common across B2B content teams: SEO expertise had become the bottleneck. While the team had strong writers and valuable existing content, every optimization decision required hands-on involvement from the SEO lead. This slowed execution, limited experimentation, and pulled focus away from higher-leverage initiatives like paid acquisition alignment and landing page optimization. CloudEagle needed a way to scale execution without scaling dependency.

THE CHALLENGE — Scaling B2B Acquisition as Search Rules Change

CloudEagle needed to grow qualified, non-brand organic traffic, but the rules of discovery were shifting. Buyers were no longer researching exclusively through traditional Google SERPs. AI-mediated discovery via Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and other LLMs was increasingly shaping how prospects evaluated software. To continue growing, CloudEagle needed to: 1. Optimize existing high-authority pages instead of relying on net-new content production. 2. Remove the SEO expertise bottleneck slowing execution across the content team. 3. Build semantic internal connections at scale so search engines and LLMs could interpret CloudEagle’s content as an authoritative knowledge base.

The Content Optimization Paradox — The pages with the most compounding potential, already indexed, already ranking, already earning signals, are also the hardest to optimize. Refreshing content requires competitive analysis, intent alignment, and internal linking decisions. Without embedded guidance, writers can’t execute these steps independently. As a result, most teams default to publishing new content while their highest-signal pages stagnate.

The AI Search Visibility Gap — Beyond Google, CloudEagle’s content needed to perform across AI discovery surfaces. Without strong semantic connections between pages, LLMs treat content as disconnected assets rather than a unified source of expertise, limiting citations and visibility in AI-native buyer journeys.

THE SOLUTION — Turning Optimization into a Scalable System with GIGA

CloudEagle deployed Quattr’s GIGA AI SEO Agent to transform optimization from a manual process where writers relied on guidance from the SEO Lead at each step instead of the writers subject matter expertise. They needed a repeatable execution system to maximize throughput. The GIGA guided workflows took the writers through a step-by-step process after onboarding with Quattr and their SEO Lead. It was clear there was a vast keyword universe hidden that Quattr unlocked.

Guided Workflows for Autonomous Execution — GIGA provided a structured, step-by-step workflow that removed guesswork for writers: 1. ICP Intent Targeting: Writers identified keywords with the highest relevance and buying intent for CloudEagle’s ICP · 2. Multi-Model Competitor Research: Teams analyzed competitors across Google, ChatGPT, and other LLMs using the same buyer queries prospects use · 3. Semantic Content Analysis: GIGA surfaced exactly where pages lacked relevance, highlighting topic gaps and optimization opportunities writers could confidently address

[image: docs/raw/img/e-case-quattr-cloudeagle-ai-citation-share-primary-2026-09-23/04-optimization-predictive-scoring-dashboard.png] — alt "Optimization done in Quattr with predictive scoring" (product dashboard screenshot)

Instead of one-off training sessions, CloudEagle embedded SEO expertise directly into the workflow, allowing writers to execute independently. More importantly, GIGA also automatically identified new internal linking opportunities directly within the natural text of the content.

Autonomous Internal Linking at Scale — To solve the discoverability problem, CloudEagle deployed Quattr’s autonomous internal linking API. The system programmatically connected 33 priority pages into a semantic, demand-weighted link graph based on real user intent. Instead of generic anchors, links were built using actual Google Search Console queries, the same language buyers use when searching.

[note: product-UI screenshot "CloudEagle blog displaying Quattr’s autonomous internal linking modules in the right sidebar and at the end of the blog" — not a data image, not saved]

This created a compounding “halo effect,” where improvements on one page reinforced performance across the entire network.

Unlocking Strategic Focus for the SEO Lead — With execution running autonomously, CloudEagle’s SEO lead was able to step out of day-to-day optimization work and refocus on higher-impact initiatives. Time previously spent reviewing content updates was redirected toward aligning SEO with paid acquisition efforts, improving landing page conversion performance, and exploring new category opportunities and competitive gaps. SEO shifted from a manual bottleneck to a strategic growth function.

THE RESULT — 3× Increase in AI Citation Share and 113% Sustained Click Growth

The impact appeared quickly and compounded through the quarter, validating that execution-led optimization, rather than monitoring alone, drives measurable growth across both traditional search and AI-native discovery. Within weeks of deploying GIGA, CloudEagle’s optimized pages began surfacing more consistently for high-intent buyer queries. Over the full pilot window, these early gains sustained and expanded.

Sustained Click Growth — Across the 12 complete weeks following intervention, organic clicks across the pilot cohort increased from 2.47K to 5.25K, representing a +113% sustained lift.

[image: docs/raw/img/e-case-quattr-cloudeagle-ai-citation-share-primary-2026-09-23/01-cloudeagle-113pct-clicks-12-weeks.png] — alt "113% Increase in Clicks Within 12 Weeks of Optimization Using Quattr"

Importantly, this growth was not driven by publishing more content. It came from optimizing and structurally connecting existing high-authority pages, allowing them to perform better for the queries that matter most to CloudEagle’s ICP.

3× Surge in AI Citation Share — Beyond traditional search, CloudEagle achieved a significant expansion in AI-native visibility. Their AI Citation Share increased by 3× post optimizations.

[image: docs/raw/img/e-case-quattr-cloudeagle-ai-citation-share-primary-2026-09-23/02-cloudeagle-3x-ai-citation-share-12-weeks.png] — alt "AI Citation Share increased to 3x within 12 weeks of optimizations using Quattr"

This shift indicates a meaningful change in how AI systems surfaced CloudEagle during buyer research journeys. The brand moved from occasional mentions to a reliably cited source for pricing, comparisons, and vendor evaluation.

[image: docs/raw/img/e-case-quattr-cloudeagle-ai-citation-share-primary-2026-09-23/03-cloudeagle-ai-overview-citation-screenshot.png] — alt "CloudEagle page was cited in AI Overview post optimization with Quattr"

Crucially, these insights were captured from real consumer-facing AI responses, not simulated prompts or API outputs, aligning measurement with how actual buyers experience AI search.

Key Outcomes — 1. Unlocked 328 net-new Page 1 queries that previously drove zero clicks. 2. Captured bottom-funnel demand at scale, with 77% of post-intervention clicks coming from high-intent, consideration-stage searches. 3. Strengthened commercial performance, as pricing, comparison, and buyer-stage content consistently outperformed baseline traffic levels.

Together, these results validated that execution-led optimization, guided by real buyer intent and reinforced by demand-weighted internal linking, can drive measurable growth in both traditional and AI-first [note: extract ends here at the 7,000-character window used; the closing sentence and the Joel Platini (Content Manager) testimonial were not transcribed]
