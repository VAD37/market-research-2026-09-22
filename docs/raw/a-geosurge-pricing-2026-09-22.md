## frequently asked questions

geoSurge helps companies understand and improve how they are represented inside AI systems like ChatGPT, Gemini and other large language models.

AI visibility is how often, how accurately and how positively your brand appears in AI-generated answers.

In simple terms, parametric visibility is what an AI model already "knows" from its training and memory - without needing to search the web in real time.

Dynamic visibility is when an AI model retrieves live information from the internet (searches for it) while generating an answer.

Corpus engineering is the is a discipline pioneered by geoSurge. It is the process of structuring and deploying information in ways that help AI systems better understand and represent a brand.

geoSurge tracks visibility across major AI systems and model families, including models used in tools like ChatGPT and Gemini.

geoSurge works with brands, enterprises, agencies and organisations that want to understand and improve their presence in AI-generated answers.

BYOK stands for "Bring Your Own Key." It allows customers to connect and use their own AI model API keys within the geoSurge platform.

Yes. geoSurge supports agencies and partners managing AI visibility for multiple brands and markets.

geoSurge focuses on the representation layer inside AI systems - not just surface-level prompt tracking or traditional search optimisation.

Getting started is simple. The geoSurge team works with you to understand your brand, markets and AI visibility goals, then helps you measure how your company currently appears across major AI systems. From there, geoSurge helps identify opportunities to improve visibility, accuracy and representation inside AI-generated answers through monitoring, analysis and corpus engineering. To get started, book a demo or speak with the team on sales@geosurge.ai
