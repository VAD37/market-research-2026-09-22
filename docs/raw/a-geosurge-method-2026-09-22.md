## Two kinds of AI visibility. Only one you can own.

When a model answers, it draws on two things: what it already knows from training, and what it looks up live. We call these parametric and dynamic visibility, and they behave nothing alike. Only one is yours to shape.

Parametric · memory — Ownable
### Durable, shapeable, yours

Feed a model the right information at the training moment and what it knows about you stays accurate. We call that discipline corpus engineering, and we pioneered it.

* Durable across prompts, regions and model versions
* Shapeable: feed the right information at the training moment
* Owned: it stays accurate even when you're not watching

Dynamic · RAG search — Volatile
### Whatever ranks, this minute

Live retrieval surfaces whatever wins at query time and shifts with every model update. Chasing it is chasing a target you don't control.

* Volatile: surfaces whatever ranks at query time
* Uncontrollable: shifts with every model update
* A moving target you're always chasing, never holding
