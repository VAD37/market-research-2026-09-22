## Pull notes — mechanical only
- CSV export endpoint returned HTTP 200 without login. Values are percentages of referrals among the listed chatbots (resp. search engines), not query counts or user counts.
- Grok, Meta AI and Amazon Rufus are not columns in the AI Chatbot export.
- Absolute referral volume (AI chatbots vs search engines) is not in either export.
