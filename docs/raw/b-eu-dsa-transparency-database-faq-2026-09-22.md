## Verbatim / as reported by the fetch tool

"Article 17 of the Digital Services Act (DSA) requires all providers of hosting services to provide clear and specific information, called statements of reasons, to users whenever they remove or otherwise restrict access to their content."

The database "specifically does not contain the content that was subject to moderation" — it records only the moderation decisions and accompanying information, with personal data removed.

The database's scope is "focused exclusively on statements of reasons from online platforms," defined by the page (per the tool's read) as services that "not only store information provided by users but also disseminate it publicly."

**"The document does not mention advertising or ad repositories. There is no reference to Article 39 or any content related to advertising transparency in this FAQ material,"** per the fetch tool's read.
