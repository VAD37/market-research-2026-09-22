## Verbatim

Title: "Three Rings Inc. — How Three Rings Inc. uses Muck Rack for Generative Engine Optimization, targeted outreach and more"

About: Three Rings Inc., co-founded 2017 by Doug Broad; "integrated PR and marketing agency" for "B2B tech companies"; headquartered in Boston; "has used Muck Rack … since 2021".

The solution — features adopted over five years, as listed:

> - Generative Pulse helps them understand how their clients show up in AI-generated answers.
> - AI Visibility Badges highlight the outlets or journalists cited most frequently in AI-generated responses.
> - Media List Agent uses AI to recommend relevant journalists and creators based on their pitch.

**Using Generative Pulse to inform AI-driven content strategies**

> AI-powered search is becoming more influential in how journalists, consumers and decision-makers find information. That’s why Three Rings uses Muck Rack’s Generative Pulse tool to help clients understand how their brand appears in AI-generated responses.

> One of their clients in the data management space uses quarterly Generative Pulse reports for a competitive comparison across AI tools for its key industry prompts. By reviewing the queries and responses, the client can pinpoint content gaps and opportunities.

> “They’re using it as a diagnostic tool as part of a broader GEO program,” Doug explains. “They look at what prospects are asking and create content that turns into authoritative, cited answers to those queries.”

> Just recently, this client closed a six-figure deal with a major consulting firm that was directly linked to an inquiry generated through ChatGPT and attributed to its GEO program.

> And Three Rings continues to find new ways to use the Generative Pulse reports. The agency uses the tool to analyze prospective clients against their competitors to inform pitch ideas and campaign plans in new business pitches. The team also recently used data from a Generative Pulse report as part of the quarterly business review KPIs for a major ad tech client.

**Building highly targeted media lists across complex markets** — ad-tech client campaign:

> Recently, Three Rings sent around 350 pitches for their ad-tech client on multiple data-backed story themes. Armed with Muck Rack’s intel, they tracked a 51% open rate, and of those opens, they saw a 61% click-through rate on the reports and assets in the pitch. In the span of two months, this resulted in more than 70 high-quality pieces of coverage in Marketing Week, EMarketer, The Agile Brand, Chain Store Age, The CPG Guys, and a key mention in a front-page Wall Street Journal story […]

> By the numbers: 350+ pitches sent · 51% open rate · 61% click-through rate · 70+ placements
