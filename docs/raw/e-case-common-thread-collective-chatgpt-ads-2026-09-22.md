## Verbatim

> "On May 7 we made a decision about ChatGPT Ads with no performance data and no comparable accounts.
>
> We went for it anyway.
>
> Sitting across 170+ brands creates a specific obligation: you move on new channels before the case study exists, because you're part of writing it. So we built the playbook, put together a clear offer, and asked a select group of brands to claim their account and add us. No performance guarantees. Just a foothold in a channel where the audience arrives with a fundamentally different intent.
>
> Twelve days later, we had our first brand live. High-AOV product, average order above $1,000.
>
> Thirty-four days in, here's what the account looks like:
>
> $7/day on May 22. $1,005/day on June 22.
>
> 142x spend growth. $9,620 in total spend. $19,000 to $38,000 in attributed revenue depending on the model. ROAS between 3.3x and 6.8x, midpoint around 5x. CTR of 0.94% across 136,000 weekly impressions at $4.41 per click.
>
> In-platform attribution isn't clean. But downstream data from Triple Whale tells a consistent story. Both models are positive.
>
> High-AOV products don't need volume to justify new channel spend. A few closed sales per week covers it. The channel doesn't need to be proven at scale before the math works.
>
> The brands that claimed their account in May now have 34 days of learning their competitors don't. The ones waiting for a clean attribution story before spending their first dollar are reading case studies instead of writing them."
