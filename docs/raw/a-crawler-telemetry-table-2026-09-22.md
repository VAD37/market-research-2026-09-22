## Caveats

- Every crawl-to-refer ratio and every crawl-purpose/client-type share in Table 1 comes from Cloudflare's telemetry over its own customer base — not a web-wide figure, per `channels.md`'s bias line for C26/C27 ("Cloudflare-customer sites only; sells bot control").
- Per the task's discard rule, no crawl-request count in either table is recorded as traffic, and no referral count is recorded as influence or sales. The crawl-to-refer ratio is recorded as its own distinct diagnostic metric, flagged with a `note: source crosses metrics` line in each underlying raw file's header.
- The three dated Cloudflare readings (2025-07, 2025-08, 2026-09) are not comparable trend points without knowing whether the underlying customer/industry mix held constant — recorded side by side per root `CLAUDE.md`, not averaged, not extrapolated.
- H1 ("Referral from AI assistants converts above organic search") is not confirmed or killed by anything in this cluster — this cluster supplies **context** for H1 (the crawl-to-refer ratios show how much crawling happens per unit of referral traffic, i.e. how rare the referral event is that H1's conversion claim would apply to) but carries no conversion-rate or sales figure itself. H13 ("At least one P1 engine publishes a countermeasure naming a technique documented in Pass 5") is likewise not scored here — this cluster's platform docs describe crawler identity and opt-out mechanics (robots.txt, Crawl-delay, Applebot-Extended, Google-Extended, nosnippet/X-Robots-Tag), which is adjacent context for H13 but names no Pass-5 manipulation technique specifically; H13 itself is P5-c7's job.
- Priority-3 rows (Meta, Grok, DeepSeek) are existence-check lines only, per `plan.md`'s "Priority-3 and priority-4 engine existence checks are already assigned to `P10-d0-rufus-p3`" and this cluster's own brief ("one existence-check line for priority-3"). No raw file was created for any of the three; each unknown line names the exact query run.
- Priority-4 engines (Naver, Kakao, Baidu) are out of scope per `scope.md` D2 — **except** Baidu appears in every Cloudflare crawl-to-refer table pulled in this cluster, since Cloudflare's own product includes it; Baidu's figures are recorded in Table 1 as found, without opening a Baidu-owned platform-primary page (out of scope per D2, "Record as deferred, not as unknown" — deferred here, not chased further).
