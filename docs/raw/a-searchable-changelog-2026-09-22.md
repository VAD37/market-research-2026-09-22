# Changelog

August 11, 2026
## July round-up

July was one of our biggest months yet. ChatGPT ads analytics arrived, the Agent picked up writing and planning powers, Reports was rebuilt from the ground up, and MCP became genuinely powerful. Here's everything in one place.

### ChatGPT ads analytics

ChatGPT is ramping up ads across the world. It's a paid layer sitting right next to the answers your buyers already trust, and an important piece of the puzzle when it comes to understanding the AI visibility landscape.

* Backdated data from day one: we've been capturing ads for over a month, so the history is there the moment you open it.
* See every advertiser: filter to your direct competitors and browse the creatives they're running.
* Check your own exposure: see which of your prompts trigger ads. Around 10 to 20% today depending on category, and rising.

### Agent updates

Read the blog post

* Content is now a mode of the Agent: ask the Agent for a blog and it opens the document workspace, canvas on the left and chat on the right. It plans, you approve, and the draft builds while you watch.
* Edit in place: highlight a line to have it rewritten, fill in your SEO, then publish straight to WordPress, Webflow or Shopify.
* Plan mode: ask for something substantial and the Agent builds a plan first, with a title, ordered steps, and one question where it needs an answer from you. It waits for your approval before running anything. Read the full blog post.

### Reports, rebuilt

* Over 80 data blocks: spanning AI visibility, AI traffic, Shopping, LLM Analytics, Search Console and GA4.
* Blocks stay live: filtering to a single competitor re-scopes the report rather than making you rebuild it.
* Share it your way: send as a PDF, a live link with a password and your own branding, or a scheduled email.
* Or skip the building entirely: ask the Agent for a 30 day executive report.

### MCP upgrade

* Provider-neutral OAuth: Claude, Cursor, ChatGPT, Grok and Gemini all connect the same way. Add the server, sign in, and pick your projects.
* 22 workflow tools: trigger audits and build reports without leaving the chat.
* Interactive cards, not paragraphs: eight of them, from Competitor Ranking to Topic Analysis, with live date ranges and rows you can expand.

### Also shipped

* Compare competitors on the Visibility Score chart: one toggle overlays up to ten competitor trend lines on yours.
* Chart change markers: charts mark themselves when prompts or locations change, so you can tell what caused a movement.
* Visibility grouped by AI platform: slice visibility by individual engine, as you already could by topic or location.
* WordPress connector: a plugin for AI traffic tracking, with WP Engine, Kinsta, Pressable and Flywheel routing straight to it.
* A five-level role system: Owner, Super Admin, Admin, Member, Viewer.

Plus smaller fixes: the Prompts table sorts by sentiment, prompts on the Research page edit with one click, Knowledge Base topics rename in place, and report folders persist across devices.

## Referral Programme

Get rewarded for bringing other teams to Searchable. Active paying customers can now refer new accounts in exchange for a recurring revenue share, while the people they refer get a discount on their first six months. A simple way to earn from a network you've already built.

What's new:

* 20% revenue share for referrers: earn 20% of every referred account's payments for their first 6 months.
* 30% discount for referees: new customers get 30% off their first 6 months on any paid plan, applied automatically at checkout.
* No cap on referrals: refer as many accounts as you like, with rewards stacking across every successful signup.
* Built-in referrer dashboard: track your unique link, clicks, signups, qualified referrals, pending balance, and payout status from inside Searchable.
* Flexible monthly payouts: Stripe (bank), PayPal, or USDC, with a $20 minimum threshold.
* Clean attribution: last-click attribution with a 30-day cookie window, and a 30-day holding period before payouts clear to cover refunds.

Find your referral link in-app under the new Referrals section.

June 5, 2026
## Public API & MCP upgrade

We've massively expanded what our public API and MCP can do. External tools, AI agents, and our own reporting agents can now pull almost everything the dashboard shows, automatically, with the same numbers as the UI.

What's new:

* Google Analytics (GA4): AI-driven traffic, referrals, top pages, and traffic sources, available directly via API.
* Sources: which sites and pages cite the brand, with content types, trends, and full drill-down.
* Sentiment: brand sentiment, history, and head-to-head competitor comparison.
* Location: visibility broken down by country and region, plus location filters on sentiment and sources.
* Query fanout: the follow-up questions AI engines ask while answering a prompt.
* Opportunities: now surfaced via the API.
* On-demand reports: generate shareable visibility and combined reports via API (previously sentiment-only).
* Trigger audits on demand: kick off audits from any external tool or agent.
* White-label and unbranded outputs: plus pagination for large datasets, expanded docs, and live tests.
* Speed boost: visibility and sentiment reads now run on ClickHouse, purpose-built for this kind of querying, so responses are dramatically faster. Every fast path ships with parity tests that prove it returns the exact same numbers as before.

Net effect: our AI connector grew from 8 to 30 tools, roughly 4x. Agents can now answer far richer questions about a brand's AI visibility without anyone exporting data by hand, and everything is much quicker.

May 1, 2026
## LLM Analytics

Read the blog post

Your next customer may meet you through an answer they never typed into Google. By the time a human visitor lands on your site, an AI system may already have crawled, summarised, compared, and judged your pages. Most analytics tools only show the click. LLM Analytics shows the AI systems reading your site before the click happens, and whether that attention turns into AI-referred visitors.

What's new:

* Bot type tracking: AI citations, training, indexing, and agent visits surface as four distinct streams, broken down by page and by platform.
* Attribution percentage: the blended ratio of crawls to referral clicks, so you can tell which platforms turn AI awareness into traffic.
* Crawl coverage: the share of your site AI is aware of, rolled up into a site health score so you know where the gaps are.
* Bot discovery: every crawler reaching your site, including the major ones from Google, OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon, Apple, TikTok and Bingbot, ready to add to your tracking when one matters.
* Granular access logs: timestamped, page-level entries showing exactly when and how each platform indexed your content.

Setup is native on Vercel, supported on Cloudflare, and available via custom REST API for any other stack. Read the full launch blog post.
