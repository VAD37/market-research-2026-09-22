## Caveats

- Every agenda pulled in this cluster names a screened count of sessions read for set O/P matches, but abstract text was captured only where the source page rendered it in the static DOM (CMW, ANA, SMX Advanced, GEO Conference) or where a per-session click was followed for the two cases graded (Christian Hustle, Hadeer ElMalah). brightonSEO's and most of SMX Munich's and SMX Advanced's matched sessions were screened at the title/track level only — a session with a hidden client-result claim in its full abstract would not have been caught. This under-counts, not over-counts, the true cleared total.
- Three of the eight fully-named events in the task's target list (Marketing AI Institute MAICON, ANA, Content Marketing World) plus SMX and brightonSEO are US/UK; the two events intended to give EU coverage are SMX Munich (Germany, partially captured) and a checked-but-empty attempt at OMR Festival (Germany). No fully EU-native event's session-level agenda was captured end-to-end this pull.
- Every graded talk here (both Fools gold cases) is self-reported by the vendor or in-house practitioner about their own company's results, with no independent measurer or baseline disclosed — consistent with `docs/method/plan.md`'s "Pass 4 is the pass most likely to return little" expectation, and with `hypotheses.md` H3/H11's kill conditions being live possibilities pending the rest of this Pass.
- GEO Conference is structurally unusual for this census: it explicitly forbids public recordings of any talk ("Is this recorded or streamed online? No."), so its only path into `raw/` as a graded case is a speaker's own independent social-media recap — which is what produced the one GEO-Conference-sourced case in this cluster. Other talks at that event (Expedia, Anthropic, Webflow, etc.) may carry stronger claims made on-stage that never surface publicly by the venue's own design.
- Session totals for CMW (156) and brightonSEO (81, Day 1 only) include non-substantive logistics entries (registration, meals, networking breaks) that the source platforms list as "sessions" with their own detail pages; this inflates the raw denominator relative to a strictly talk-only count. Neither file fully decomposes the substantive-vs-logistics split.
