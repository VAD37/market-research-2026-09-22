What is a ChatGPT ads tracker?
A ChatGPT ads tracker is a tool that monitors sponsored placements appearing inside ChatGPT’s answers. It’s different from AI-visibility or mention tracking, which watches where brands appear organically. SE Ranking’s version shows the advertiser behind each placement, the destination link, and how that presence changes over time, inside your wider Competitive Research.
Is this the same as ChatGPT visibility or brand-mention tracking?
No. Mention tracking shows where your brand appears organically in ChatGPT’s answers. This shows the paid ads inside those answers and the advertiser behind each one — a separate layer of the AI-search picture, and one that rarely overlaps: SE Ranking’s research found the advertiser is also cited among organic sources in just 3.63% of ChatGPT ad placements.
Ads on ChatGPT are new — is this actually happening at scale yet?
Yes. SE Ranking’s August 2026 analysis of 50,006 commercial prompts found ChatGPT already showing a paid placement — typically one sponsored card per answer — on 25.94% of them, so this isn’t an edge case; it’s a live layer of ChatGPT’s current answers.
What if ChatGPT ads aren’t showing up for my prompts yet?
Coverage follows the markets and prompt types where ChatGPT ads are currently live and expands as OpenAI rolls out further, so you’re set up to read the paid layer the moment it reaches your priority prompts.
Do I need a separate subscription for this?
No. Ad detection sits inside Competitive Research in SE Ranking (Competitive Research → AI Search → Prompts → Ads), next to the AI mentions and rankings you already track — no separate contract or add-on.
ChatGPT’s ad auctions rotate — is a single check reliable?
No — the same prompt can show an ad in one session and none in the next. Detection runs on a recurring cadence rather than a one-off snapshot, so you read presence and share-of-voice trends in ChatGPT over time instead of a single moment.
Do I need to run the prompts myself to see the ads?
No. Detection runs independently across tracked prompts on a recurring cadence, so you don’t need to manually trigger or interact with ChatGPT to see what’s showing up.
