Headline metrics (verbatim, as displayed): "36.2% AI Visibility (from 2.4% baseline)" / "25.6% Share of Citations (#1, ahead of Tractive)" / "+36.2% Organic traffic month-over-month"

"The Client — MiniFinder Sweden AB is a Scandinavian IoT company selling GPS trackers for pets, hunting dogs, seniors, vehicles, and fleet management. It operates a proprietary app platform with a direct e-commerce model. When the company decided to expand into Germany, it was entering a market dominated by well-funded incumbents with years of brand recognition. The real challenge was winning AI search visibility before purchase decisions were made, in a fragmented category where AI engines had already formed strong brand associations."

"The Ask — Could a Scandinavian brand with limited German presence challenge category leaders in AI-generated search results before spending years building awareness the traditional way? MiniFinder asked iGrow to establish and systematically grow AI visibility across ChatGPT, Google AI Overview, Copilot, Perplexity, and Gemini for their priority categories, and to track competitive positioning against Tractive, Garmin, Weenect, and PAJ in real time."

"Steps Taken in Rankscale — 01 Audit & Benchmark: 82 commercial prompts were configured in Rankscale's Search Terms module across 7 product clusters: Jagd GPS, GPS Hund, GPS Katze, senior GPS, fleet logbook, generic trackers, and personal safety. This established a German market AI Visibility baseline of ~2.4% and pinpointed where MiniFinder was absent from AI-generated answers. 02 Competitive Research: Rankscale's Competitor Analysis module benchmarked MiniFinder weekly against Tractive, Weenect, Garmin, PAJ, Apple, and Fressnapf across all major AI engines. Topic Performance and AI Engine Performance views identified GPS-Tracker für Haustiere on Gemini as the single largest untapped growth opportunity. 03 Execution & Reporting: AI-optimized content was produced to target the identified gaps, with Rankscale's monthly dashboard exports forming the basis of client reporting and strategic alignment calls. Progress was tracked in real time as MiniFinder climbed AI engine rankings across every tracked category."

"Results — MiniFinder grew from a 2.4% baseline to 36.2% AI Visibility in 90 days, securing the #2 position in the German GPS tracker category across all major AI engines. Top 3 Visibility reached 34.8%. Google AI Overview climbed to 75.0%, and Gemini (the identified opportunity) jumped to 31.2% (+15.2 points), making it the fastest-growing AI engine channel. MiniFinder now leads the category with a 25.6% Share of Citations, edging ahead of Tractive at 24.8%, a brand that has dominated GPS tracking for years. In the GPS-Tracker für Haustiere topic, visibility grew by 21.4 percentage points to 33.0%. Watch mit GPS reached 100% visibility. These were the exact gaps Rankscale surfaced in the research phase. AI search gains compounded into organic: month-over-month organic traffic grew 36.2%, making it the fastest-growing sales channel in May 2026. The AI visibility work didn't just win citations. It reinforced the entire search presence, creating a compounding growth loop that validated the channel as a primary acquisition driver."

"Rankscale Search Terms module — 82 commercial prompts tracked across 7 product clusters in the German market: Jagd GPS — 'Welchen GPS-Tracker für Jagdhunde kannst du mir empfehlen?' ... GPS Katze — 'Was ist der beste GPS-Tracker für Katzen?' Personal safety — 'Mobiles Überfallalarm mit GPS So funktioniert es'"

Client quote: "Content is everything today. Since we have been consistently producing, we are ranking. iGrow keeps us on track. Congratulations on what you have achieved so far." — Client, MiniFinder Sweden AB
