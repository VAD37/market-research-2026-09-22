Conversations that Convert: Copilot Checkout and Brand Agents
by
Nayna Sheth,
Ravi Yada
January 8, 2026

Your customers are already shopping with AI. And the gap between "I'm interested" and "I'm buying" is shrinking, with the fight for conversions moving upstream. A shopper asks a question, gets guidance, compares options, and decides—all in the same interaction.

This shift is happening everywhere: inside AI assistants like Copilot and on brand websites. Wherever shoppers are, they now expect AI to know what they need and help them act on it.

For merchants, the opportunity is clear: whoever shows up the strongest in the moments that matter will close the sale. AI can now bring high-intent shoppers straight to checkout, offering interactive guidance at the speed of a prompt.

Today we're unveiling two capabilities to help merchants thrive in this landscape shift:

Copilot Checkout turns conversations into conversions—instantly. No redirect, no friction, and you stay the merchant of record.
Brand Agents bring AI-powered guidance for customers to your own site—your brand's voice, built for fast, scalable adoption.

Introducing Copilot Checkout

A shopper is hosting a dinner party and asks Copilot for a dress recommendation. Within seconds, they've compared three options, asked follow-up questions, and made a decision. In the past, this journey would've taken a dozen tabs and possibly ended up with an abandoned cart.

Copilot Checkout can help solve that problem.

By being there for the customer's spark of inspiration, the quick product comparison, the question of value, and the decision to purchase, Copilot Checkout evolves from an AI platform into a true retail companion. Conversation to conversion all happens in one place, frictionless. We know that meeting the customer where they are—and standing ready for when they want to make that purchase—is core to the success of any retailer.

We also know so many businesses don't want to just hand their customers to an AI agent that won't put in the work to keep them engaged with a brand. With Copilot Checkout, you stay the merchant of record. You own the transaction, the customer data, and the relationship.

The result is an empowered merchant and a happy customer. Less friction, greater access, more conversions.

Meet Brand Agents: Your brand's voice, powered by AI

Every great in-store experience has a knowledgeable associate... someone who understands your brand, listens to the customer, and helps them find the right product with confidence. Brand Agents bring that same experience to your website.

Brand Agents are AI-powered shopping assistants that speak in your brand's voice and guide customers naturally from curiosity to purchase. Deployed in hours—not weeks—they turn everyday questions into meaningful conversations that help shoppers discover more, decide faster, and feel confident clicking "buy." The result is a more intuitive shopping experience and measurable performance gains. Across merchants, sessions assisted by Brand Agents deliver higher engagement and stronger conversion than sessions without them.

Alexander Del Rossa, a premium sleepwear retailer, saw over 3X higher conversion rates in Brand Agent-assisted sessions compared to unassisted sessions.

"Integrating Brand Agents into our Shopify store was the best example of AI integration seen to date. Brand Agents learned our inventory and provided recommendations that required little engagement from our team."

— Tony Baldwin, CTO, Alexander Del Rossa

Customer-centric interactivity that feels natural

Just as good store associates don't force customers straight to the cash register, Brand Agents don't force customers through menus or filters. They meet shoppers where they are, helping them explore and intentionally moving them to purchase.

Intuitive conversations: A customer might start with, "What would be a good gift for my mom?" The agent responds in your brand's voice, asks the right follow-up questions, compares relevant products, and narrows options based on fit, preferences, and intent—just like a great associate would.
Product comparison, guidance, and assistance: As shoppers get closer to purchase, Brand Agents help remove friction. They answer last-minute questions about shipping or returns, surface checkout links at the right moment, and recommend complementary products that feel helpful without being pushy.
Upselling and cross-selling: Because Brand Agents understand your catalog and customer behavior, they can also support post-purchase moments—suggesting add-ons, replenishments, or related products based on what a customer has already bought.

The outcome is simple: more confident shoppers, better discovery, and higher conversion... all without sacrificing your brand voice or customer trust.
