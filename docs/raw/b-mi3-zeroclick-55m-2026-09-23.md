ZeroClick has successfully raised $55 (US) million to launch an AI supported advertising network. The funding round was supported by Anthos, Ludlow, Anfa, Wonder, Protagonist, and Ryan Hudson, the founder of ZeroClick. Hudson is known for previously growing Honey to a $4 billion sale to PayPal, with backing from the same group of investors.
ZeroClick's advertising model integrates ads into AI assistants' reasoning processes, allowing advertiser-promoted context to be considered in real time. The platform is already utilised by over 10,000 advertisers and millions of users of internally developed products.
"As AI transforms how people search, learn, and decide, ZeroClick gives brands a new way to connect to customers. Instead of shouting the loudest or interrupting workflows, ZeroClick enables advertisers to provide additional context that enriches answers for people and their agents in moments of discovery and decision," said a ZeroClick statement.
The company is expanding its capabilities to AI developers and platforms, including browsers, extensions, and AI-native products and services.
"ZeroClick ads integrate natively into AI assistants' reasoning processes. Advertiser promoted context is considered by AI in real time as answers are formed, improving AI responses with timely, transparent, and highly relevant information and offers," said the statement.
