## Verbatim

Title: "How Plaid grew Answer Engine referral traffic 300% with Profound"

"When Answer Engine referral traffic to Plaid started climbing faster than any other channel, the company's two-person organic growth team had no way to know what ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Claude were actually saying about Plaid. They deployed Profound, using Answer Engine Insights to replace the black box with visibility and Profound Agents to automate organic growth initiatives at scale. Since partnering with Profound, LLM referral traffic to plaid.com is up over 300%, with conversions from that traffic up 210%."

Quote: "Profound isn't just a vendor to us. They are a partner we can ideate with and think through new ways of doing things." — Hannah Hughes, Chief Marketing Officer, Plaid.

**"Building for an AI-powered financial future":** "Plaid is a financial data network that connects more than 9,000 apps and services to over 12,000 financial institutions. More than 150 million consumers have used Plaid-powered tools to manage their financial lives, and the company serves everyone from early-stage fintechs to large enterprises and banks." Quote: "AI is changing the way people manage their money, and institutions are evolving to keep up. We see this primarily as more personalized experiences, and to deliver those, financial institutions need consumer data that's safe and permissioned. That's what Plaid provides." — Hannah Hughes. "We've seen a similar evolution in how our customers discover new products. In the past they were using traditional search methods, and now they're also turning to LLMs to make those same discoveries. Plaid wanted to be ahead of the curve." — Hannah Hughes.

**"A hockey stick with no data behind it":** Quote: "It's no longer just about optimizing our content for Google. AEO matters right now because our buyers are turning to AI models and asking questions or comparing vendors. If Plaid isn't showing up, at the moment it matters most, we might miss an opportunity to educate those buyers on how Plaid can help their business." — Sarah Shaffer, Organic Growth Manager, Plaid. "The catalyst arrived in May 2024, when Google launched AI Overviews. At that moment AI Search became undeniable. We were already seeing LLM traffic grow faster than traditional search. It looked like a hockey stick, up and to the right, and had no sign of slowing down. Buyers were going to AI Search and we had no visibility into what these LLMs were saying, or if they were mentioning Plaid at all." — Sarah Shaffer.

Quote: "Before Profound, AI Search was such a black box. We knew our customers were asking questions but we had no idea what these LLMs were saying about Plaid." — Sarah Shaffer.

**"Finding a partner, not a vendor":** "Plaid needed more than a monitoring tool. The team needed a workbench for citation analysis, prompt tracking, competitive benchmarking, and content creation." Quote: "From the very beginning, Profound was the clearest thinker in the space. It was such an ambiguous black box we were trying to navigate, and Profound was the pioneer in building an all-in-one platform for AEO. And the Profound team was never trying to sell us. There was a problem, and they wanted to be the partner to help us solve it." — Sarah Shaffer.

**"From reporting tool to workbench":** "Since onboarding, Sarah has migrated her workflows to Profound. For example, she built a Content Optimizer Agent that pulls from sources like their prompt tracking, People Also Ask data, Perplexity search results, and more to then piece it together into one autonomous workstream that audits pages and rewrites them to earn citations." Quote: "Before, we'd mainly do research, a competitive gap analysis, a keyword gap analysis, spot-checking the SERP. It was a very manual process. Now Profound tells us exactly what needs to be fixed, and it's an endless feedback loop. It can run without us even having to press start." — Sarah Shaffer. "Sarah also uses an FAQ Generator Agent that surfaces the exact questions buyers are asking on Answer Engines ... And a set of AEO Health Report Agents automate the topical reporting on visibility, citations, and competitive benchmarking that used to take weeks to assemble by hand."

**"Two hours, not two weeks":** "Sarah remembers a request that came across for competitive analysis and content strategy for over 300 prompts. Two years ago, that would have been out of reach for a two-person team." Quote: "When we first got the ask to analyze hundreds of prompts, I thought it would be impossible. But with Profound, we connected our SEO and AEO workflow in Profound Sheets, loaded in every prompt, and ran the Profound Agents workflow at scale. In seconds, Profound produced very high-quality drafts we could build off of and ship." — Sarah Shaffer. "This project probably should have taken two weeks. With Profound, our team spent less than two hours. For someone like me who's done this manually in the past, that time savings is a game-changer because we're able to scale our work without compromising the quality of output." — Sarah Shaffer.

**"Rapid gains in LLM traffic and conversion quality":** "Since onboarding Profound, LLM referral traffic to plaid.com is up over 300%, and conversions from that traffic are up 210%, evidence that the audience arriving through Answer Engines isn't just larger, it's higher intent." Quote: "LLM referral traffic to plaid.com is up over 300% since we onboarded Profound, with conversions up 210%, which tells us the audience is high quality, not just high volume." — Sarah Shaffer.

"Team-wide, the pace has changed just as much as the traffic." Quote: "Profound Agents have allowed us to work 10x or even 100x faster than we ever have. Repeatable tasks that used to take 40 hours can happen in minutes." — Sarah Shaffer.

**"From marketing manager to Marketing Engineer"** and **"Building toward a fully integrated marketing system"** sections: qualitative, no additional quantified AI-visibility metrics — role-change and strategic-positioning quotes from Sarah Shaffer and Hannah Hughes, reproduced in full in the Pull notes source but not carrying a gradable number.

Closing quote: "The team working with Profound has been ahead of the curve. Having a tool like this helps us move our AEO strategy at the pace of AI innovation." — Hannah Hughes. Choose a partner that pushes you forward." — Sarah Shaffer.
