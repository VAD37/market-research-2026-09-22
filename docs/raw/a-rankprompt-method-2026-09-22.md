## The complete AEO loop, across 6 AI platforms

One prompt, asked to ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Claude, Gemini and Grok on the same day. One names you, the others do not. Rank Prompt shows you why, hands you the work, and measures it again the next week.

01. ### Measure
Ask the six engines the questions your buyers actually ask.
1. Ask the six engines your buyers' real questions, every week
2. Score every answer named or not, and where you rank — 0 to 100
3. Watch the competitors who gets named instead, engine by engine — #1 to #20

02. ### Understand
See what those answers are built on.
1. Open every source each cited URL, grouped by domain

4. Pitch the sites AI reads — outreach to the domains it cites — verified contacts
