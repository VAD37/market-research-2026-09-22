## Verbatim

Headline: "The Estée Lauder Companies Announces Partnership with Profound to Expand AI Visibility Across Its Global Brand Portfolio"
Date: 15 Sep, 2026. Dateline: NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--

"The Estée Lauder Companies Inc. announced a global strategic partnership with Profound, positioning itself as prestige beauty's leader in AI-native discovery. The partnership provides visibility into brand representation across leading generative AI platforms and opportunities to optimize that visibility.

As consumers increasingly rely on ChatGPT, Gemini, and other AI assistants for product discovery and personalized beauty advice, the companies are partnering to define the next era of beauty discovery.

The partnership will give the company a unified view of how its brands appear across major AI search platforms globally, including when and how they are recommended. The company will use insights and Profound's tools to optimize content across product pages, blogs, YouTube channels, and content creation—ensuring information is optimized for consumer visibility and LLM comprehension.

By integrating enterprise-wide AI representation insights into its One Operating Ecosystem, Estée Lauder strengthens its ability to understand how consumers encounter brands across emerging touchpoints."
