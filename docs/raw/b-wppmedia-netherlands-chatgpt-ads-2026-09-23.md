News6th Aug 2026
WPP Media as supporting partner ChatGPT Ads in the Netherlands
WPP Media is among a group of strategic partners building experience with advertising within ChatGPT. Through the unique, global partnership between WPP and OpenAI, WPP Media Netherlands is now putting this innovation into practice. Starting at the end of August, we will begin the first tests in the Dutch market.
The search landscape is changing faster than ever. With approximately 4 million weekly active users in the Netherlands, generative AI is emerging as a crucial channel where consumers not only ask questions but also discover products, compare options, and make decisions. Research by OpenAI shows that as many as 20% of conversations in ChatGPT have a direct commercial intent.
This is where innovation and brand advantage come together. As ChatGPT Ads launches as a new media channel in the Netherlands, WPP Media Netherlands is at the forefront, rolling out the first live tests for local advertisers in August.
Our clients have a front-row seat to the biggest transformation in digital search this decade. By claiming paid positions in ChatGPT alongside organic visibility, advertisers are guaranteed a prominent and direct spot in the conversational process. This offers the opportunity to proactively guide user decision-making at the precise moment they are in the discovery phase.
Advantage Through Global Experience 🌍
The main advantage for our Dutch clients? We aren’t starting from scratch. Through our global WPP network, we are already running pilots in markets such as the US, Canada, and the UK. The valuable learnings we’ve gathered there—from building the right conversational creative variants to optimizing context hints—are being applied directly to the Dutch market.
What does this deliver for clients in concrete terms?
- Maximum First-Mover Advantage: Brands build domain authority and visibility within this new channel from day one, before the competition steps in.
- Higher Conversion Opportunities: Brands can reach a highly engaged audience with active buying intent, precisely at a moment when consumers are genuinely open to advice.
- More Efficient Media Budget: No guesswork. Thanks to proven best practices from international markets, brands test directly with top-performing creatives and formats to deliver the highest return on investment.
How Does It Work?
Instead of traditional keywords, ChatGPT Ads revolves around intent within a natural conversation. A user no longer just searches for "best coffee machine," but asks: "What is the difference between a pod machine and an espresso machine in terms of taste and cost?" Ads appear directly beneath the AI's response, at the exact moment the consumer is making a decision.
The Perfect Match for Our Total Search Proposition
This development aligns seamlessly with our Total Search strategy. Within WPP Media, we no longer look at search behavior in silos. Total Search is all about being present at every touchpoint where the consumer conducts research: from Traditional Search (Google, Bing) to Retail Media (Bol, Amazon), Social Search (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube), and now Conversational & AI Search within platforms like ChatGPT.
By integrating ChatGPT Ads into our Total Search approach from day one, we help our brands claim a leading position in tomorrow's AI-driven search culture.
Want to know how your brand can benefit from our global insights and local pilots? Get in touch with your WPP Media team! 📈
