"The agentic commerce platform: Shopify connects any merchant to every AI conversation
January 11, 2026
by Shopify

Shopify now enables native commerce at scale across all major AI channels and built an open standard, co-developed with Google.

AI is the next great platform shift in commerce. Every day, millions of people turn to AI chats to discover, browse and buy. Agentic commerce is the future and in 2025 we set a vision to build its foundation. Today we're accelerating that vision.

We're announcing the Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP), a new open standard co-developed with Google to bring commerce to agents at scale. Native shopping on Google surfaces is rolling out soon, which means Shopify merchants can sell directly in AI Mode in Google Search and the Gemini app. We are also releasing an update to our Microsoft Copilot integration with a new embedded checkout experience...

A protocol to cover all commerce

The Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP)—co-developed by Shopify and Google, and already endorsed by 20+ retailers and platforms—is an open standard for AI agents to connect and transact with any merchant...

...

Direct selling in Google AI Mode and Gemini app

...Shopify merchants are also eligible for Google's new Direct Offers pilot. Select merchants will soon be able to present exclusive deals directly in AI Mode...

We're also rolling out an update to our Microsoft integration, enabling Shopify merchants to sell through Copilot Checkout, a new embedded checkout that allows users to shop directly in Copilot.

...Brands like Monos, Gymshark, and Everlane will soon sell directly in AI Mode in Google Search and the Gemini app, and merchants like Keen and Pura Vida are using Copilot Checkout to reach their customers.

...

The backbone of agentic commerce

With millions of merchants and billions of transactions processed, no platform understands commerce diversity like Shopify..."
