## Verbatim

### Q2 2025 transcript
- "The fourth opportunity is Meta AI."
- "Its reach is already quite impressive with more than a billion monthly actives."
- "The percent of people using Meta AI is growing and we're seeing new users' AI retention increase too, which is a good sign for that continued use."
- "WhatsApp continues to be the largest driver of queries as people message Meta AI directly for tasks such as information gathering, homework assistance, and generating images."

### Q3 2025 transcript
- "More than a billion monthly actives already use Meta AI, and we see usage increase as we improve our underlying models."
- "Our Business AIs will enable tens of millions of businesses to scale these conversations and improve their sales at low cost."
- "In the US, we're also starting to roll out the ability for merchants to add their Business AIs to their website so we can support the full sale funnel from ad to purchase."

### Q4 2025 transcript
- "Within Meta AI, the number of daily actives generating media tripled year-over-year in Q4."
- "Finally, we're seeing good early traction with our Business AIs in Mexico and the Philippines, with over 1 million weekly conversations between people and Business AIs now happening on our messaging platforms."

### Q1 2026 transcript
- "We're seeing similar gains within Meta AI following the broad roll out of our new model, with double-digit percent increases in Meta AI sessions per user."
- "Muse Spark is now powering Meta AI in direct chat threads across our Family of Apps, as well as the standalone Meta AI app and website, giving billions of people globally access to our latest model."

### Q2 2026 transcript
- "Since we rebuilt Meta AI and integrated Muse Spark, we've seen a 60% increase in the number of people interacting with the assistant each day and that continues to grow quickly week-over-week."
- "WhatsApp is already the leading surface where people engage with Meta AI"
