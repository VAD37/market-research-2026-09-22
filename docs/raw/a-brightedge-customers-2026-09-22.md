### NinjaOne case — full text

BrightEdge x NinjaOne
How NinjaOne Won 61% Share of AI Mentions in the IT Operations Category in Under a Year
61% — share of AI mentions. 52% — share of AI citations.
NinjaOne holds 61% share of AI mentions in the IT operations category, more than 20 points ahead of the next-closest brand tracked. Its share of AI citations reaches 52%, ahead of every other source tracked, including Reddit, YouTube, and G2.

About NinjaOne: NinjaOne unifies IT to simplify work for nearly 40,000 customers in 140+ countries. The NinjaOne Unified IT Operations Platform delivers endpoint management, autonomous patching, backup, and remote access in a single console to improve efficiency, increase resilience, and reduce spend. The company partnered with BrightEdge to extend its category authority into AI-generated answers.

The Business Challenge: NinjaOne wanted to know exactly where it stood in AI search: share of voice, mentions, and citations, measured against every competitor across every AI engine. That's what led NinjaOne to BrightEdge, bringing SEO and AEO tracking into one workflow.

NinjaOne's Strategy with BrightEdge: Group prompts the way the business runs — NinjaOne grouped its prompt tracking by product line, audience, and brand and feature-parity topics. Start new launches with data — early topic ideas from product marketing get run through Data Cube X and AI Hyper Cube to find priority topics and where those conversations are already happening. NinjaOne usually reaches category leadership on new topics within three to six months. Take SEO authority into AI search — PR, affiliate marketing, paid search, and customer advocacy teams all have their own access to this data now. Turn insight into weekly action — a share-of-mentions report across every product group each week helps determine which content gets refreshed, what gets written next, and where to focus.

The Results: NinjaOne's share of AI mentions grew from single digits to 61% over the course of a year, more than 20 points ahead of the next-closest brand tracked. "Once we started tracking by prompt group and visualizing it, we saw exactly how much influence we had over the conversation. We knew what to write, what to refresh, and where to focus. That leveled up our SEO and AEO overnight." — Tucker Smith, Senior SEO Manager, NinjaOne. Its share of AI citations climbed at the same pace, from single digits to 52%, ahead of every other source tracked, including Reddit, YouTube, and G2. Organic rankings held throughout, and the AI citation growth was built on the same content authority that was already ranking in Google.

How the Team Operates Now: AI visibility used to sit with one team and one report. Now it's a shared resource used across various teams at NinjaOne. In June alone, the platform had 300+ logins from more than 20 users spanning four teams outside SEO. "BrightEdge started as our SEO platform. Now our PR, affiliate, paid search, and customer advocacy teams can pull from the same data. AI visibility became something the whole company can monitor." — Tucker Smith, Senior SEO Manager, NinjaOne.

### Riskonnect case — full text

BRIGHTEDGE × Riskonnect
How Riskonnect Built on SEO Success to Win 57% AI Share of Voice in Four Months
495% — Growth in organic traffic to AI-optimized pages. 227 — AI Overview rankings tracked in four months.

By late 2025, Riskonnect was seeing more visitors land directly on the demo request page, signaling that buyers had already done their research before they ever arrived on its website. Riskonnect operates in a crowded risk management software market, where buying decisions increasingly start with AI search, not with perusing a vendor's website. Riskonnect already had a strong organic SEO performance built across key topics. The team wanted to bring that same success to AI engine optimization. And the window to build that before the rest of the category caught up was narrow.

ABOUT RISKONNECT: Riskonnect is a risk management software platform, helping organizations manage risk, claims, compliance, and resilience programs in one connected system. Riskonnect partnered with BrightEdge to extend its category authority into AI-generated answers, using BrightEdge's AI visibility, content, and competitive intelligence solutions in one workflow.

THE BUSINESS CHALLENGE: Across the industry, top-of-funnel B2B traffic was shifting as buyers increasingly sourced product information directly from large language models. Riskonnect's own data showed the same thing. But there was a second, harder problem underneath it: standard search optimization answers "are we ranking at the top?"; AI search adds "are we being described accurately?" Google Search remained the primary channel driving buyers to the category. The goal was to win visibility across both at once, not to trade one for the other.

RISKONNECT'S STRATEGY WITH BRIGHTEDGE: Define the prompt universe with data, not guesswork — Riskonnect used Data Cube X and AI Hyper Cube to define its prompt universe algorithmically. Build content around real AI prompts, not speculative topics — the team identified risk/compliance/resilience topics appearing most often in AI Overview responses and built or rewrote content to answer them. Reinforce authority through syndication — Riskonnect syndicated core themes to third-party industry publications. Track visibility across three connected signals — AI Overview rankings, competitive standing in AI-generated responses, and traffic, via Data Cube X, AI Brand Presence, and Google Search Console together.

THE RESULT: When the prompt set was created in February 2026 and tracking began, Riskonnect's AI Overview rankings started building immediately, climbing from the earliest baseline to 227 by April and continuing to grow through May. "BrightEdge helped us get a real idea of the scope of where we needed to operate. Intuitively, we were looking at a narrow set of prompts. AI Hyper Cube helped us understand where the playgrounds were, rather than guessing at it ourselves." — Scott Fenstermaker, VP of Digital Marketing, Riskonnect.

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR OTHER BRANDS: Share of voice is most meaningful when measured against your own category competitors, tracked over time. For Riskonnect, the real test is simple: does a risk professional researching a software solution find Riskonnect in the answer, right when they are deciding who to consider, and are the company and its products described correctly.

### Bloomfire case — full text

BrightEdge × Bloomfire
How Bloomfire Made Its Content AI-Ready and Grew AI Referral Traffic by 30%
2x — AI agent traffic to optimized pages. 30% — lift in AI referral traffic.

About Bloomfire: Bloomfire is pioneering the shift from knowledge management to enterprise intelligence. It helps organizations capture, organize, and surface institutional knowledge so employees and buyers find real answers, fast. Bloomfire partnered with BrightEdge to make sure the AI agents now researching software on buyers' behalf see its content the way it's meant to be read, using BrightEdge Agent Edge, built on AWS.

The Business Challenge: Bloomfire checked its own traffic data and found its comparison and category pages were some of the most visited on the site, and many of those visits were AI agents reading the pages on a buyer's behalf. The goal was to win the AI reader without giving up the site, the human experience, or the search rankings already in place.

Bloomfire's Strategy with BrightEdge: BrightEdge built Agent Edge to give AI agents a version of Bloomfire's content made specifically for how they read it, delivered automatically and built on AWS. Bloomfire's site, its visitors, and its search rankings stayed exactly as they were. Bloomfire rolled it out carefully: a baseline was measured first, then a limited set of pages went live so the results could be proven before expanding further. Once the numbers held up, delivery expanded across the site. "Without automation, Bloomfire would effectively have been maintaining two versions of its website: one for humans and one for AI agents." — Brian Zander, VP Marketing, Bloomfire.

The Results: AI referral traffic jumped 30% in July 2026, compared to the prior 90 days. Agent traffic to optimized pages doubled, and it held steady even as AI traffic slowed elsewhere. Citations and mentions of Bloomfire in AI answers grew right along with it. "The results gave us confidence to scale, with Bloomfire seeing an uplift in AI-driven traffic, referrals, citations, and mentions across AI experiences." — Brian, VP Marketing, Bloomfire.

**evidence_grade: Bronze** (on intake). Present: brand named (Bloomfire); date window — "30% in July 2026, compared to the prior 90 days" is the most complete date+baseline pairing of the three cases in this file; intervention described (phased Agent Edge rollout, baseline measured first). Missing: named engine(s) — "AI agents" / "AI experiences" generic, no ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini named; numeric baseline figure for the 90-day comparison period — only the percentage delta is given, not the underlying counts; sample size — none; independent measurement — BrightEdge again. Traffic-only claim (AI referral traffic, agent traffic), no revenue link — Bronze.
