> From Bottleneck to Growth Engine
> Banking has never been smarter, and Chime is the most loved banking platform delivering financial progress for every day Americans. Chime’s organic growth team faced a challenge every high-growth company encountered: a large, complex content library that had become difficult to maintain.
> With over 700 blog posts, strict compliance requirements, and a refresh process that relied on a combination of manual and custom GPT effort, the team was struggling to keep moving at the speed of search. They were only able to refresh about 50 posts per quarter, even though hundreds had the opportunity to drive further impact.
> The Challenge
> Chime built an internal, GPT-powered content workflow tool to scale content creation and ideation, a major step forward in increasing production. However, Gisele Goes, Chime’s Director of Organic Growth, knew there was still more opportunity to optimize blogs for their highest performance. She began exploring how to turn a complex and manual process into one built for maximum optimization.
> Before AirOps, Gisele and her team had to:
> Analyze SEO/AEO performance signals
> Identify content gaps and opportunities to improve rankings
> Create detailed content briefs for writers
> Draft and edit content using the internal workflow
> Manage internal reviews and approvals before publication
> This meant each refresh:
> Could take weeks to complete
> Required the team to invest significant time into coordination and manual analysis
> Left the team with less time to explore more AEO and SEO opportunities
> The optimization process was fragmented and manual, with GPT handling writing, but the true bottleneck happened before that point: identifying what to update, prioritizing it, and building a clear strategy for AEO and SEO optimization.
> The Solution
> Chime partnered with AirOps to unlock end-to-end AEO and SEO optimization, automating a process with automation, prioritization, and AI workflows that was primarily manual. Instead of treating all 700 posts equally, AirOps surfaced the biggest growth opportunities to boost visibility and rankings across AI search and Google, eliminating guesswork and focusing the team on content that mattered most. End-to-end content optimization on steroids.
> The exhaustive refresh process was collapsed into one system: briefs generated automatically, compliance checks built in, and one-click publishing directly to the CMS. What once took 45 minutes per refresh could now be completed in less than 10 minutes, freeing up time for more strategic initiatives.
> The Results
> The entire transformation happened in just six weeks. In July, AirOps configured Chime’s workflows and trained the team by mid-July. By the end of the month, the new system was live in production.
> The results were immediate and measurable.
> 70% increase in refresh velocity — from 16 posts per month to 27 posts per month on average
> 89% time reduction — from 45 minutes to under 5 minutes per refresh ‍
> 3x citation increase — increased citations in priority questions in less than 4 weeks
> These operational gains translated into real business impact. Chime established a reliable weekly publishing cadence and saw rankings begin to recover toward target positions. Perhaps more importantly, the team had the bandwidth to think strategically again.
> Looking Ahead
> Chime is expanding its freelance pool to support AirOps’ output, experimenting with new content, and tracking performance gains, with the long-term goal of optimizing its entire blog library and lowering acquisition costs through organic growth. The takeaway is simple: scale breaks manual processes, and with AirOps, Chime turned a bottleneck into a growth engine built for speed, compliance, and scalability.
