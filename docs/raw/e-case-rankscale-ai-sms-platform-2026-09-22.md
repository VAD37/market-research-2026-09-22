"01 Audit & Benchmark — Audited the client's current AI visibility across problem-aware and category-level prompts (e.g., 'best AI SMS platform for X'). Used Rankscale to benchmark positioning versus competitors in AI-generated answers."

"02 Prompt Research — Analyzed qualitative data including demo call transcripts to surface the exact prompts most likely to drive qualified demos. Uploaded dozens of high-intent prompts into Rankscale and organized them into themed clusters."

"03 Sprint & Track — Set benchmark metrics for a 3-month AI visibility sprint. Secured brand mentions and published targeted content aligned to prompt clusters. Shared monthly progress updates with a live Rankscale dashboard link."
