## Verbatim

> "STAMFORD, Conn., AUGUST 6, 2026 — CMOs Must Prioritize Strategically Critical Platforms and Strengthen Independent Measurement"

> "By 2028, more than 70% of global ad spend and 80% of U.S. ad spend will flow through self-serve advertising platforms in which AI materially influences media buying, cost and outcomes, according to Gartner, Inc., a business and technology insights company."

> "AI is deeply embedded in the algorithms that advertising platforms use to determine which ads are shown, to whom and at what cost. This 'back-office' AI is distinct from GenAI used to develop advertising creative. It operates behind the scenes to influence how advertising is bought, priced and delivered."

> "'AI was expected to make advertising more efficient, but improved platform economics does not necessarily translate into lower costs for the advertiser. CMOs need independent evidence that they're getting the returns they expect,' said [Eric] Schmitt [VP Analyst in the Gartner Marketing practice]."

> "Gartner clients can read more in the report Market Overview for Advertising Platforms."
