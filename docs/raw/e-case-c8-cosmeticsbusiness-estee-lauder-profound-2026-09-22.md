## Verbatim

Headline: "Estée Lauder Companies to scale GEO and AI marketing with Profound partnership"
Byline: Lynsey Barber. Published: 15-Sep-2026. Categories: Marketing, Digital.

"Estée Lauder Companies (ELC) partnered with AI marketing platform Profound to expand Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) and AI marketing across its brand portfolio including MAC Cosmetics and Jo Malone London.

The deal aims to scale 'AI-powered discovery across its beauty brands' amid increasing consumer use of ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude for product recommendations.

Profound will deliver 'a unified view of how its brands appear across major AI search platforms globally' and show 'when and how ELC brands are recommended, as well as where opportunities exist to improve visibility and accuracy.'

Profound's tools will optimize content across product pages, blogs, and YouTube channels to ensure 'product information, ingredients, claims and benefits are optimised for consumer visibility.'"

Quotes: Aude Gandon (Global Chief Digital and Marketing Officer, ELC): "The way people discover beauty is being rewritten in real time, and we intend to shape that shift rather than reacting to it." James Cadwallader (CEO and Co-founder, Profound): "ELC recognises that understanding how its brands are represented across AI is now essential to serving consumers wherever they choose to discover products and information."

Related context noted by the article: this follows ELC's recent expansion of AI capabilities with executive promotions for Brian Franz and Amber English.
