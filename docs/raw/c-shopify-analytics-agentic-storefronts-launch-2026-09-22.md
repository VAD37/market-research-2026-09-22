## Verbatim

"Millions of merchants can sell in AI chats
March 24, 2026
by Shopify

AI is the new front door to commerce. Shopify is what's behind it.

Shopify didn't wait for the agentic era of commerce to arrive—we already built for it.

Starting this week, millions of merchants can sell to ChatGPT users via Agentic Storefronts. Agentic Storefronts give merchants out-of-the-box access to major AI channels—ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, AI Mode in Google Search, and the Gemini app—managed centrally from the Shopify Admin.

...

Upgrading the shopping experience in ChatGPT

Hundreds of millions of ChatGPT users can now shop from Shopify merchants without ever leaving their chat. Users find products across the Shopify ecosystem through Shopify Catalog, then complete purchases via an in-app browser. Merchant customizations carry over—brand experience, pricing logic, payment methods, and checkout customizations. On desktop, ChatGPT links out directly to the merchant's store in a separate browser tab.

For merchants, it's equally seamless: Products become discoverable in ChatGPT by default via Agentic Storefronts, with no separate integrations, no apps, and no transaction fees beyond standard processing rates.

Orders flow into the admin with ChatGPT referral attribution, so merchants can see exactly where sales came from. Crucially, merchants remain the merchant of record, retaining ownership of customer relationships and data.

...

Driving momentum across AI channels

...Thousands of merchants are already selling in Microsoft Copilot, powered by Shopify. Shop Pay is also coming soon to Copilot... On Google, select brands have started selling in AI Mode in Google Search and the Gemini app, powered by the Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP)... UCP has broad industry support from leaders like Walmart, Target, Etsy, American Express, Mastercard, Stripe, and Visa. And soon, UCP will power checkout in some Meta experiences..."
