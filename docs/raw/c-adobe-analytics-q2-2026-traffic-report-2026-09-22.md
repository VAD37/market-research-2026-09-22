"Methodology
Adobe Digital Insights (ADI) offers the most comprehensive set of insights of its kind based on analysis that covers more than *one trillion* visits to U.S. retail sites and more than *100 million* SKUs, more than any other technology company. Analysis is significantly more in-depth and precise compared to other data sources because only Adobe has access to this volume of real-time, granular, transactional consumer data. It is aggregated and anonymized to provide insights on consumer spending and emerging trends.

Adobe additionally conducted consumer surveys of more than 5,000 U.S respondents in March 2026 on the use and attitudes toward generative AI (GenAI), focusing on how consumers leverage AI in their online shopping journey—from discovery and product research to purchase decisions. The survey reveals a clear story of growing usage and improving perceptions of GenAI.
