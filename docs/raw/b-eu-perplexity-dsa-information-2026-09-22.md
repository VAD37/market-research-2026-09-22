## Verbatim

"We recently updated our consumer Privacy Notice. We added more detail about cookies, first-party advertising measurement, and your privacy choices. We also clarified that we do not sell your personal data or send your queries, prompts, or conversation content to advertisers."

"Digital Services Act (DSA) Information ... Last Updated 7 May 2026

This article provides information relevant to the EU Digital Services Act (DSA)."

"**Point of Contact for Authorities**

Perplexity has designated a single point of contact for direct communication with EU Member State authorities, the European Commission, and the European Board for Digital Services. Authorities may reach Perplexity's designated contact by emailing dsa-authorities@perplexity.ai. This channel is reserved for government authorities and regulatory bodies acting in their official capacity under the DSA."

"**Point of Contact for Users in the EU**

Users in the EU can contact Perplexity for any DSA-related matters, including reporting illegal content and questions about content decisions, via our support team support@perplexity.ai. This serves as Perplexity's single point of contact for EU users under the DSA."

"**Legal Representative:**

VeraSafe has been appointed as Perplexity's DSA legal representative in the European Union, pursuant to Article 13 of the DSA. ... VeraSafe Ireland Ltd. Unit 3D North Point House North Point Business Park New Mallow Road Cork T23AT2P Ireland"

"**Average Monthly Active Recipients in the EU:**

In accordance with Article 24(2) of the DSA, for the six-month period ending 7 May 2026, Perplexity has calculated that its average monthly active recipients in the EU is significantly below the 45 million threshold relevant to the DSA."

## Pull notes — mechanical only

- Confirms, in Perplexity's own words, that it is **not** a designated VLOP/VLOSE — "significantly below the 45 million threshold." Consistent with its absence from the Commission's designated-services list (`b-eu-ec-designated-vlops-vloses-list-2026-09-22.md`).
- No mention anywhere on this page of an advertising repository or Article 39 — expected, since Article 39 obligations attach only to designated VLOPs/VLOSEs and Perplexity states it does not meet the threshold.
- The page does carry one advertising-adjacent line, quoted above, from an unrelated privacy-notice update banner at the top of the article: a statement that Perplexity does "not sell your personal data or send your queries, prompts, or conversation content to advertisers." This is a general privacy claim, not a DSA Article 39 disclosure, and is recorded here verbatim rather than interpreted.
- No exact figure is given for Perplexity's EU average monthly active recipients — only "significantly below" 45 million. Recorded as stated; not converted to a number.
