Headline: "Beauty Briefing: What to know about Sephora's and Ulta's AI partnerships"
Byline: Emily Jensen. Published: May 12, 2026.

"This week's briefing examined Sephora's and Ulta's artificial intelligence initiatives, including ChatGPT and Google Gemini integrations. [...]

Sephora launched an in-app ChatGPT integration allowing shoppers to use loyalty points within the OpenAI interface. Ulta Beauty partnered with Google Gemini to surface product assortments in shopping results and deploy a new AI assistant.

According to NielsenIQ, consumers conduct 'over 1 billion beauty-related searches per week on ChatGPT.' A Tinuiti report from April 2026 indicated 'more than a third of beauty shoppers — and over half of Gen Z — are using AI to research or purchase products.'

[...] Amazon sold '$8.1 billion worth of beauty products in Q1 2026 alone, representing a 13% increase' year-over-year. Amazon's Rufus AI bot helped drive sales; shoppers using Rufus 'spent 80% more' than non-users during November and December.

Ulta's net sales 'increased 11.8% to $3.9 billion in Q4 2025.' LVMH's selective retailing division (including Sephora) 'saw organic revenue growth of 4%, down 3% on a reported basis' in Q1 2026.

[...] Meghana Dhar, technology and AI advisor, noted: 'People are increasingly starting their shopping journey on these chatbots. I think the question is: Are they converting via that?' [...]

'The TLDR is that I don't think anyone knows the right go-to-market with [AI] shopping. Everyone's trying to figure it out. It's a race to figure it out.' — Meghana Dhar

Circana reported U.S. prestige beauty retail dollar sales grew '6% to $8.1 billion in the first quarter of 2026.' Mass beauty sales grew '7% to $18.1 billion in the same period.'"
