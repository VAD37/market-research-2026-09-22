## Verbatim (as extracted)

### Methodology

"22-prompt AEO category prompt set" run across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Gemini over a "30-day continuous measurement" window, yielding "1,936 total responses, 2,219 unique cited domains" and 17,551 total citations. The dataset is noted to predate "ChatGPT's August 2026 Reddit citation change," making it "a historical baseline rather than current representation" by the article's own framing.

### The seven eligibility bars

Content must simultaneously demonstrate: (1) passage-level retrievability (lists, tables, definitions); (2) verifiable authority signals; (3) crawlability by OAI-SearchBot and GPTBot; (4) clear recency indicators; (5) corroboration across 2-3 authoritative sources; (6) source-layer presence on cited surfaces; (7) engine-specific format preferences.

### "Source-layer presence" strategy (the corpus-seeding instruction)

A three-layer effort allocation is recommended: "Owned" (~25% effort — reference pages on your domain); "Earned editorial" (~35% effort — trade publications, third-party comparisons); "Community" (~30% effort — Wikipedia, Reddit, forums, G2). The article states this involves work to "pitch it to trade press, answer relevant community threads, and update Wikipedia where notability allows."
