> ## What Is Generative Engine Optimization?
>
> Generative engine optimization (GEO) is the practice of making your brand easier for AI platforms like Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity to cite, recommend, and mention when people ask questions.
>
> At its core, GEO strengthens how your brand and its key entities (products, services, people, locations, categories, and ideas) are defined, connected, and reinforced across the web. That matters because large language models (LLMs) don't behave like traditional search engines. They don't just rank pages. They retrieve information, interpret context, and generate answers. Traditional SEO relies on crawlability, indexability, and rankability. GEO adds a fourth pillar: retrievability, or how easily AI systems can find, trust, and prioritize your brand when generating a response.
>
> LLMs synthesize information beyond your website, drawing from knowledge graphs, publisher sites, review platforms, directories, and forums.
