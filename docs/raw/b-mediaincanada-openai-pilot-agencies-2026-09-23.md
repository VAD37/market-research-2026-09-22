## Verbatim

(Image: Pexels)
OpenAI announced in mid-January that it would begin testing ads in the U.S. across both its free tier and its lowest-cost subscription, ChatGPT Go, which has also rolled out globally. The testing has officially begun, with major agency groups, including WPP, Omnicom Media and Dentsu, participating.
According to OpenAI, ads are being served to adult users on the free and go tiers. The company said the ads do not influence ChatGPT’s responses, and that user conversations are not shared with advertisers. OpenAI added that the test is designed to gather early feedback from both users and advertisers, helping ensure that ads within ChatGPT create value for all parties.
WPP has confirmed its participation in the OpenAI Ad Pilot Program, describing it as more than a simple test of ad placement in ChatGPT. The agency said its goal is to understand how users engage with early ad formats and brand messaging, using the pilot as a lens into how conversational AI can reshape advertising.
Rather than treating generative AI as just another extension of search or social channels, WPP is approaching the experiment as part of a broader research strategy. The agency is exploring how to deliver value and maintain trust in AI-driven experiences, while testing campaigns across categories including automotive, CPG, entertainment, gaming, luxury, retail, technology, and travel. Clients taking part include Adobe, Audemars Piguet, Audible, Ford and Mazda.
In a Feb. 9 press release confirming participation in the OpenAI Ad Pilot Program, Will Swayne, global practice president of media and integrated solutions at Dentsu, said the agency views large language models as the next frontier for media. “Adopting an AI-native mindset means we continuously transform how we operate – harnessing AI to turn information into advantage, strengthening collaboration and keeping human value at the center, creating impact beyond optimization,” Swayne said, noting that clearly labeled ads will appear below relevant conversations in ChatGPT, creating a new opportunity to reach users in a “highly engaged, high-intent environment.”
Omnicom Media, which also reported it has secured placements for more than 30 of its clients in the program, highlighted a similar opportunity from the advertiser perspective. “The introduction of ads in ChatGPT creates a new way for brands to introduce products and services that consumers might not otherwise encounter through organic interactions alone – appearing at a moment when engagement and relevance are inherently high,” said Justin Wroe, chief commercial officer.
The agency noted that participating brands span multiple Omnicom Media agencies and represent a broad range of consumer categories, including apparel, automotive, beauty, CPG, hospitality, retail, QSR and technology.
