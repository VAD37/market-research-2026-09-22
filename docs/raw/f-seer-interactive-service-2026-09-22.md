> ## Four studies on how AI search decides which brands to cite
>
> Seer Interactive has been running generative engine optimization experiments since 2024. These four pieces cover what changed in ChatGPT's query fan-outs, how content recency affects citations across ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, why brand accuracy has to be fixed before category rankings, and how to pressure-test a GEO case study that promises results in a week.
>
> 1. **01 Research · June 2026 — The Proof: ChatGPT 5.5's fanout patterns reveal the importance of brand.** Wil Reynolds and Nick Haigler, Seer Interactive. "ChatGPT 5.5's fan-out sub-queries increasingly contain brand names — 'Seer Interactive GEO research' rather than 'top GEO agencies.' Across 617 prompts, and a single prompt re-run 30 times, Seer found that when your brand appears in the fan-out you are no longer competing inside the answer; you are the source being searched for."
> 2. **02 Study · July 2026 — Study: Content Recency's Impact on AI Visibility in 2026.** Sonny Vasquez, Seer Interactive. "Across 4,124 pages cited in LLM answers where both publish and update dates were readable, 72% had been updated in the past year but only 42% were published in the past year. Seer's 2026 content recency study found that the freshness LLMs reward is produced by maintaining old pages, not by publishing new ones. Based on citations from ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, March–June 2026."
> 3. **03 Framework · May 2026 — Stop Chasing AI Rankings Before You Fix How LLMs See Your Brand.** Alisa Scharf, Chief AI Officer, Seer Interactive. "In Seer's GEO work since 2024, no brand has had fully accurate representation across LLMs. One company whose employees averaged 10-plus years of tenure was characterized as having high turnover on the strength of a single negative review. Seer's recommendation: track the percentage of your brand attributes that LLMs report accurately, and treat that as your leading indicator before share of voice or category rankings."
> 4. **04 Playbook · May 2026 — The Problem & The Fix on GEO case studies promising results in 1 week.** Wil Reynolds, Founder and CEO, Seer Interactive. "Seer's three-step diligence on any fast AI visibility case study: look at the pages that were actually published, check whether the visibility spike held, and set it beside Google's 2006 penalties against BMW and J.C. Penney for publishing pages at scale. Then a three-week alternative built on branded prompt accuracy, page-level grounding data and real customer language — defensible to a board, at close to the same speed."
