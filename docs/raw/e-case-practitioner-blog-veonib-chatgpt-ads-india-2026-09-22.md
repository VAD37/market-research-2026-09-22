## Key Insights

- ChatGPT Ads deducts a $100 hold when you add billing information, even before running campaigns.
- Minimum daily budget for ChatGPT Ads campaigns is ₹750 in India.
- Campaign objectives are limited to Reach, Clicks, and Conversions—far fewer than Facebook or Google Ads.
- Context hints let you target specific topics or chat contexts, but remain optional in the beta.
- The campaign received 123 impressions and 38 clicks but zero conversions over two days.
- Website analytics showed no traffic fluctuation despite 38 reported clicks, suggesting tracking gaps.
- UTM parameters were not set up, making it impossible to trace traffic sources in analytics.
- ChatGPT Ads dashboard provides limited data—no query-level insights on where clicks came from.
- Average CPC was close to Google Ads, but CPM appeared higher than expected.
- ChatGPT Ads is still in beta and significantly less robust than Google, Meta, or even TikTok Ads.

## Frequently Asked Questions

What is ChatGPT Ads and how does it work in India?
ChatGPT Ads is an advertising platform within ChatGPT that lets businesses show ads to users during conversations. In India, it launched recently and works similarly to global versions but with limited features.

Why does ChatGPT Ads charge $100 upfront?
OpenAI places a $100 hold on your card as a security measure when you add billing information. It's not an actual charge but a pre-authorization mentioned in their documentation.

What is the minimum daily budget for ChatGPT Ads in India?
The minimum daily budget for ChatGPT Ads campaigns in India is ₹750. You cannot run campaigns with a lower daily spend.

What targeting options does ChatGPT Ads offer?
ChatGPT Ads offers location targeting, platform selection (web, Android, iOS), custom audience uploads, and context hints for topic-based targeting. It lacks the granular targeting of Facebook or Google Ads.

How many campaign objectives are available in ChatGPT Ads?
There are only three campaign objectives: Reach, Clicks, and Conversions. This is significantly fewer than platforms like Facebook or Google Ads.

Why did the ChatGPT Ads campaign get zero conversions?
The campaign received 38 clicks but no conversions. Possible reasons include limited ad relevance, insufficient data for optimization, or tracking issues since website analytics showed no traffic from the ads.

Why did website analytics show no traffic from ChatGPT Ads?
UTM parameters were not set up in the campaign, making it impossible to track traffic sources in analytics. Additionally, ChatGPT Ads may not pass proper referral data.

Is ChatGPT Ads better than Google or Meta Ads?
No, ChatGPT Ads is still in beta and far less robust than Google, Meta, or even TikTok Ads. It lacks detailed reporting, optimization tools, and proven results.

What data does ChatGPT Ads provide in its dashboard?
The dashboard shows impressions, clicks, CTR, average CPC, CPM, and spend. However, it does not provide query-level insights or detailed audience data.

Should I run ChatGPT Ads for my business in India?
It depends. If you want to experiment with a new platform and have budget to spare, it's worth testing. However, for reliable results, Google and Meta Ads remain more proven options.
