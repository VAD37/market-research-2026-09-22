### gist.ai/answers

> The AI answer engine your audience can trust. Our embedded AI answer engine drives engagement and enhances monetization.
> Gist Answers is an easy-to-implement widget that's fully customizable and powered by generative AI. For publishers, it's an opportunity to provide a deeper experience for users while generating revenue and promoting content. For brands, it's a unique way to create contextually relevant advertising.
> Share the value — Every answer includes attribution, and an opportunity for contextually relevant advertising to enable fair and scalable revenue sharing.
> What is Gist Answers and how does it work? Gist Answers is an AI-powered audience engagement and content discovery tool that embeds directly on your site. It allows readers to ask questions and receive clear, well-sourced answers grounded in your content. When your coverage does not address a question, Gist Answers supplements responses with information from the largest licensed content library in generative AI. Every answer includes clear citations and ProRata's proportional attribution, so readers can see exactly which publishers contributed. Publishers maintain full control of their content. It is never used to train AI models and can be removed from the network at any time.
> How does Gist Answers increase audience engagement and page views? Early integrations show that readers who interact with Gist Answers have: 3x longer sessions; 2.5 - 3x more page views; a significantly higher likelihood of returning.
> What is the business model for implementing Gist Answers? Gist Answers is free to implement and generates revenue through three distinct paths:
> Sponsored Questions: High-intent questions generated by Gist Ads are shared on a 50/50 basis with you.
> Generative Ads: Gist Ads appearing within answers generate revenue that is shared 50/50 between ProRata and the publishers whose content contributed to the response, distributed proportionally based on contribution.
> Partner Inventory: You retain 100% of the revenue from any additional advertising inventory on the answer page.
> Additionally, there are no development costs or AI token costs required to run Gist Answers.
> How is ProRata's proportional attribution different from a standard citation? A standard citation tells your audience where information came from. ProRata's proportional attribution goes further by accurately and repeatably measuring how much each source actually shaped the answer. [...] Publishers whose content contributes more to an answer receive a proportionally larger share of the ad revenue that the answer generates.
> Does Gist Answers use my content to train AI models? No. [...] We do not train an LLM using your content.
> How do I get started with Gist Answers? Get started in just three steps: Sign a partnership agreement. Request one through our contact form, or if you're a member of the News/Media Alliance, FIPP, or the DPCMO, opt in through our umbrella agreements with these organizations. Configure and integrate your widget. [...] Go live. Most partners are live within days [...]
