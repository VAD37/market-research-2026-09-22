### Author and date

Justin McKelvey, described in the piece as a "Fractional CTO, 15 years experience, 50+ products shipped." Published 2026-08-07.

### Methodology

Citations tracked "across two engines and 90+ commercial buyer queries" using "DataForSEO's LLM-mentions dataset (US, English, pulled July 31, 2026)." The author operates "a weekly citation monitor" on his own domains — one expert blog and one productized-services site.

### Citation counts by source

AI Consultant space (405 total mentions): "businessinsider.com 131 · reddit.com 110 · bls.gov 58," plus timesofindia.indiatimes.com 54 and axios.com 41.

Bookkeeping space (24,130 mentions): "reddit.com 5,963 · indeed.com 1,992 · forbes.com 1,585 · quickbooks.intuit.com 1,474." "Not one individual practitioner" appears in the top sources.

### The four-move playbook

1. "Publish your prices" — the author's business holds all ChatGPT citations from its cost pages.
2. "Be genuinely useful on Reddit" — Reddit appears in both engines' top three.
3. "Earn press, even small press" — outlets like Business Insider dominate.
4. "Publish original data" — the only content type both press and models cite.

### Key finding (own-domain result against commercial interest)

The author's expert blog received "14 citations on Perplexity and zero mentions in ChatGPT's citation dataset" — same author, opposite outcomes across the two engines.
