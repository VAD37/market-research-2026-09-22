## Are you struggling with AI search visibility?

Many businesses face these common challenges when trying to grow their presence in AI search engines:

* Struggling to track your brand across multiple AI platforms? With ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews all showing different results, it's nearly impossible to manually monitor where your brand appears—or doesn't.
* Low discoverability in AI-generated answers? Your competitors are getting cited while your brand remains invisible. Without the right optimization, AI engines simply don't know you exist.
* Inconsistent brand mentions across AI engines? One AI might recommend you while another ignores you completely. This inconsistency costs you qualified leads and damages brand authority.
* No idea which content AI engines actually cite? Traditional SEO metrics don't tell you what makes AI engines choose one source over another. You're optimizing blind.
* Wasting hours on manual spot-checks? Typing prompts into ChatGPT and taking screenshots isn't scalable. You need systematic tracking to make data-driven decisions.

Searchable solves these challenges with automated AI visibility tracking, competitive benchmarking, and actionable recommendations—all in one platform. Choose the plan that fits your needs and start dominating AI search results.
