## Verbatim

"How it works — From invisible to recommended, in four steps.

01. Measure — Enter your URL. Beacon audits your visibility across the major AI assistants in about 60 seconds.
02. Diagnose — You get a Visibility Score, a local ranking, and the exact gaps holding you back.
03. Implement — Beacon's agents make the changes for you, from website copy to schema to your business profile. You approve, they ship.
04. Track — Your score updates weekly, so you can see the work paying off and stay ahead of competitors.

What you get — Not a report. Work that gets done. Every output is built to ship, and Beacon can implement it for you once you approve.

AI Visibility Score — A 0 to 100 score benchmarked against local competitors, refreshed every time Beacon scans.

Competitive gap analysis — See exactly where competitors show up in AI answers and you do not, and what they are doing differently.

Optimized website copy — Content rewritten the way AI systems read, trust, and recommend a business.

Schema and FAQ structure — The technical markup and Q&A that tell AI assistants exactly what you do and where.

Google Business Profile — Specific profile updates that lift your local AI visibility, handled for you.

Optimized site preview — See how your site looks after optimization before you commit to anything."

What Beacon is, verbatim: "Beacon is made for small, local businesses, usually with one or two locations. It answers a question most owners cannot: why are the new AI assistants, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Grok, not recommending you? Then it does something about it. Beacon advises you on the marketing improvements that actually move the needle and implements those changes for you once you give the go-ahead. Made for one or two locations. No enterprise software, no marketing degree. Covers the assistants that matter. ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Grok, and more as they arrive. Advises, then acts. Beacon finds the fixes and carries them out for you. Nothing ships without you. You approve before any change goes live."
