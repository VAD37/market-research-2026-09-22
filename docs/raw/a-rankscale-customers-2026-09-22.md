## Verbatim — Austrian Optical Retail Chain (full case study, key sections)

"AI Visibility grew from a 14.7% baseline to 75.3% in 5 months, with a Recognition Rate of 85% across all tracked prompts. The client reached a 38% Top-3 Rate, appearing among the top 3 cited sources for over a third of all tracked prompts. Total tracked engagement reached 185 mentions and 372 citations across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot, and Google AI Mode."

"Steps Taken in Rankscale — 01 Audit & Benchmark: Brand-, category-, and location-specific prompts were configured in Rankscale's Search Terms module (e.g. 'optiker [city]' patterns across dozens of Austrian towns, plus 'who is the best optician in [city]?' comparison prompts), establishing a baseline AI Visibility of 14.7%. 02 Competitive Research: Rankscale's Competitor Analysis module benchmarked the client weekly against the market's established optical chains across all four major AI engines... 03 Execution & Reporting: ithelps Digital produced AI-optimized, location-specific content to close the identified gaps, using Rankscale's monthly dashboard exports as the basis for client reporting and strategy calls."

Per-engine location-prompt table (verbatim): "'optiker [smaller town]' (4 example markets): Copilot 71-83%, Google AI Mode 77-100%, ChatGPT 77-83%, Perplexity 83-91%. 'Welcher Optiker ist der beste in [major city]?' (2 example cities): Copilot –, Google AI Mode 67-83%, ChatGPT 63-67%, Perplexity 91-100%." Plus: "Across dozens of Austrian towns and cities, location-specific optician prompts now average 82% Found Rate across all four tracked AI engines."
