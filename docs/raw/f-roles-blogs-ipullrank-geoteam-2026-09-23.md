### New roles defined (iPullRank's framework, "Roles of the Future")

**Relevance Engineer** — "the cornerstone of any GEO-first organization, connecting traditional technical SEO with AI-first content architecture." Term introduced by Mike King at SEO Week 2025: "using a simple analogy — optimization is like being a car mechanic who fixes stuff and makes small tweaks to help cars go a bit faster. But what we actually need to be doing is building (in other words, engineering) cars, not fixing them." King: "SEO is a checklist culture" — engineering for relevance instead establishes technical standards. iPullRank defines "Relevance Engineering (r19g)" as "the intersection of information retrieval, user experience, artificial intelligence, content strategy, and digital PR to improve visibility across all types of search." King, from his "AI Mode piece": "SEOs spent the past twenty-five years preparing content to be parsed and presented based on how it ranks for a single query. Now we're engineering relevance to penetrate systems of reasoning across an array of queries."
Core responsibilities listed: Semantic Content Architecture; NLP-Driven Content Optimization; AI Retrieval Optimization; Experimental Design & Validation ("formulating hypotheses and running controlled experiments... A/B testing passage performance and comparing prompt effectiveness across AI platforms"); Performance Monitoring & Analysis; Technical Infrastructure ("building custom analysis scripts, automated workflows, and proofs of concept").
Key skills: NLP understanding; programming/automation (Python/scripting); semantic search optimization; information architecture/retrieval systems; vector embeddings; data analysis; prompt engineering and AI testing.

**Retrieval Analyst** — "specializes in understanding how AI systems select, synthesize, and cite information." Core responsibilities: AI Citation Analysis ("analyzing query fan-out patterns and synthetic queries — the background questions LLMs ask themselves during the retrieval process"); Competitive Intelligence; Platform-Specific Optimization; Content Performance Tracking (cites Duane Forrester's "new dashboard" for the GenAI era: "chunk-retrieval frequency, embedding relevance score, and AI citation count," from "Duane Forrester Decodes – '12 New KPIs for the GenAI Era'"). Key skills: AI platform behavior analysis; data analysis/pattern recognition; competitive research; A/B testing for AI optimization; cross-platform performance measurement; query expansion/AI reasoning chains.

**AI Strategist** — "lead the overall plan for how a brand shows up across AI-powered search and discovery... look beyond Google to focus on the entire AI ecosystem." Core responsibilities: Strategic AI Roadmapping; Cross-Platform Integration; Future-Proofing; Stakeholder Education ("shifting the internal conversation from viewing GEO as a 'traffic play' to understanding it as a core strategic system for brand visibility... a crucial reframe, given that 85.5 percent of SEO experts believe generative AI will replace traditional SEO roles" [stat as stated in source, uncited to a named study on this page]). Key skills: generative search behavior forecasting; multiplatform AI optimization strategy; AI model capabilities assessment; business-strategy development; agentic systems/multi-agent architecture understanding.

### Org chart — "Org Chart Placeholder: Future-Proof r19g Team"

Verbatim role list with one-line descriptions, presented as "how forward-thinking organizations are structuring their teams for the generative era":
- **Head of GEO** — "The strategic leader who owns the entire GEO function, reporting directly to the CMO or Head of Growth. They connect business goals with technical execution."
- **Relevance Engineering Lead** — "Reports to the Head of GEO and manages the technical infrastructure team. They oversee all the technical work that makes content discoverable and citable by AI systems."
- **Content Optimization Specialists** — "focus on semantic markup, entity optimization, and content structure for AI consumption... work directly with content teams to implement semantic improvements."
- **Structured Data Experts** — "implement and maintain Schema markup and other structured data formats."
- **Crawlability & Indexing Specialists** — "ensure AI systems can efficiently discover and process content... optimize for multiple AI platforms that each have their own different crawling and processing requirements."
- **Site Architecture Engineers** — "design technical infrastructure that supports both human users and AI crawlers."
- **Retrieval & Analytics Lead** — "manages the measurement and optimization team... figures out what's actually working and why."
- **AI Visibility Analysts** — "track how your brand appears across different AI platforms. They monitor citation rates, brand mentions, and content chunk performance across platforms like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and AI Overviews."
- **Data Scientists** — "develop predictive models and work with large datasets... build custom tools for analyzing semantic similarity and embedding distance."
- **Content Engineer** — "builds scalable content systems that blend AI automation with human creativity for maximum impact."
- **AI Content Creators** — "make content specifically optimized for AI understanding and citation."
- **Prompt Engineers** — "develop and test prompts for various AI systems to ensure consistent brand representation."

"Building a GEO team isn't just about hiring new people or learning new tools. The biggest shift is cultural — moving from an optimization mindset to an engineering mindset... GEO requires building systems that work consistently across multiple AI platforms."
