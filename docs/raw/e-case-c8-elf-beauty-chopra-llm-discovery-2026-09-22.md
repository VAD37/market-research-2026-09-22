## Verbatim

Headline: "'Culture is moving at the speed of swipe': Why E.l.f. Beauty considers itself an entertainment company as much as a beauty brand"
Byline: Emily Jensen. Published: Apr 1, 2026.

"When E.l.f. Beauty was founded more than two decades ago, the idea that consumers would buy beauty products online was something of a novelty. But in 2026, consumers are happily buying cosmetics on digital platforms, plus their online behavior is evolving faster than most companies can anticipate.

E.l.f., which owns E.l.f. Cosmetics, as well as Naturium and Hailey Bieber's Rhode brand, is attempting to keep up by diving headfirst into social e-commerce platforms like TikTok Shop and making use of artificial intelligence and large-language models like ChatGPT, even as their usage remains controversial among consumers.

E.l.f. Beauty chief digital officer Ekta Chopra stated that 'almost 60% of discovery is happening on LLMs, specifically ChatGPT and Google.' She emphasized the importance of presence throughout the consumer journey, noting 'the whole funnel is collapsing.'

Chopra explained that E.l.f. has developed an internal team to enhance product visibility to LLMs and uses AI to scrape data, helping products appear in specific customer searches on platforms like ChatGPT.

'This is not just, "Hey, this is what the consumer is searching for. I'm going to build this content,"' Chopra said, describing a 'whole mindset shift' needed across teams focused on solving future workforce dynamics rather than short-term problems.

[...] For E.l.f., competing on these platforms requires reconceptualizing its identity. Chopra stated: 'We call ourselves not a beauty brand anymore. We are entertainment, when you really think about it.' [...]

Regarding different industry approaches, Chopra noted that while Ulta joined TikTok Shop, Sephora announced a ChatGPT integration. She characterized this as Sephora's statement of trust in ChatGPT as a consumer platform, comparing it to 'Ulta-Sephora wars.'

Chopra highlighted Sephora's decision to connect its 80 million loyalty members with ChatGPT for personalization, calling it significant 'when you think of all the trust issues [with AI] and everything.'

E.l.f. invited Google to its New York offices to help teams better leverage AI. However, Chopra emphasized that while the company uses AI for efficiency, it maintains reliance on human creativity."
