Title: "The growth system for the AI-first buyer journey | AirOps"

"platform — The growth system for the AI-first buyer journey — The winning playbook for marketing teams is built on an AI Search platform that gives you intelligence on how your buyers actually use AI, then turns it into action across the highest-impact channels for your brand. Measure the outcomes, connect them to revenue, and feed it back into the system to build a connected system that drives growth."

"## Connect intelligence, action, and measurement in a continuous loop — ### Intelligence — See where your brand stands with the buyers who matter, where competitors have an advantage, and which opportunities deserve investment. ### Action — Turn those opportunities into action across channels with agents that learn from every run, review, and result to improve the next one. ### Measurement — See what shipped, how buyers and AI agents responded, and how that activity connects to conversions, revenue, and your next investment."

"## Measure the market, not the prompt list — AirOps starts by defining the market around your growth priorities. From there, it shows where your brand stands, how much opportunity is at stake, and which gaps are worth closing.

Prompt-tracking tools run a selected list of questions through AI engines and count how often your brand appears for any buyer, not your ICP.

Generic, volume-based prompt tracking scores give you a false sense of confidence. A strong visibility score across a broad category feels good, but often hides a weakness with the customers you actually need to win.

### Category Influence Overview — Total influenced — ### $54.2M — $13.7M brand, $40.5M competitors

1. See the market — Understand your brand's position across audiences, products, and buyer journey.
2. Size the opportunity — Determine the buyer stage where closing the competitive gaps will drive maximum impact.
3. Know where to focus — Build your strategy and execute against the most important opportunities."

"## The data advantage behind every insight — Built on real buyer signals to know what drives real outcomes for your brand. AirOps has years of proprietary data on what buyers search for, what they see, and what they do next. That data is the foundation for defining territories around the market your business is actually going after, not just where your brand appears in AI search.

### 47M observed actions — reveal what worked to drive buyer action, and where.
### 6B citations — show which pages and publishers answer engines trust.
### 10M AI search journeys — show how people move from answers to downstream engagement.
### 70M high value prompts — give concrete insight on how people actually make decisions"

"## A leading corporate card provider wanted to grow their share of voice with enterprise buyers. While prompt tracking said they had already won, AirOps opportunity data showed the true gap. — 55% Overall Share of Voice — 1% Share of Voice with Target Customer" [note: anonymised, unnamed brand — does not qualify as a named case per the evidence bar's first item]

"## Run the work required to win the category — AirOps turns your brand's measured opportunity into a repeatable growth program. Agents research, create, publish, review, monitor, and refresh your content. The system learns from each run, building on the decisions and outcomes that came before, so the work is constantly improving over time to drive maximum results."

"### Run long-horizon agents to scale complex growth programs — The platform breaks down growth programs into goals. It calls the right models and tools for each, and delegates work to specialist subagents. Agents save the work they produce, and memory lets each run build on what the system already found, completed, and learned. Playbooks, Workflows, Multi-model selection, Agent tool library, Proprietary agent tooling, Native external tool connectors, MCP connector support, Grids, Artifacts, Playbook collaboration and versioning"

"### Trace every action from forecast to revenue — AirOps builds an evidence trail across every opportunity, action, change, and result. Score work against your team's standards for quality, brand, and discoverability before it ships, then see how visibility, citations, influence, traffic, and engagement change afterward."

"### See which opportunity gaps closed, and what happened next — Track shifts in visibility, recommendations, citations, sentiment, and position. Break results down by model, audience, region, topic, buying stage, competitor, and source. Compare these shifts in AI visibility with the site and agent behavior that comes next, including search performance, traffic, engagement, crawler activity, and LLM referrals. Use that evidence to identify which actions are worth scaling. — Campaign measurement, Page and prompt level performance, Agent Analytics"

"### Connect AI discovery to revenue attribution — AI-influenced demand doesn't often come with a clean AI referral. Bring share of voice, prompt-volume weights, citation and action trends, content changes, AI crawler activity, assistant referrals and page performance data into your Media Mix Model. See how AI search contributes alongside other channels, and invest more confidently. — Share of Voice, Agent Analytics"
