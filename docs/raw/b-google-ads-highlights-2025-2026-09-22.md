### Access new AI-powered ad formats (full subsection, verbatim)

"Get consumers from discovery to decision faster with Ads in AI Overviews. Last year, we introduced Ads in AI overviews in the US and this year, we expanded to desktop and more countries globally. By integrating ads into helpful, AI-powered responses, you'll be able to bring your expertise straight to your customers and help them make an informed purchase decision."

"Connect in new moments with AI Mode. We're testing ads in AI Mode in the US, giving advertisers the opportunity to connect with people asking deeper, more complex questions—previously untapped moments on Search. Where relevant, ads may appear below and integrated into AI Mode responses."
